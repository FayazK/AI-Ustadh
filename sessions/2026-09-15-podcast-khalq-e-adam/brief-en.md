# Adam, Human Origins, and the Questions of Science

**An English brief of the Urdu podcast episode of 15 September 2026** (session `sessions/2026-09-15-podcast-khalq-e-adam/`, research library `research/khalq-e-adam/`). The full episode runs about 52,000 words of dialogue; this brief keeps the findings and the sources.

**Format.** The recurring cast (a host who is an ordinary Muslim with a little Quran knowledge, plus a classical-lens scholar and a contemporary-lens scholar) took questions from three guests introduced by name and expertise: a historian, a geologist, and a paleontologist. Their religion was never discussed.

**Ground rules the episode held to.** (1) No established scientific fact is denied. (2) Nothing is attributed to the Quran that it does not say. (3) Where scholars differ, the holders are named. (4) Hadith are cited with number and grade, with the grader named. (5) Where the honest answer is "unknown", it is said so. Items the research library could not verify (for example, a bashar/insan theory attributed online to Dr. Israr Ahmed) were deliberately not used.

## 1. Was Adam the first human? What the fossils and DNA say

**The science, stated exactly.** The earth is 4.55 ± 0.07 billion years old (Patterson 1956), refined to 4.54 ± 0.05 (Dalrymple 2001). The oldest *Homo sapiens* fossils: Jebel Irhoud, Morocco, 315 ± 34 ka (Hublin et al., *Nature* 546, 2017; dating by Richter et al., same volume); Omo Kibish, Ethiopia, >233 ka (Vidal et al., *Nature* 601, 2022); Misliya, Israel, 194-177 ka (Hershkovitz et al., *Science* 359, 2018). Neanderthals lived c. 430-40 ka; the Harbin "Dragon Man" skull was identified as Denisovan in June 2025 (Fu et al., *Science* 389 and *Cell* 188, both online 18 June 2025). Non-Africans carry about 1-4% Neanderthal DNA (Green et al., *Science* 328, 2010), refined to 1.5-2.1% (Prüfer et al., *Nature* 505, 2014), from admixture around 47 ka (Sümer et al., *Nature*; Iasi et al., *Science*, both December 2024). Long-term effective population size was about 10,000 (Li & Durbin, *Nature* 475, 2011; Ayala, *Science* 270, 1995).

**"Mitochondrial Eve" is not the first woman.** She is the most recent common ancestor along the maternal line only, about 200 ka in Africa (Cann, Stoneking & Wilson, *Nature* 325, 1987; modern estimates c. 150-200 ka); "Y-chromosomal Adam" is dated c. 120-156 ka by Poznik et al. (*Science* 341, 2013) and c. 254 ka (192-307 ka) by Karmin et al. (*Genome Research* 25, 2015). Neither was alone, neither was first, and they need not have been contemporaries. The guests' own "all evidence points to one woman in Africa" was therefore half right and half a misreading, as the paleontologist conceded.

**The two genetic facts that matter.** (a) Current methods exclude a two-person bottleneck within the last c. 500,000 years but cannot detect one earlier (the 2017-18 Venema, Buggs, Swamidass, Schaffner exchange ended in broad agreement on this; BioLogos formally revised its position in 2020-21). (b) Genealogical ancestry is not genetic ancestry: the most recent common *genealogical* ancestor of all living humans lived only c. 2,000-5,000 years ago, and the "identical ancestors point" c. 5,000-15,000 years ago (Rohde, Olson & Chang, *Nature* 431, 2004). So "everyone alive descends from one couple" is trivially true; the only contested claim is that one couple were humanity's *sole* ancestors.

**What the texts fix.** Adam was created directly, without parents, from earth (3:59; 15:26-29; 32:7-9; 38:71-72); the rooh was breathed into him; humanity is addressed as *Bani Adam* and Adam and Hawwa as "your two parents" (7:27); "Allah created you from one soul" (4:1). Jalajel (Yaqeen 2018; expanded 2020) states this as the classical stance shared by Ash'ari, Maturidi and Salafi theologians alike. The Quran gives no date, no age of the earth, and says nothing about fossils or other hominins.

**The four contemporary positions** (Shoaib Ahmed Malik, *Islam and Evolution: Al-Ghazali and the Modern Evolutionary Paradigm*, Routledge 2021):

1. **Creationism**: no common descent at all (Harun Yahya, *Atlas of Creation*; Hamza Yusuf's recorded view that macroevolution is "a narrative", citing Behe).
2. **Human exceptionalism**: evolution is acceptable for all non-human life, but Adam was specially created and all humans descend from him alone. Nuh Ha Mim Keller, "Islam and Evolution: A Letter to Suleman Ali" (1995): for creatures other than man evolution "does not seem to contradict revelation", but man descending from a non-human species "is unbelief (kufr)". Yasir Qadhi, "Adam & Evolution: The Red Lines of Faith" (EPIC Masjid, Feb 2022): the evidence for evolution is "quite solid"; "I personally do not have ... an alternative for the theory of evolution"; Neanderthals and Denisovans are real non-human "humanoid" species; Adam "half a million years ago, no problem"; on the 6,000-year chronology: "That's your narrative, O Christians and Jews. It's not our narrative". This is the position most often stated by contemporary scholars who engage the science (Qadhi, Keller, Nazir Khan), though no survey of scholars exists.
3. **Adamic exceptionalism**: Adam and Hawwa were created miraculously and placed among an existing population, with which their descendants intermarried. Malik holds this "scripturally compatible" within Ash'ari theology; it parallels Joshua Swamidass, *The Genealogical Adam and Eve* (IVP Academic, 2019). Malik finds positions 1-3 all compatible with scripture, rejects position 4, and remains non-committal among the three.
4. **No exceptions**: full human evolution (Nidhal Guessoum, *Islam's Quantum Question*, 2011; Rana Dajani, who teaches human evolution, *Nature* 520, 2015, without spelling out her reading of Adam; Shabir Ally reading 20:122 "then his Lord chose him" as selection from an existing pool).

Plus **tawaqquf** (David Solomon Jalajel, "Theological Non-Commitment (Tawaqquf) in Sunni Islam and Its Implications for Muslim Acceptance of Human Evolution", Yaqeen Institute 2018): affirm what scripture affirms (parentless Adam; all humans from him and Hawwa) and suspend judgment on what it is silent about (prior hominins, coexistence, intermarriage): "once again, scripture is silent".

**The host's thread, answered.** Under position 2: there were no humans before Adam; the older fossils are non-human species, or Adam is far older than any folk date. Under position 3: the earlier populations had biological parents like any species, were not *mukallaf*, and the Quran does not mention them for the same reason it does not mention dinosaurs: its address is to *Bani Adam*, and silence is neither denial nor affirmation. The classical scholar in the episode held position 2 and pressed 4:1 and the hadith "you are the father of mankind" (Bukhari 4712 / Muslim 194, sahih); the reply from position 3 is that 4:1 addresses "you", the humans being spoken to, who are indeed all descended from the couple. Both scholars named their preference and called the other view within bounds.

**The 60-cubit hadith.** "Allah created Adam sixty cubits tall ... people have been decreasing in stature since" (Bukhari 3326 / Muslim 2841, sahih, muttafaq alayh). No 27-metre human exists in the fossil record. Ibn Hajar, *Fath al-Bari* 6/367 (Kitab Ahadith al-Anbiya, on 3326), wrote after noting that the dwellings of ancient peoples are of ordinary size: "ولم يظهر لي إلى الآن ما يزيل هذا الإشكال" ("nothing has yet appeared to me that removes this difficulty"). The offered reconciliations (height in Paradise; shrinkage on earth; the rarity of fossilisation) were presented as attempts, attributed (islamqa 20612, 22058; Mohammed Hijab), and not as settled. The episode's stance: a sahih hadith is not rejected, science is not denied, and a difficulty is called a difficulty.

## 2. "Adam came 6,000 years ago" vs 300,000 years

No Quranic verse and no sahih marfu' hadith gives a date for Adam or an age for the earth. The 6,000 and 7,000-year figures come from al-Tabari's *Tarikh* (Dar al-Turath ed., 1/10-17): Ka'b al-Ahbar's 6,000, Wahb ibn Munabbih's 5,600, Ibn Abbas's symbolic "week of the Hereafter" (7,000), and a Persian regnal count; al-Tabari himself rejected the isnad of the only marfu' report. Ibn Kathir, *al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya* 1/223, called the Torah dates "inserted"; at 1/214 he records the "six days = 6,000 years" computation of Ibn Abbas, Mujahid and al-Dahhak (preferred by al-Tabari) and closes it with "والله أعلم" rather than adopting it. The only sahih textual datum is "between Adam and Nuh were ten *qurun*" (Ibn Hibban 6190 / al-Hakim 3039: sahih on Muslim's conditions per al-Hakim and al-Dhahabi; al-Albani, *Sahihah* 2668, isnad sahih; Ibn Abbas's report in al-Hakim 4057, sahih on Bukhari's conditions), and Ibn Kathir (*al-Bidaya* 1/237-238) notes that if *qarn* means a generation the gap is "thousands of years". Yasir Qadhi (Ask Shaykh YQ #158) says 50,000-60,000+ years "makes more sense" than 6,000. Verdict: the clash is between a folk chronology and science, not between the Quran and science.

## 3. Who was on earth before Adam? Were there advanced civilisations?

**Texts.** On 2:30 ("I am placing a *khalifa* on the earth"), al-Tabari records Ibn Abbas's report that jinn inhabited the earth first and shed blood (the isnad is weak per Mahmud Shakir; the al-Hakim route is sahih al-isnad but *mawquf*), and al-Tabari prefers that Allah simply informed the angels; Ibn Kathir reads *khalifa* as "generations succeeding one another" and the angels' question as inference; al-Qurtubi adds vicegerency. The "Hinn and Binn" pre-jinn creatures appear in *al-Bidaya* 1/166 without any isnad. Ibn Arabi's reported vision of a man from 42,000 years before Adam (*al-Futuhat*, as reported) and the Shia report of a million Adams (al-Saduq, *al-Tawhid*, as reported) are isolated or non-Sunni and are no basis for belief; islamqa 72470: "there is nothing in the Quran or Sunnah to indicate ... people living on earth before Adam". The Quran names *jinn* as prior inhabitants, never a human civilisation.

**Archaeology.** The oldest monumental site is Göbekli Tepe, c. 9500 BCE (Schmidt); writing appears c. 3300-3200 BCE (Uruk, Abydos); the world population at 10,000 BCE was c. 1-10 million (HYDE 3.2: 4.4 million). Claims of a lost advanced civilisation (Graham Hancock) were rejected by the Society for American Archaeology's open letter of 30 November 2022; the "Silurian hypothesis" paper (Schmidt & Frank, *International Journal of Astrobiology* 18, 2019; online 2018) shows what durable traces an industrial civilisation would leave, and none exist. Claim 7 fails on both sides.

## 4. Are all humans children of Adam and Hawwa?

4:1 ("created you from one soul and from it its mate"), 49:13, 7:27, 2:213. Hadith: "Your Lord is one and your father is one ... no Arab has superiority over a non-Arab ... except by taqwa" (Musnad Ahmad 23489; al-Arna'ut: isnad sahih; al-Albani, *Sahihah* 2700); "People are the children of Adam, and Adam was from dust" (Tirmidhi 3955, hasan gharib; 3956, hasan sahih); "Allah created Adam from a handful taken from all the earth, so his children came red, white, black and between" (Abu Dawud 4693 / Tirmidhi 2955; al-Tirmidhi: hasan sahih; al-Albani: sahih), which is the text's own explanation of human colour diversity. On Hawwa: the majority reads 4:1 with the rib hadith (Bukhari 3331 / Muslim 1468, sahih), which is about temperament, not deficiency (Omar Suleiman); Abu Muslim al-Isfahani read "from it" as "of her kind" (reported by al-Razi, *Mafatih al-Ghayb* on 4:1, who defends its coherence against al-Qadi Abd al-Jabbar's objection); Maududi (*Tafhim*, 4:1 note 1): "the Quran is silent on the matter". Scientifically, all living humans are one species of single African origin; Neanderthal DNA is a difficulty for position 2 (answered by "Allah created the diversity") and expected under position 3.

## 5. What is the rooh?

**Simple version:** what is present in life and absent in death, described by the Quran as "of the command (*amr*) of my Lord" (17:85). **Deep version.** 17:85 was revealed when Jews in Madinah asked the Prophet about the rooh (Bukhari 125 / 4721, sahih). The word has several Quranic senses: Jibril (26:193), revelation (42:52; Maududi reads 17:85 itself this way, *Tafhim* note 103), Isa as "a rooh from Him" (4:171), and the rooh breathed into Adam (15:29; 32:9). Classical position: its essence is not disclosed (Ibn Kathir on 17:85). Al-Ghazali (*Ihya*, Book 21) distinguishes the physicians' "subtle vapour" from "the subtle knowing, perceiving thing" that 17:85 refers to; Ibn al-Qayyim's *Kitab al-Ruh* is the reference work. Rooh vs *nafs*: 39:42 (Allah takes souls at death and in sleep), 91:7-10, 89:27; Yasir Qadhi: rooh plus body equals nafs. Ensoulment: at 120 days (Bukhari 3208 / Muslim 2643, sahih) with the "forty-two nights" narration (Muslim 2645, sahih) reconciled by the majority; "souls are like conscripted soldiers" (Bukhari 3336, mu'allaq; connected in Muslim 2638). Animals have the life-rooh; *taklif* is the distinction; Neanderthals: tawaqquf.

**Science.** The "hard problem" (Chalmers 1995) is unsolved; the 2023-25 adversarial test of the two leading theories, Global Neuronal Workspace and Integrated Information Theory (Cogitate Consortium, *Nature*, 30 April 2025, n = 256), found neither won; the AWARE-II cardiac-arrest study (Parnia et al., *Resuscitation*, 2023: 567 arrests, 28 interviewed, about 20% recalled a death experience, no hidden visual target ever identified) neither proves nor disproves a soul. The embryological milestones (heartbeat c. day 21-22; organogenesis complete at 8 weeks; quickening 16-20 weeks) neither confirm nor refute the 120-day hadith. All of this is 17:85's territory: no over-claiming in either direction.

## 6. People no prophet reached: "already in Paradise"?

**The concept the claim misses:** *taklif*. Paradise and Hell are for the accountable (humans and jinn). Animals are gathered and retribution is taken between them (Muslim 2582, sahih); that they are then told "be dust" is a separate report (al-Tabari on 78:40; al-Hakim 3231; the short wording graded strong by al-Albani). On position 3, pre-Adamic populations were simply not accountable; "sent to Paradise" is a category error.

**For humans never reached.** The rule is 17:15: "We never punish until We have sent a messenger." The scholarly views, each named: al-Ash'ari (*al-Ibana*), citing the test hadith: not punished without a trial; al-Maturidi: reason obliges belief in a Creator, not the details of Islam; al-Ghazali (*Faysal al-Tafriqa*, tr. Keller): three categories, never heard / heard a distorted version (treated like never heard) / heard truly; Ibn Taymiyyah and Ibn al-Qayyim (*Tariq al-Hijratayn*, eight positions surveyed, the test chosen): they are tested on the Day of Judgment, per the hadith of the four who will plead (the deaf, the insane, the senile, and the one who died in a *fatra*), who are commanded to enter a fire; those who obey are saved and those who refuse are "dragged into it" (Musnad Ahmad 16301 / 16302; Ibn Hibban 7357; al-Albani, *Sahihah* 1434: isnad sahih; al-Arna'ut: hasan / sahih; al-Bayhaqi: sahih; dissent from al-Qurtubi, "لم يصح", and Ibn Abd al-Barr). Children: "Allah knows best what they would have done" (Bukhari 1384 / Muslim 2659, sahih). Surveys: Jonathan Brown (Yaqeen Institute, 2018); Mohammad Hassan Khalil, *Islam and the Fate of Others* (Oxford, 2012); Yasir Qadhi (Soteriology Pt 3) extends the *fatra* category to "those who take their verdict", anyone who never heard Islam properly, including isolated communities today. Muqbil al-Wadi'i called the US and Europe "a modern *ahl al-fatra*"; Rida and al-Qaradawi held that only an "attractive and compelling" presentation counts as the message reaching someone.

**The Sentinelese.** North Sentinel Island (Andaman and Nicobar, India): 50-200 people; their Andamanese neighbours' lineages trace to the 50-60 ka dispersal out of Africa (the Sentinelese themselves have never been sampled); protected by the Andaman and Nicobar Islands (Protection of Aboriginal Tribes) Regulation, 1956; John Chau killed November 2018; Mykhailo Polyakov's illegal landing, March 2025; the Great Nicobar mega-project and the Shompen (Al Jazeera / ABC, June 2026); at least 196 uncontacted peoples worldwide (Survival International, 27 October 2025). Islamic ethics: no compulsion (2:256), no harm ("لا ضرر ولا ضرار", Ibn Majah 2340/2341, chains da'if per Darussalam, hasan by corroboration per al-Nawawi, *Arba'in* 32), wisdom (16:125). Contact that kills through disease is itself a harm the sharia forbids. Verdict on claim 5: not punished for what never reached them; the final judgment is Allah's; "automatic Paradise" overstates every school.

## 7. "Knowing is the culprit"

The sharpest objection, answered in seven moves. (1) The premise is wrong: on the test view the unreached are examined, and even on the Ash'ari view "not punished" is not "given the ranks of the near ones". (2) No human is knowledge-free: the *fitra* (30:30; "every child is born on the fitra", Bukhari 1385 / Muslim 2658, sahih; "I created My servants *hunafa'*, then the devils came", Muslim 2865, sahih), the covenant (7:172), reason and the signs (41:53; 3:190-191); al-Maturidi holds the mind already bound to know the Creator. An island without knowledge of God does not exist in the Quran's anthropology; an island without the detailed sharia does. (3) The message is elevation, not a trap: 21:107; "Allah raises those who believe and those given knowledge in degrees" (58:11); 39:9; 35:28; the hundred levels of Paradise (Bukhari 2790, sahih); the pre-message state is called "manifest error" (62:2), not bliss. (4) Rejection, not knowledge, is the culprit: "no one hears of me ... and does not believe but enters the Fire" (Muslim 153, sahih): the operative word is *hears*; nobody says it is better never to hear of a cure. (5) If ignorance were the best route, Allah would not have sent messengers or made conveying the message a joy: "if Allah guides one man through you, it is better for you than red camels" (Bukhari 4210 / Muslim 2406); "convey from me even one verse" (Bukhari 3461). (6) "Reached" means intelligible reception, not geography (al-Ghazali's second category; Qadhi); a person in Lahore who only ever met a caricature is not automatically accountable, and a Sentinelese is not automatically saved. (7) The Judge knows what each would have done (Bukhari 1384) and wrongs no one (99:7-8; 4:40; the believer's promise is explicit, 4:122). In one line: knowledge is a ladder, not a burden; ignorance is a hope of pardon, not a ticket.

## 8. Were prophets sent only to specific regions?

"There was no nation but a warner passed among it" (35:24); "We sent to every nation a messenger" (16:36); "of them We have narrated to you, and of them We have not" (40:78; Ibn Kathir: the unnamed prophets are "many, many more" than the 25 named; that the named ones are those the Quran's first audience knew is a common explanation, not Ibn Kathir's wording). The number "124,000 prophets, 315 messengers": the long Abu Dharr hadith is weak (Ibn Hibban 361 / Ahmad 21546; al-Arna'ut: da'if jiddan), while al-Albani graded the 124,000 figure sahih li-ghayrihi (*Sahihah* 2668) and the short Abu Umamah route (Ibn Hibban 6190) is sahih. Yasir Qadhi ("How Many Prophets") reads *ummah* in 35:24 as a major civilisation, not every tribe. Speculations about prophets in India and Persia exist but are labelled speculation: Mirza Mazhar Jan-i-Janan (18th c.) is reported to have written that Ram and Krishna may have been righteous men or prophets (Friedmann, *JAOS* 95, 1975); Abul Kalam Azad on Zoroaster (*Tarjuman al-Quran*); Muhammad Hamidullah on Buddha as Dhul-Kifl. Absence of a record is not absence: oral cultures forget, and 16:36 itself says some of every nation went astray.

## 9. "The East produced prophets, the West produced philosophers"

Tested as a historian would. Every major religion originated in Asia; philosophy began in Ionian Greece but also in India, China and Persia, and Ionia lies east of Rome; Christianity, the West's religion, is West Asian. Greek philosophy survived through the Baghdad translation movement (Dimitri Gutas, *Greek Thought, Arabic Culture*, 1998); al-Farabi, Ibn Sina and Ibn Rushd shaped Aquinas; Renaissance astronomy drew on Arabic models (George Saliba, *Islamic Science and the Making of the European Renaissance*, 2007). Western dominance is about 500 years old (printing, colonial extraction, industrialisation, institutions: Acemoglu & Robinson, *Why Nations Fail*, 2012). The honest present: OIC states hold c. 24% of world population but produced c. 8.3% of research papers (SCImago 2018 count) and spend c. 0.4% of GDP on R&D (SESRIC, late 2000s) against a world average of 1.79% (UNESCO Science Report 2021); five science Nobel laureates of Muslim background or from Muslim-majority countries (Salam 1979, Zewail 1999, Sancar 2015, Bawendi 2023, Yaghi 2025); Pervez Hoodbhoy, *Islam and Science* (1991). The contemporary scholar conceded the gap fully: the cause is not "prophets" but abandoning what they commanded (96:1; 3:190-191). The guest historian kept the present-day gap and dropped the causal story.

## 10. Nuh's flood: global or regional? And 950 years

**Texts.** 11:25-49 (the Ark rests on al-Judi, 11:44); 71:26 ("leave not on the earth/land a single dweller of the disbelievers"); 37:77 ("We made his descendants the survivors"); 29:14 (950 years among his people); Nuh "the first messenger to the people of the earth" (Bukhari 3340 / Muslim 194, sahih). **Classical majority:** global (al-Tabari; Ibn Kathir on 37:77 and 71:26: all present humanity descends from Nuh's sons). The "fifteen cubits above the mountains" detail is an Ahl al-Kitab report (*al-Bidaya* 1/263), and the Sam/Ham/Yafith hadith (Tirmidhi 3931) is hasan per al-Tirmidhi but da'if per al-Albani, al-Arna'ut and Darussalam; the "curse of Ham" is Genesis 9 and the Talmud, not the Quran (Ahmad Baba of Timbuktu rejected it in 1615). **Modern minority, regional:** Maududi, *Tafhim al-Quran*, Surah Hud note 46: "The Qur'an, however, does not explicitly say so ... it is quite plausible that at that point in history the human population was confined only to the area which was overtaken by the Flood"; Javed Ahmad Ghamidi (video of 1 June 2023; gist from a rough auto-caption, not verbatim): the flood was punishment on Nuh's nation, and the idea that all the animals of the world were aboard is not what the Quran says; even Zakir Naik (debate with William Campbell, 1 April 2000): "nowhere does the Quran say the full world was submerged". The ikhtilaf is legitimate because *al-ard* means both "the land" and "the earth", and the Quranic pattern is that punishment falls on the nation that rejected its own messenger (17:15; 26:208-209). Claim 10 matches a real scholarly position.

**Geology and history.** No global flood layer exists; a global flood would need about three times all the water on earth; sea level has risen 120-130 m since 20 ka, including Meltwater Pulse 1A (14-18 m in under 350 years), drowning Sundaland and the Persian Gulf shelf; the Black Sea deluge hypothesis (Ryan & Pitman, *Marine Geology* 138, 1997) is contested and much reduced (Aksu et al., *GSA Today* 2002; Yanko-Hombach et al., 2007; Aksu & Hiscott, 2022); Woolley's 1929 "flood layer" at Ur (c. 3500 BCE) is local. Mesopotamian flood texts (Ziusudra, Atrahasis, Gilgamesh XI: Lambert & Millard, *Atra-hasis*, 1969; George, *The Babylonian Gilgamesh Epic*, 2003) and the Sumerian King List's antediluvian reigns show a shared ancient memory of a great flood and a pattern of enormous antediluvian ages.

**950 years.** Stated explicitly by the Quran (29:14); Ibn Kathir (*Tafsir* on 29:14): sent at 40, 950 among his people; on the reports of his total age he writes "فيه نظر" (*al-Bidaya* 1/280-281). Adam's own lifespan of a thousand years appears in the hadith where Adam gives Dawud sixty years (Tirmidhi 3368: hasan gharib per al-Tirmidhi; hasan sahih per al-Albani; hasan per Darussalam; sahih per al-Hakim); the related hadith of Adam giving Dawud forty years (Tirmidhi 3076, hasan sahih) states no lifespan for Adam. The mainstream reads it literally as a divine exception, a matter of revelation, not evidence. The circulating "people lived longer because the atmosphere was different" has no Islamic basis (it is Whitcomb & Morris's 1961 "vapour canopy" import). The documented human maximum is 122 (Jeanne Calment). Neither is revelation proven from Sumerian tablets nor disproven by biology's current maximum.

## 11. Ten myths worth dropping (of the 30 debunked)

1. **"Adam was 90 feet tall, and giant skeletons prove it."** The photos are a 2002 Worth1000 Photoshop-contest entry (Snopes; National Geographic); a sahih hadith ends up discredited by a fake defence.
2. **"Adam's footprint is on Adam's Peak, Sri Lanka; he descended in India."** *Al-Bidaya* 1/187, 1/231: Israiliyyat; islamqa 141280: no evidence.
3. **"Adam spoke Arabic; Arabic is the language of Paradise."** Fabricated (Ibn al-Jawzi, *al-Mawdu'at*; al-Albani; islamqa 83262).
4. **"Neanderthals were jinn."** No text; jinn are made of fire (55:15) and are unseen (7:27); Neanderthals left bones, hearths and DNA.
5. **"The crooked rib means women are deficient."** Bukhari 3331 is about temperament; the "rib of her future husband" version is baseless (Banuri fatwa).
6. **"Hawwa tempted Adam."** 7:20-22 addresses both; 20:121 names Adam; the blame narrative is Genesis 3.
7. **"Accepting any evolution is kufr" / "Islam accepts Darwin fully."** Both overstatements: see the four positions; islamweb 361168's own caveat against takfir of individuals; Al-Azhar's 1999 ruling on Abd al-Sabur Shahin's *Abi Adam* (as reported by Usama Hasan: "we disagree with his interpretations, but these do not exceed the limits").
8. **"The apes verses (2:65; 7:166) describe evolution."** They describe punishment (*maskh*); transformed peoples left no progeny (Muslim 2663).
9. **"Anyone who never heard of Islam goes to Hell" / "goes straight to Paradise."** Both wrong: 17:15 and the test hadith.
10. **"Noah's Ark was found on Mount Ararat / at Durupinar."** The Quran names al-Judi (11:44); "the mountains of Ararat" is Genesis 8:4, and the single-peak identification is a later Christian tradition; the Durupinar formation is natural (Collins & Fasold, 1996).

## 12. What remains open

Tawaqquf: Adam's date; whether Neanderthals had the accountable rooh; the essence of the rooh (17:85). Genuine ikhtilaf: sole vs shared ancestry; the fate of the unreached; the flood's extent; where Adam's Paradise was (heavenly per the majority, Ibn Kathir on 2:35; an earthly garden per the view al-Qurtubi attributes to the Mu'tazila). The paleontologist left still unsatisfied by "humanoid, not human" for Jebel Irhoud; the historian still wanted a record of a prophet outside Afro-Eurasia; the geologist dropped "6,000 years" but noted, correctly, that no "Adam layer" was ever promised. The classical scholar thanked them: "these are the questions that strengthen us."
