# Scientific Research — Laylatul Qadr Related Topics

## 1. Universe is Inherently Dark
- The universe is 96% dark matter and dark energy; only ~5% is visible matter
- Space is inherently dark — light exists only temporarily near stars
- Olbers' Paradox: why is the night sky dark if the universe is infinite?
- The "Cosmic Dark Ages" — early universe was cloaked in darkness before first stars
- Source: [ESA - The Dark Universe](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Euclid/The_dark_Universe)
- Source: [Live Science - Universe is darkness](https://www.livescience.com/space/space-exploration/scientists-confirm-that-most-of-the-universe-is-darkness-and-nothing-more)
- Quranic connection: Surah Yasin 36:37 — "And a sign for them is the night. We strip from it [the light of] day"

## 2. Tahajjud/Night Prayer — Health Benefits (Peer-Reviewed)
- **Cortisol reduction:** Randomized controlled trial showed tahajjud prayer (8 rak'ahs, 3-7x/week, 6 weeks) reduced cortisol by 8.91% and blood glucose by 19.71%
- Source: [ResearchGate - Meta-Analysis of Tahajud Prayer](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/343878014_Meta-Analysis_Study_of_Tahajud_Prayer_to_Reduce_Stress_Response)
- **Psychological benefits:** Increases calm, reduces stress/anxiety, increases self-confidence
- Source: [ResearchGate - Tahajjud from Psychological and Health Perspectives](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398686549_Tahajjud_Prayer_from_Psychological_and_Health_Perspectives)
- **Meditation effect:** Tahajjud serves as meditation, allowing individuals to reflect and reduce daily worries
- Source: [Yaqeen Institute - Tahajjud: Fuel for Self and Society](https://yaqeeninstitute.org/read/paper/tahajjud-fuel-for-the-self-and-society)

## 3. Melatonin and Night
- Melatonin is produced by pineal gland mainly at night — regulates sleep-wake cycle
- Bright light and screens interrupt melatonin production
- Meta-analysis confirms melatonin significantly improves sleep quality (PSQI)
- Source: [ScienceDirect - Melatonin systematic review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0149763422002123)
- Connection: The Quranic emphasis on night worship aligns with the body's natural rhythm — the quietest, darkest hours are when the body is most receptive to rest and reflection

## 4. Fasting and Cognitive Function
- Ramadan fasting has been studied for effects on cognition, mood, and hormonal regulation
- Studies show intermittent fasting can improve brain function and reduce inflammation
- The combination of fasting + night worship during last 10 nights creates a unique physiological state
