The Crooked Rib - Omar Suleiman
One of the most controversial aspects
of the creation of Hawa, of Eve,
is what was she created from.
And there's some very condescending things
that are said about Eve,
and are used by extension
to degrade women, and to belittle women,
and to portray women as being inherently deficient.
This all comes from
the story from the Bible, from Genesis, that
Hawa was specifically created from
Adam (عليه السلام)'s rib.
And by extension
it finds it way in our tradition as well.
The Prophet (ﷺ) never said that Eve
was specifically created from
the rib of Adam (عليه السلام).
Number one Allah (سبحان و تعالى) says,
(لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ فِىٓ أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍۢ) (QS. At-Tiin: 4)
"That We have created men in the best creation."
Both men and women
were created without deficiency.
(وَلَقَدْ كَرَّمْنَا بَنِىٓ ءَادَمَ) (QS. Al-Isra: 70)
"We have honored all of the children
of Adam (عليه السلام)."
That's to be establish that
wherever this discussion goes,
that men and women were created in the same fashion.
They are both created
with the same level of completeness.
Which is why Allah (سبحان و تعالى) says,
(إِنَّا خَلَقْنَـٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍۢ وَأُنثَىٰ)
(وَجَعَلْنَـٰكُمْ شُعُوبًۭا وَقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوٓا۟) (QS. Hujurat: 13)
"That We have created you first and foremost,
from male and female,
and then made you nations and tribes."
So Allah addresses
misogynism or sexism, as well as racism.
In fact He addresses sexism before racism,
and at the end of it all,
(إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ أَتْقَىٰكُمْ) (QS. Hujurat: 13)
"The most noble of you in the side of Allah,
is the one who has the most taqwa."
Which is unseen, it's in the heart.
So, no matter what your gender is,
or what your race is,
you have the same ability
to reach Allah (سبحان و تعالى),

Now, what is the creation of Hawa?
Allah (سبحان و تعالى) says,
(يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَـٰكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍۢ وَأُنثَىٰ) (QS. Hujurat: 13),

What is it? What is (مِنْهَا)?

she was extracted

What is the context of this hadith?
This hadith is actually very beautiful,
