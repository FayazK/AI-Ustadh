# Research Index: خلقِ آدم (Creation of Adam, human origins, evolution, the rooh, ahl al-fatra, Nuh's flood)

**Canonical key:** `khalq-e-adam`
**Created:** 2026-09-15
**Anchor references:** Surah Al-Baqarah 2:30-39 · An-Nisa 4:1 · Al-Hujurat 49:13 · Al-Isra 17:15 · Fatir 35:24 · Al-Isra 17:85 · Hud 11:40-44
**Scope:** Was Adam the first human (fossils, Neanderthal DNA, Mitochondrial Eve, population genetics); who was on earth before Adam (jinn, pre-Adamic reports); are all humans from Adam and Hawwa; the four contemporary positions on Islam and evolution (creationism / human exceptionalism / Adamic exceptionalism / no exceptions) with attributed holders; the rooh (17:85, Ghazali, Ibn al-Qayyim, ensoulment hadith, consciousness science); ahl al-fatra and isolated peoples (Sentinelese); "a warner to every nation" and prophets outside the Middle East; the "East = prophets, West = philosophers" trope; the Israiliyyat 6,000-year chronology vs the 300,000-year Homo sapiens record; Nuh's flood global vs regional and Nuh's 950 years; the "is knowledge a liability?" objection.
