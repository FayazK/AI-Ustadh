## 1. The creation and stature of Adam (AS)

### 1.1 Adam created sixty cubits tall; people have been decreasing since

- **Text (summary):** Allah created Adam sixty cubits tall, told him to greet a group of angels ("as-salamu alaykum", they added "wa rahmatullah"), and said everyone who enters Paradise will be in the form of Adam; "the creation has not stopped decreasing until now" (فلم يزل الخلق ينقص حتى الآن). Urdu gist: آدمؑ کا قد ساٹھ ہاتھ تھا، جنت میں داخل ہونے والے اسی صورت پر ہوں گے، اور اس کے بعد سے مخلوق کا قد گھٹتا رہا۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3326 (Kitab Ahadith al-Anbiya), also 6227 (Kitab al-Isti'dhan, with the words "on His/his image"); Sahih Muslim 2841 (Kitab al-Jannah). Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari and Muslim; muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3326 , https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6227 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2841
- **Notes:**
  - **Ibn Hajar's admitted difficulty.** In Fath al-Bari, Kitab Ahadith al-Anbiya, Bab khalq Adam (on hadith 3326; 6/367 in the Dar al-Ma'rifa pagination cited by islamqa), Ibn Hajar first explains "لم يزل الخلق ينقص" as "every generation is shorter than the one before, and the decrease ended at this Ummah and stabilised." He then quotes Ibn al-Tin (that the decrease is gradual, like growth, unnoticeable across two days but clear across many). Then he writes, verbatim (read on islamweb's Fath al-Bari text): «ويشكل على هذا ما يوجد الآن من آثار الأمم السالفة كديار ثمود، فإن مساكنهم تدل على أن قاماتهم لم تكن مفرطة الطول على حسب ما يقتضيه الترتيب السابق، ولا شك أن عهدهم قديم، وأن الزمان الذي بينهم وبين آدم دون الزمان الذي بينهم وبين أول هذه الأمة، ولم يظهر لي إلى الآن ما يزيل هذا الإشكال». Translation: "The dwellings of Thamud show their statures were not excessively tall as the sequence would require... and until now nothing has appeared to me that removes this difficulty."
  - In the same passage Ibn Hajar records (a) a hasan-isnad marfu' report via Ibn Abi Hatim from Ubayy ibn Ka'b that Adam was "a tall man with much hair, like a lofty date palm"; (b) Ahmad's report via Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib that Adam was "sixty cubits by seven cubits wide" (see 10.4 below: the width clause is weak); (c) Abd al-Razzaq's report that when Adam descended "his feet were on the earth and his head in the sky, so Allah reduced him to sixty cubits". On (c) Ibn Hajar says the sahih hadith's apparent meaning is that he was created at sixty cubits from the start, "and that is the reliable position" (وهو المعتمد). Ibn Kathir says the same of report (c): "وفي هذا نظر" (al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya, al-Turki ed., 1/214).
  - Ibn Hajar also notes that "cubit" may mean the cubit customary to the listeners, or Adam's own cubit (a man's cubit is a quarter of his height), and prefers the former.
  - **Modern answers.** islamqa.info/en/answers/20612 and 22058: accept the hadith as it stands, argue that decrease over generations is possible for Allah, and cite "giants" in the past; islamqa.info/ar/answers/329542: candidly concedes Ibn Hajar's difficulty remains, adds that it applies equally to the Pharaonic era, and closes with "knowledge belongs to Allah"; abuaminaelias.com ("Was Adam sixty cubits tall while on Earth?"): argues the sixty cubits describe Adam's form in Paradise (the hadith says "everyone who enters Paradise will be in the form of Adam"), i.e. a statement about the unseen, not a biological claim about earthly stature. Podcast use: the honest position is that the hadith is sahih, that the classical commentator himself flagged the archaeological tension six centuries ago, and that reconciliations exist but none is agreed.

### 1.2 Adam created from a handful taken from all the earth (red, white, black and between)

- **Text (summary):** "Allah created Adam from a handful He took from all of the earth, so the children of Adam came according to the earth: red, white, black and in between; easy and hard (tempered); bad and good." Urdu gist: آدمؑ ساری زمین کی ایک مٹھی مٹی سے بنے، اسی لیے اولاد سرخ، سفید، سیاہ اور درمیانی رنگوں کی ہوئی۔
- **Source:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4693 (Kitab al-Sunnah); Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2955 (Chapters on Tafsir); also Musnad Ahmad. Narrator: Abu Musa al-Ash'ari.
- **Grade:** Al-Tirmidhi: «هذا حديث حسن صحيح» (read from the Arabic on sunnah.com). Al-Albani: Sahih (displayed on sunnah.com for Abu Dawud 4693). Darussalam: Sahih (Tirmidhi 2955).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4693 , https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2955
- **Notes:** No text-critical difficulty. Classical exegetes (al-Razi on 4:1 quotes Ibn Abbas) connect the name "Adam" to "adim al-ard" (the surface of the earth). Relevant to the podcast's question of human "races": the hadith makes colour variation original and non-hierarchical (see 6.2).

### 1.3 "Allah created the soil on Saturday... Adam after Asr on Friday"

- **Text (summary):** The Prophet took Abu Hurairah's hand and said: Allah created the soil (التربة) on Saturday, the mountains on Sunday, the trees on Monday, the disliked things on Tuesday, light on Wednesday, spread the animals on Thursday, and created Adam after Asr on Friday, in the last hour of Friday. Urdu gist: سات دنوں میں مخلوقات کی ترتیب اور آدمؑ کی تخلیق جمعہ کے آخری وقت میں۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2789 (Kitab Sifat al-Qiyamah). Also Musnad Ahmad 8341; Sahih Ibn Hibban 6161; al-Nasa'i al-Kubra 11392.
- **Grade:** Included by Muslim in the Sahih, but **ma'lul (defective) according to a strong line of hadith critics**:
  - Al-Bukhari, al-Tarikh al-Kabir 1/413: «قال بعضهم عن أبي هريرة عن كعب، وهو أصح» (some narrate it as Abu Hurairah from Ka'b al-Ahbar, and that is more correct), i.e. it is Ka'b's statement, not the Prophet's.
  - Ali ibn al-Madini (as reported by al-Bayhaqi, al-Asma' wa al-Sifat 2/250, per islamqa 218080): held that Isma'il ibn Umayyah took it from Ibrahim ibn Abi Yahya (a rejected narrator), not directly from Ayyub ibn Khalid.
  - Yahya ibn Ma'in and Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi are also listed by Ibn Taymiyyah among those who judged it an error (al-Jawab al-Sahih 2/443; Majmu' al-Fatawa 17/235: «معلول قدح فيه أئمة الحديث كالبخاري وغيره»; Majmu' 18/18: the correct view is that it is weak).
  - Ibn al-Qayyim, al-Manar al-Munif p.72: "it is in Sahih Muslim, but the error occurred in raising it (to the Prophet); it is the statement of Ka'b al-Ahbar."
  - Ibn Kathir, Tafsir 3/422 (on 41:9): "al-Bukhari and more than one of the huffaz spoke about this hadith and made it not marfu'."
  - Al-Mu'allimi (al-Anwar al-Kashifah p.192): doubts the Ibn Jurayj-from-Ata' route.
  - Defenders: Ibn Hibban (included it); Ibn al-Arabi (Ahkam al-Qur'an 4/83: "thabit"); al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah no. 1833 (2/726: "its men are trustworthy"; Sahih al-Jami' 3235: sahih); islamqa.info/ar/answers/218080 leans to authenticity.
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2789
- **Notes:** The reasons given for weakness are two: an isnad problem (the two narrators Ayyub ibn Khalid and Isma'il ibn Umayyah, and the Ka'b attribution), and a matn problem, that it lists seven days of creation whereas the Quran states six and ends with Adam (Ibn Taymiyyah, al-Tawassul wa al-Wasilah p.172). For the podcast: quote it as "in Sahih Muslim but disputed by al-Bukhari and others", never as an unchallenged Prophetic chronology.

## 2. The rooh (soul)

### 2.1 The Jews asked about the rooh and 17:85 was revealed

- **Text (summary):** Ibn Mas'ud: walking with the Prophet in Madinah, some Jews asked "O Abu al-Qasim, what is the rooh?" The Prophet stayed silent, revelation came, and he recited: "They ask you about the rooh. Say: the rooh is from the command of my Lord, and you have been given of knowledge only a little" (17:85). Urdu gist: یہود نے روح کے بارے میں پوچھا تو یہ آیت اتری کہ روح میرے رب کے حکم سے ہے اور تمہیں تھوڑا علم دیا گیا ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 125 (Kitab al-Ilm) and 4721 (Kitab al-Tafsir); also Sahih Muslim 2794.
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:125 , https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4721
- **Notes:** Scholars differ on whether the ayah forbids inquiry into the rooh's essence or merely states that its full reality is not disclosed; Ibn al-Qayyim's Kitab al-Ruh is the standard classical treatment (he holds the rooh is a created body of a subtle kind). The verse is the foundational text for "the rooh is not a subject for empirical science".

### 2.2 The rooh is breathed into the fetus after 120 days

- **Text (summary):** Ibn Mas'ud: "The creation of each of you is gathered in his mother's womb forty days (as a drop), then a clot for a like period, then a lump for a like period; then an angel is sent who breathes the rooh into him and is commanded to write four things: his provision, his lifespan, his deeds, and whether he is wretched or blessed." Urdu gist: چالیس، چالیس، چالیس دن کے تین مراحل کے بعد فرشتہ روح پھونکتا ہے اور چار باتیں لکھتا ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3208 (Bad' al-Khalq), also 3332, 6594; Sahih Muslim 2643.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3208 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2643
- **Notes:** See 2.3 for the reconciliation with the 40/42-night reports.

### 2.3 The 42-night narration (Hudhayfah ibn Asid)

- **Text (summary):** "When forty-two nights have passed over the drop, Allah sends an angel who shapes it, creates its hearing, sight, skin, flesh and bones, then asks: My Lord, male or female? ... then writes its lifespan and provision." Urdu gist: بیالیس راتوں کے بعد فرشتہ صورت بناتا ہے اور تقدیر لکھتا ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2645 (Kitab al-Qadar), with other wordings "forty nights", "forty-some nights".
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2645
- **Notes:** The reconciliation adopted by the majority: the 42-night hadith describes the angel's **shaping (تصوير)** and the writing, not the **breathing of the rooh**; the nafkh is fixed at 120 days by Ibn Mas'ud's hadith, which is more explicit. This is the treatment in Ibn Rajab, Jami' al-Ulum wa al-Hikam, hadith 4, and in Ibn al-Qayyim, al-Tibyan fi Aqsam al-Qur'an (on the stages of the embryo) **(not re-verified online in this session)**. A minority reading holds that shaping and ensoulment can both occur around forty days; contemporary fiqh councils that fix "ensoulment" for abortion rulings almost all use 120 days.

### 2.4 "Souls are conscripted soldiers"

- **Text (summary):** Aishah: "Souls are conscripted troops: those that knew one another (in the world of souls) are drawn together, and those that did not, differ." Urdu gist: روحیں جمع شدہ لشکر ہیں، جو وہاں پہچانی گئیں وہ یہاں مل جاتی ہیں۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3336 (Ahadith al-Anbiya), as a mu'allaq (suspended) report in al-Bukhari; connected with full isnad in Sahih Muslim 2638 (from Abu Hurairah).
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari mu'allaq; Muslim mawsul).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3336
- **Notes:** Used by scholars for the pre-existence of souls before bodies, though Ibn al-Qayyim (Kitab al-Ruh) argues the hadith does not prove a full life of souls before the body; it proves affinity based on Allah's prior knowledge or a prior encounter.

## 3. Prophethood of Adam, the number of prophets, and the generations to Nuh

### 3.1 Adam was a prophet, "nabi mukallam"; ten qurun between him and Nuh; 315 messengers (Abu Umamah, short version)

- **Text (summary):** A man (identified as Abu Dharr in the Musnad Ahmad route) asked: "Was Adam a prophet?" The Prophet: "Yes, a prophet spoken to (مكلَّم)." "How long between him and Nuh?" "Ten qurun." (In the fuller text: "How many messengers?" "Three hundred and fifteen, a great multitude.") Urdu gist: آدمؑ نبی تھے جن سے اللہ نے کلام کیا؛ ان کے اور نوحؑ کے درمیان دس قرن تھے؛ رسول تین سو پندرہ تھے۔
- **Source:** Sahih Ibn Hibban 6190 (and 3113); al-Tabarani al-Kabir 8/118 no. 7545; al-Hakim, al-Mustadrak 3039 (2/262); al-Bayhaqi, al-Asma' wa al-Sifat 440. Narrator: Abu Umamah al-Bahili, via Mu'awiyah ibn Sallam, from his brother Zayd, from Abu Sallam.
- **Grade:** Al-Hakim: «صحيح على شرط مسلم», al-Dhahabi agreed. Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/237: "this is on the conditions of Muslim, and he did not include it." Al-Haythami, Majma' 8/213: its men are the men of the Sahih. Al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah no. 2668 (6/359): «إسناده صحيح». Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Ibn Hibban 6190; Zad al-Ma'ad takhrij 1/45): isnad sahih on Muslim's conditions. Al-Wadi'i, al-Sahih al-Musnad 480: sahih.
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com. Grader entries: https://dorar.net/hadith/search?q=نبي+مكلم
- **Notes:** This short Abu Umamah route is the strongest text for (a) Adam's prophethood, (b) the "ten qurun" gap, and (c) the 315 messengers. The 124,000 figure is **not** in this route; it comes from the weak routes in 3.2 and 3.3.

### 3.2 The long hadith of Abu Dharr (124,000 prophets, 313/315 messengers, Adam a "nabi mukallam", Idris first to write, etc.)

- **Text (summary):** A long question-and-answer between Abu Dharr and the Prophet covering the first prophet (Adam), the number of prophets (124,000) and messengers (313 or 315), the revealed scrolls, the Arab prophets, and more. Urdu gist: ابوذرؓ کی لمبی حدیث جس میں انبیاء کی تعداد ایک لاکھ چوبیس ہزار بتائی گئی۔
- **Source:** Sahih Ibn Hibban 361 (also 807 in another numbering); Musnad Ahmad 21546 (Shu'ayb ed.; 21415 in another numbering); Abu Dawud al-Tayalisi 480; al-Bazzar 4034; al-Hakim 4166 and 4217; Abu Nu'aym, Hilyat al-Awliya 1/166; al-Tabari, Tarikh 9/120.
- **Grade:** Disputed; the majority of hadith specialists weaken the full text.
  - Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut: «إسناده ضعيف جدا» (very weak), both on Ibn Hibban 361 and Musnad Ahmad 21546; on Musnad 21552 (another route): isnad da'if.
  - Al-Haythami, Majma' 1/201: "in it is Ibn Lahi'ah, who is weak"; 1/164 (route of Ahmad 21586): al-Mas'udi, trustworthy but his memory became confused.
  - Ibn Kathir, Irshad al-Faqih 2/336: "very gharib; and from another route from Abu Dharr with an isnad that is not bad (لا بأس به)".
  - Al-Suyuti, al-Durr al-Manthur 5/132: "the correct view is that it is da'if, neither sahih nor fabricated."
  - Ibn Abd al-Barr, al-Istighna 3/1557: munkar. Ibn al-Jawzi placed a version (from Ali) in al-Mawdu'at 2/440.
  - Ibn Baz: "its isnad has criticism" (Majmu' Fatawa 31/3); "da'if" (Nur ala al-Darb 1/79). Ibn Uthaymin (Tafsir Surat Ghafir p.75): "some scholars said it is not sahih, although Ibn Hibban authenticated it."
  - Those who strengthened it: Ibn Hibban (by inclusion); al-Shawkani, al-Fath al-Rabbani 1/484: isnad hasan; al-Ajluni, Kashf al-Khafa 2/130: "sahih and has supporting witnesses"; al-Sakhawi, al-Maqasid al-Hasanah 365: "the witnesses to this meaning are many"; al-Albani: "sahih" (Hidayat al-Ruwat 5669, and in a recorded fatwa, Fatawa Jeddah tape 17: «الحديث هذا صحيح»).
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com. Grader entries: https://dorar.net/hadith/search?q=مائة+ألف+وأربعة+وعشرون+ألفا
- **Notes:** Al-Albani's considered written verdict (al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 2668, quoted verbatim by islamweb fatwa 200249): «وجملة القول: إن عدد الرسل المذكورين في حديث الترجمة صحيح لذاته، وأن عدد الأنبياء المذكورين في أحد طرقه، وفي حديث أبي ذر من ثلاث طرق، فهو صحيح لغيره» ("In sum: the number of messengers in the main hadith is sahih in itself, and the number of prophets, found in one of its routes and in three routes of Abu Dharr's hadith, is sahih li-ghayrihi"). So: 315 messengers = sahih (per al-Albani, on the Abu Umamah route); 124,000 prophets = sahih li-ghayrihi per al-Albani, da'if jiddan per Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut, da'if per Ibn Baz and al-Suyuti. The number should be presented on the podcast as "widely quoted, weak or at best strengthened by multiple weak routes, not a matter of certainty". Ibn Uthaymin (Sharh al-Safariniyyah 580): "reports have come about it that are not of the strength that gives certainty."

### 3.3 The Abu Umamah long route (124,000 prophets, 313 messengers)

- **Text (summary):** Same content as 3.2 with "313 messengers".
- **Source:** Musnad Ahmad 22288; al-Tabarani al-Kabir 8/259 no. 7871; Ibn Abi Hatim, Tafsir 962. Route: Mu'an ibn Rifa'ah, from Ali ibn Yazid al-Alhani, from al-Qasim Abu Abd al-Rahman, from Abu Umamah.
- **Grade:** Ibn Kathir, Tafsir 2/423 (on 4:164): "in it are Mu'an ibn Rifa'ah al-Salami, weak; Ali ibn Yazid, weak; and al-Qasim Abu Abd al-Rahman, also weak"; al-Bidaya 2/140: weak from this route. Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Musnad 22288): isnad da'if jiddan. Al-Albani, Sahihah 6/359: "Ali ibn Yazid al-Alhani is weak, Mu'an ibn Rifa'ah is layyin" (he used it only as a supporting witness). Ibn Baz: da'if.
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com.
- **Notes:** This is the route usually cited for "124,000" in Urdu literature; on its own it is weak.

### 3.4 "Between Adam and Nuh were ten qurun, all upon Islam / a true shari'ah" (Ibn Abbas)

- **Text (summary):** Ibn Abbas, on 2:213: "Between Nuh and Adam were ten qurun, all of them upon a shari'ah of truth; then they differed, so Allah sent the prophets as bringers of glad tidings and warners." Another wording: "all of them upon Islam." Urdu gist: آدمؑ اور نوحؑ کے درمیان دس قرن گزرے جو سب حق پر تھے، پھر اختلاف ہوا تو انبیاء بھیجے گئے۔
- **Source:** Tafsir al-Tabari on 2:213 (no. 4048; 4/275) via Qatadah, from Ikrimah, from Ibn Abbas; al-Hakim, al-Mustadrak 4057 (2/546; also numbered 3654/3699 in other editions), al-Bazzar 4815, Ibn Abi Hatim 15184.
- **Grade:** Mawquf on Ibn Abbas (his statement, though of the kind that has the ruling of marfu' because it is not said by opinion). Al-Hakim: «صحيح على شرط البخاري», al-Dhahabi agreed. Ibn Kathir, Tafsir on 2:213: "the first (report) from Ibn Abbas is more sound in isnad and meaning." Al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 7/854: sahih. Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (takhrij Sharh al-Tahawiyyah 425): sahih.
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com. Tafsir Ibn Kathir text: https://tafsir.app/ibn-katheer/2/213
- **Notes:**
  - **Attribution slip:** Ibn Kathir in al-Bidaya 1/237 and 1/246 writes "and in Sahih al-Bukhari from Ibn Abbas...". The report is **not** in Sahih al-Bukhari; it is in al-Tabari and al-Hakim (his own Tafsir cites al-Tabari and al-Hakim only). Do not cite it as "Bukhari".
  - **Meaning of qarn (central to "Adam 6,000 years vs Homo sapiens 300,000 years").** Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/237-238, read verbatim: «فإن كان المراد بالقرن مائة سنة، كما هو المتبادر عند كثير من الناس، فبينهما ألف سنة لا محالة، لكن لا ينفي أن يكون أكثر... وإن كان المراد بالقرن الجيل من الناس، كما في قوله تعالى: وكم أهلكنا من القرون من بعد نوح... وكقوله عليه السلام: خير القرون قرني، فقد كان الجيل قبل نوح يعمرون الدهور الطويلة، فعلى هذا يكون بين آدم ونوح ألوف من السنين، والله أعلم». That is: if qarn = 100 years, the gap is 1,000 years; if qarn = a generation, and the pre-Flood generations lived for very long ages, the gap is "thousands of years". Ibn Kathir also notes that Abu Umamah's hadith restricts the gap to ten qurun, while Ibn Abbas adds that all were on Islam, which refutes the historians and Ahl al-Kitab who claim Qabil and his sons worshipped fire.
  - Ibn Hajar's general discussion of qarn is in Fath al-Bari on the hadith "خير القرون قرني" (Kitab Fada'il Ashab al-Nabi), where he defines a qarn as "people of one era who share in some matter", records opinions ranging from ten to 120 years, and holds that in the Prophet's usage a qarn is best taken as roughly 100 years on the evidence of the hadith to Abd Allah ibn Busr "you will live a qarn" (he lived 100 years). **(Not re-verified online in this session; volume/page unverified.)** Ibn Kathir's text above is the one to quote, since it is confirmed.
  - islamqa.info/ar/answers/276637 (chronology of the prophets) states explicitly that no Quranic verse or sahih hadith gives an absolute date for Adam; the only textual anchors are the two "ten qurun" reports.

## 4. Ahl al-fatra (people who never received a message) and the children of the mushrikin

### 4.1 The four who will plead on the Day of Judgment and are tested with the Fire (al-Aswad ibn Sari')

- **Text (summary):** "Four will plead on the Day of Judgment: a deaf man who heard nothing, a mentally deficient man, a senile old man, and a man who died in a fatrah (gap between messengers). The deaf: 'Lord, Islam came and I heard nothing.' The simpleton: 'Islam came while the boys were pelting me with dung.' The senile: 'Islam came and I understood nothing.' The one who died in the fatrah: 'Lord, no messenger of Yours came to me.' Allah takes their pledge to obey Him, then sends a messenger to them: enter the Fire. By Him in Whose hand is my soul, if they entered it, it would be cool and safe for them." The Abu Hurairah version adds: "and whoever does not enter it will be dragged into it." Urdu gist: بہرا، پاگل، بہت بوڑھا اور فترت میں مرنے والا قیامت کے دن حجت کریں گے، ان کا امتحان آگ سے لیا جائے گا۔
- **Source:** Musnad Ahmad 16301 (Shu'ayb ed.; 16344 in another numbering; old edition 4/24), Abu Hurairah version Ahmad 16302 (16345); Sahih Ibn Hibban 7357; al-Tabarani al-Kabir 1/287 no. 841/844; al-Bayhaqi, al-I'tiqad p.169 and al-Qada' wa al-Qadar 644, 645; Ibn Abi Asim, al-Sunnah 404; al-Diya al-Maqdisi, al-Mukhtarah 1454, 1456; Ishaq ibn Rahawayh 41, 42.

  - Al-Albani, **al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 1434**: «إسناده صحيح»; Sahih al-Jami' 881: sahih; Raf' al-Astar 113: isnad jayyid; takhrij Kitab al-Sunnah 404 (Abu Hurairah route): sahih.
  - Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut: Musnad 16301: hasan; Ibn Hibban 7357: isnad sahih; Musnad 16302 (Abu Hurairah): isnad hasan.
  - Al-Bayhaqi (al-I'tiqad): «هذا إسناد صحيح». Al-Haythami, Majma' 7/216-218: "its men, in the routes of al-Aswad ibn Sari' and Abu Hurairah, are the men of the Sahih." Ibn Kathir, Jami' al-Masanid 443: "isnad jayyid, strong, sahih". Ibn al-Qayyim, Tariq al-Hijratayn p.333 and Ahkam Ahl al-Dhimmah 2/1139: isnad sahih, connected. Al-Wadi'i, al-Sahih al-Musnad 24: sahih.

- **Notes:** This is the key text for the podcast's "what about people who never heard of a prophet?" It underwrites the majority-Sunni position (al-Ash'ari reports it as the position of Ahl al-Sunnah; al-Bayhaqi, Ibn Kathir, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Taymiyyah) that ahl al-fatra are **tested in the plain of Judgment**, not condemned. See 4.4 and 4.5.

### 4.2 The children of the mushrikin: "Allah knows best what they would have done"

- **Text (summary):** The Prophet was asked about the children of the mushrikin (who die young). He said: "Allah knows best what they would have been doing." Urdu gist: مشرکین کے بچوں کے بارے میں فرمایا: اللہ خوب جانتا ہے وہ کیا عمل کرتے۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1384 (Kitab al-Jana'iz), 6599-6600 (Kitab al-Qadar); Sahih Muslim 2659b (Kitab al-Qadar); Sunan Abi Dawud 4712 (Aishah, with "they follow their parents").
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh). Abu Dawud 4712: sahih al-isnad (al-Albani, on sunnah.com).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1384 , https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6599 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2659b , https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4712
- **Notes:** The Prophet's answer refers their fate to Allah's knowledge; Ibn al-Qayyim reads the imtihan hadith (4.1) as showing how that knowledge becomes manifest (see 4.5, eighth position).

### 4.3 The dream: the Prophet sees Ibrahim surrounded by children, including the children of the mushrikin

- **Text (summary):** In the long dream hadith of Samurah ibn Jundub, the Prophet is taken by two angels through scenes of punishment and reward; in a garden he sees a tall man (Ibrahim) surrounded by children, "the children who died upon the fitrah." The Muslims asked: "And the children of the mushrikin?" He said: "And the children of the mushrikin." Urdu gist: خواب میں ابراہیمؑ کے گرد بچے دیکھے، پوچھا گیا مشرکین کے بچے بھی؟ فرمایا: ہاں۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 7047 (Kitab al-Ta'bir); also 1386, 3354.
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:7047
- **Notes:** Ibn Kathir and Ibn al-Qayyim cite this as the main proof that the children of the mushrikin are in Paradise; Ibn al-Qayyim reconciles it with the imtihan reports: those whom Allah knows will obey have their souls placed with Ibrahim already.

### 4.4 Every child is born upon the fitrah

- **Text (summary):** "Every child is born upon the fitrah; then his parents make him a Jew, a Christian or a Magian, just as an animal gives birth to a whole animal; do you see any mutilated one?" Urdu gist: ہر بچہ فطرت پر پیدا ہوتا ہے، والدین اسے یہودی، نصرانی یا مجوسی بناتے ہیں۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1385, 6599; Sahih Muslim 2658b; also Muslim 2865 (Iyad ibn Himar: "I created My servants hunafa', then the devils came and turned them away from their religion").
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1385 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2658b , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2865
- **Notes:** Ibn Kathir (on 7:172) and al-Hasan al-Basri read the "witnessing" of the Alast verse as this fitrah (see 7.3).

### 4.5 Ibn Kathir's collection of the fatra reports (Tafsir of 17:15) and Ibn al-Qayyim's eight positions (Tariq al-Hijratayn)

- **Text (summary):** Not a hadith; two classical syntheses. Ibn Kathir, on 17:15 "We never punish until We send a messenger", lists ten hadiths (al-Aswad ibn Sari' first, then Anas, al-Bara', Abu Sa'id, Samurah, Aishah, Khadijah's question, and others), grades several of them in passing, and then summarises the scholars' positions. Ibn al-Qayyim, Tariq al-Hijratayn ('Ata'at al-Ilm ed., pp. 842-877, "Tabaqah 14: those who have neither obedience nor disobedience, neither kufr nor iman"), lists **eight** madhhabs on the children of the mushrikin and, by extension, ahl al-fatra.
- **Source:** Tafsir Ibn Kathir 17:15 (read on tafsir.app); Tariq al-Hijratayn (read on shamela.ws/book/251, pages 928-963).
- **Grade:** n/a (scholarly analysis). Ibn Kathir's verdicts inside it: the Anas report "they are servants of the people of Paradise" is weak (Yazid al-Raqashi); the Khadijah report "if you wish I will let you hear their screaming in the Fire" has a matruk narrator (Abu Aqil Yahya ibn al-Mutawakkil), and Ibn Taymiyyah called it "a fabricated lie" (Dar' al-Ta'arud 8/398, quoted in the editor's note to Tariq al-Hijratayn p.846).
- **Link:** https://tafsir.app/ibn-katheer/17/15 ; https://shamela.ws/book/251/928
- **Notes:**
  - **Ibn al-Qayyim's eight positions (verbatim headings):** (1) al-waqf: suspend judgment, "Allah knows best what they would have done"; (2) they are in the Fire (some mutakallimun and mufassirun; one Hanbali view; al-Qadi Abu Ya'la attributed it to Ahmad, which Ibn Taymiyyah called a mistake based on the fabricated Khadijah report); (3) they are in Paradise (Ibn Hazm and others, on Bukhari 7047); (4) a station between the two stations, ahl al-A'raf (Abd al-Aziz al-Kinani: "those who died in the fatrah and the children of the mushrikin"); (5) under Allah's unconditioned will (the Jabriyyah); (6) servants of the people of Paradise (weak Anas reports); (7) their ruling follows their fathers in both worlds (on the Sa'b ibn Jaththamah hadith "they are of them", Bukhari/Muslim); (8) **they are tested in the plain of Judgment, a messenger is sent to them and to everyone the call did not reach; whoever obeys enters Paradise and whoever disobeys enters the Fire.** Ibn al-Qayyim: «وبهذا يتألف شمل الأدلة كلها، وتتوافق الأحاديث» (with this all the evidences come together).
  - **Ibn Abd al-Barr's objection and the reply.** Ibn Abd al-Barr (quoted by Ibn Kathir and by Ibn al-Qayyim p.872-877) said the imtihan hadiths "are not strong, no proof stands by them, and the scholars reject them, because the Hereafter is an abode of recompense, not of action or trial." Ibn Kathir's reply: some of these hadiths are sahih, some hasan, some weak but strengthened; the Hereafter being an abode of recompense does not preclude obligation in its plains before entry into Paradise or the Fire, as in "the day the shin is bared and they are called to prostrate" (68:42) and the sahih hadith of the last man to enter Paradise being made to give pledges. Ibn al-Qayyim adds: "the isnad of al-Aswad's hadith is better than many hadiths used as proof in legal rulings; that is why the imams Ahmad, Ishaq and Ali ibn al-Madini narrated it."
  - Ibn Kathir's own conclusion: the imtihan view "is the one al-Ash'ari related from Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah, and which al-Bayhaqi supported in Kitab al-I'tiqad."

## 5. Hawwa (AS) and the rib

### 5.1 Woman created from a rib

- **Text (summary):** "Treat women well, for woman was created from a rib, and the most crooked part of the rib is its top; if you try to straighten it you will break it, and if you leave it, it stays crooked. So treat women well." Urdu gist: عورت پسلی سے پیدا کی گئی، سیدھی کرو گے تو ٹوٹ جائے گی، عورتوں سے بھلائی کرو۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3331 (Ahadith al-Anbiya), also 5185-5186; Sahih Muslim 1468 (Kitab al-Rada'). Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3331 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:1468
- **Notes (literal vs figurative, attributed precisely):**
  - **Majority (literal):** al-Razi, Mafatih al-Ghayb on 4:1 (read on tafsir.app): "The first view, held by the majority: when Allah created Adam He cast sleep upon him, then created Hawwa from one of his left ribs... they cite the Prophet's words 'woman was created from a crooked rib'."
  - **Abu Muslim al-Isfahani (d. 322 AH), via al-Razi on 4:1:** «والقول الثاني، وهو اختيار أبي مسلم الأصفهاني: أن المراد من قوله "وخلق منها زوجها" أي من جنسها» ("from her kind"), citing 16:72, 3:164, 9:128 where "min anfusikum" means "of your own kind". Al-Razi reports al-Qadi (Abd al-Jabbar) preferring the first view because otherwise mankind would be "from two souls, not one", then al-Razi himself answers: "min" can denote the starting point, and since Allah could create Adam from dust He could create Hawwa from dust too, "so what benefit is there in creating her from one of Adam's ribs?" Al-Razi therefore records the figurative reading of the verse as a legitimate minority view; note that Abu Muslim's view is about the **verse**, and he does not deny the hadith's text.
  - **Muhammad Abduh / Rashid Rida, Tafsir al-Manar on 4:1:** Rida records Abduh's lesson that "khalaqa minha zawjaha" need not mean creation from Adam's body, and that the rib hadith is best understood as **tamthil** (an image for the disposition of women, as the Muslim 1468 wording "she will not remain straight for you on one way" suggests), while Rida himself is more cautious and treats the literal creation as the apparent meaning of the hadith. **(Volume/page not re-verified online in this session; attribute as "reported by Rida from Abduh in al-Manar, on 4:1".)**
  - The hadith itself does not name Adam or Hawwa; the "rib of Adam" detail enters through the tafsir tradition (Ibn Abbas and others in al-Tabari on 2:35, 4:1) and Genesis 2:21-22.

### 5.2 The name "Hawwa" in sahih hadith

- **Text (summary):** "Were it not for Banu Isra'il, meat would not rot; and were it not for Hawwa, no woman would ever betray her husband." Urdu gist: اگر حوا نہ ہوتیں تو کوئی عورت اپنے شوہر سے خیانت نہ کرتی۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3330 (Ahadith al-Anbiya); Sahih Muslim 1470.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3330 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:1470
- **Notes:** The Quran never names Adam's wife; her name Hawwa rests on this sahih hadith and on the tafsir tradition (al-Razi quotes Ibn Abbas: she was called Hawwa because she was created from a living being, hayy). The "betrayal" is explained by the commentators as her role in persuading Adam to eat from the tree, not marital infidelity.

## 6. Universality of the message and the equality of mankind

### 6.1 "I was sent to the red and the black"

- **Text (summary):** Jabir: "I was given five things no one before me was given: every prophet was sent to his own people specifically, and I was sent to all the red and the black; the spoils were made lawful for me...; the earth was made a mosque and pure for me...; I was aided by terror at a month's distance; and I was given intercession." Urdu gist: ہر نبی اپنی قوم کی طرف بھیجا گیا، مجھے سرخ و سیاہ سب کی طرف بھیجا گیا۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 521 (Kitab al-Masajid); parallel in Sahih al-Bukhari 335, 438.
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:521
- **Notes:** "The red and the black" is glossed as Arabs and non-Arabs, or humans and jinn, or all colours of mankind (al-Nawawi). Together with 10:47 "for every nation there is a messenger" and 35:24 "there is no nation but a warner passed among it", this is the textual basis for "prophets were sent to every nation".

### 6.2 "You are all from Adam and Adam is from dust; no Arab has superiority over a non-Arab..."

- **Text (summary):** From the sermon at Mina during the days of tashriq: "O people, your Lord is one and your father is one. No Arab has superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab over an Arab, nor a red over a black, nor a black over a red, except by taqwa. Have I conveyed?" Urdu gist: تمہارا رب ایک، تمہارا باپ ایک؛ عربی کو عجمی پر، گورے کو کالے پر کوئی فضیلت نہیں سوائے تقویٰ کے۔
- **Source:** Musnad Ahmad 23489 (from "a man who heard the Prophet's sermon at Mina", via Abu Nadrah); al-Harith, Bughyat al-Bahith 51; Abu Nu'aym, Ma'rifat al-Sahabah 7300. Jabir's wording: Abu Nu'aym, Hilyah 3/100; al-Bayhaqi, Shu'ab al-Iman 5137.
- **Grade:** Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Musnad 23489): «إسناده صحيح»; (takhrij Sharh al-Tahawiyyah 510): its men are trustworthy, isnad sahih. Ibn Taymiyyah, Iqtida' al-Sirat al-Mustaqim 1/412: isnad sahih. Al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 2700 (Jabir wording): isnad sahih; Sahih al-Targhib 2964: sahih li-ghayrihi. Al-Wadi'i, al-Sahih al-Musnad 1523: sahih. Al-Busiri (Ithaf 1/218): men trustworthy, with a note on al-Jurayri's late confusion, "and it has witnesses."
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com (the site's Musnad Ahmad coverage stops well before 23489; https://sunnah.com/ahmad:23489 returns 404).
- **Notes:** "Children of Adam, and Adam is from dust" also occurs in Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3955 (al-Tirmidhi: hasan gharib; Darussalam: hasan) and 3956 (al-Tirmidhi: hasan sahih, "and this is more sound with us than the first"; Darussalam: hasan), both from Abu Hurairah: "Allah has removed from you the arrogance of Jahiliyyah and its boasting about ancestors; (a person is) a pious believer or a wretched sinner; people are the children of Adam and Adam is from dust." Links: https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3955 , https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3956

### 6.3 Whoever hears of the Prophet and does not believe enters the Fire

- **Text (summary):** "By Him in Whose hand is Muhammad's soul, no one of this community, Jew or Christian, hears of me and then dies without believing in that with which I was sent, but he will be of the people of the Fire." Urdu gist: جو یہودی یا نصرانی میرے بارے میں سنے اور ایمان لائے بغیر مرے وہ جہنمی ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 153 (Kitab al-Iman). Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:153
- **Notes:** The condition "hears of me" (يسمع بي) is the pivot: accountability begins when the message actually reaches a person. Scholars (al-Ghazali in Faysal al-Tafriqah; Ibn Taymiyyah; Ibn al-Qayyim) distinguish between one to whom the message reached in a form that establishes the proof (بلوغ الحجة) and one who heard only a distorted rumour; the latter is treated like ahl al-fatra by many.

## 7. "In His image", the Adam-Musa debate, and the covenant of Alast

### 7.1 "Allah created Adam in His image"

- **Text (summary):** Bukhari 6227 / Muslim 2841: "Allah created Adam on his/His image (على صورته), sixty cubits tall..." Muslim 2612e: "When one of you fights his brother, let him avoid the face, for Allah created Adam on His image." Urdu gist: اللہ نے آدمؑ کو "اس کی صورت" پر بنایا؛ چہرے پر مارنے سے منع فرمایا۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6227 (Kitab al-Isti'dhan); Sahih Muslim 2841 and 2612e (Kitab al-Birr).
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh). The **added wording "on the image of al-Rahman"** (Ibn Umar) is separately graded: Ibn Khuzaymah, Kitab al-Tawhid 1/85: "it has three defects"; al-Daraqutni, al-Ilal 3077: the mursal (from Ata') is more correct; al-Albani: da'if (al-Silsilah al-Da'ifah 1176; takhrij al-Sunnah 517); Ibn Taymiyyah, Bayan Talbis al-Jahmiyyah 6/441: "the least that can be said of this wording is that it is hasan"; Ibn Hajar, Fath 5/217: its men are trustworthy; al-Hakim 3285: sahih on the two Shaykhs' conditions; Ibn Baz: sahih.
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6227 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2612e , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2841
- **Notes (classical interpretations, briefly):**
  - Ibn Hajar, Fath al-Bari (Anbiya, on 3326, read on islamweb): Abd al-Razzaq's wording "on his image, his height sixty cubits" "supports those who say the pronoun refers to Adam", meaning Allah brought him into existence in the form he was created in, without passing through embryonic stages, a complete man from the moment the rooh was breathed. He also cites the view "on his image, i.e. no one shared in creating him", and (in the Isti'dhan chapter) al-Maziri's list: the pronoun refers to Adam, or to the man being struck (in the "avoid the face" wording), or to Allah as an idafah of honour, like "the she-camel of Allah".
  - Ibn Khuzaymah (Kitab al-Tawhid, bab dhikr al-akhbar allati...): held the pronoun refers to the struck man / Adam, and rejected the "surat al-Rahman" wording as an error (three defects); Ibn Taymiyyah criticised him at length for this in Bayan Talbis al-Jahmiyyah and reported from Ahmad and Ishaq that the salaf understood the pronoun as referring to Allah, affirmed without likeness ("there is nothing like unto Him", 42:11). This is also Ibn Baz's position (Majmu' Fatawa 25/128).
  - For the podcast: the safe formulation is that the hadith is sahih, that the salaf and later Sunni theologians differed on the referent of the pronoun, and that no school of Ahl al-Sunnah takes it to mean physical resemblance.

### 7.2 The Adam-Musa debate

- **Text (summary):** "Adam and Musa argued. Musa said: O Adam, you are our father, you disappointed us and expelled us from Paradise. Adam said: O Musa, Allah chose you with His speech and wrote for you with His hand; do you blame me for a matter Allah decreed for me forty years before He created me? So Adam defeated Musa in argument (فحج آدم موسى)", three times. Urdu gist: آدمؑ اور موسیٰؑ کا مناظرہ؛ آدمؑ نے کہا مجھے اس بات پر ملامت کرتے ہو جو میری پیدائش سے چالیس سال پہلے لکھی جا چکی تھی؟
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6614 (Kitab al-Qadar), also 3409, 4736, 4738, 7515; Sahih Muslim 2652.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6614
- **Notes:** The classical reading (Ibn Taymiyyah, Ibn al-Qayyim, Shifa' al-Alil) is that Adam won not by excusing sin with qadar but by pointing out that the **consequence** (expulsion, which became the origin of human life on earth) was decreed; blame for a sin that was repented of is not permissible.

### 7.3 The covenant of Alast (7:172)

- **Text (summary):** Umar ibn al-Khattab was asked about 7:172 and said: I heard the Prophet, when asked about it, say: "Allah created Adam, then wiped his back with His right hand and brought out from him offspring, and said: I created these for Paradise and they will do the deeds of the people of Paradise; then He wiped his back and brought out offspring and said: I created these for the Fire..." A man asked: then what use are deeds? "When Allah creates a servant for Paradise He employs him in the deeds of its people until he dies on one of them..." Urdu gist: عمرؓ کی روایت: اللہ نے آدمؑ کی پشت پر ہاتھ پھیرا اور اولاد نکالی، کچھ جنت کے لیے کچھ جہنم کے لیے۔
- **Source:** Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3075; Muwatta Malik, Kitab al-Qadar, Book 46 hadith 2 (Arabic no. 1627); Sunan Abi Dawud 4703; Musnad Ahmad 311; al-Nasa'i al-Kubra 11190; Sahih Ibn Hibban.
- **Grade:** Al-Tirmidhi: «هذا حديث حسن، ومسلم بن يسار لم يسمع من عمر، وقد ذكر بعضهم في هذا الإسناد بين مسلم بن يسار وبين عمر رجلا مجهولا» (hasan, but Muslim ibn Yasar did not hear from Umar, and some insert an unknown man between them). Darussalam: da'if (Tirmidhi 3075); Musnad Ahmad 311 on sunnah.com: "Sahih because of corroborating evidence, isnad da'if" (Darussalam). Al-Albani on Abu Dawud 4703: «صحيح إلا مسح الظهر» (sahih except the wiping-of-the-back clause). Ibn Kathir (on 7:172) lists the routes and accepts it as supported.
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3075 , https://sunnah.com/malik/46/2 , https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4703 , https://sunnah.com/ahmad:311
- **Notes:**
  - The **Ibn Abbas report** "Allah took the covenant from Adam's back at Nu'man (Arafah) and brought out from his loins every soul He would create, like tiny ants, and made them testify: Am I not your Lord?" (Musnad Ahmad 2455; al-Nasa'i al-Kubra 11191; al-Hakim 75 and 4048: sahih al-isnad) is graded by Ibn Kathir "isnad jayyid, strong, on Muslim's condition, **but mawquf is more established**" (al-Bidaya 1/83); Ibn Mandah: seven narrators transmit it mawquf; al-Nasa'i: Kulthum ibn Jabr is not strong; al-Albani: sahih (Sharh al-Tahawiyyah takhrij 240); Shu'ayb: isnad on Muslim's condition; Ahmad Shakir: isnad sahih.
  - **Ibn Kathir on 7:172** (read on tafsir.app): the hadiths establish that Allah brought Adam's offspring out of his loins and distinguished the people of Paradise from the people of the Fire, "but the taking of testimony from them there that He is their Lord occurs only in the hadith of Kulthum ibn Jabr from Ibn Abbas and the hadith of Abd Allah ibn Amr, and we have shown both are mawquf, not marfu'. Hence some of the salaf and khalaf said: what is meant by this witnessing is **their being created upon the fitrah of tawhid**," citing Bukhari 1385, Muslim 2865, and al-Hasan al-Basri's explanation of the verse. Ibn Kathir then argues for the fitrah reading from the wording "from the children of Adam, from their backs" (plural), i.e. generation after generation.
  - **Adam gives forty years to Dawud (item 26):** Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3076: "When Allah created Adam He wiped his back and every soul He would create fell from it; He put a gleam of light between the eyes of each... Adam saw Dawud, asked his lifespan, was told sixty years, and said: Lord, add forty years from my life to his. When Adam's life ended the angel of death came; Adam said: do I not have forty years left? He said: did you not give them to your son Dawud? Adam denied, so his offspring deny; Adam forgot, so his offspring forget; Adam sinned, so his offspring sin." Al-Tirmidhi: «هذا حديث حسن صحيح، وقد روي من غير وجه عن أبي هريرة»; Darussalam: hasan; al-Hakim 2/325: sahih on Muslim's conditions; Ibn Mandah: sahih. Link: https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3076 . The Ibn Abbas version with "sixty years given" and "Allah completed Dawud's to 100 and Adam's to 1,000" (Musnad Ahmad 2713; al-Tayalisi 2815) is graded by Shu'ayb "hasan li-ghayrihi except the completion clause", and Ibn Kathir says of Ali ibn Zayd in it: "nakarah" (al-Bidaya 1/208). So **Adam's 1,000-year lifespan is in a hasan-sahih marfu' hadith** (Tirmidhi 3076 implies it: "was written for me a thousand years", explicit in the Ahmad wording). Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/232, reconciles with the Torah's 930: the Torah counts only his years on earth after descent, 930 solar = 957 lunar, plus 43 years in Paradise = 1,000.

## 8. Israiliyyat and weak reports about Adam's story

### 8.1 Adam descended in India (Sarandib / Sri Lanka); Hawwa in Jeddah

- **Text (summary):** Various reports: "Allah sent Adam down at Dahna in the land of India" (Ibn Abbas, via al-Tabari); "Adam was sent down in India, Hawwa in Jeddah, Iblis in Dastumaysan near Basra, and the serpent in Isfahan" (al-Hasan al-Basri, via Ibn Abi Hatim); "Adam came down in India with the Black Stone and a handful of the leaves of Paradise, which he scattered in India so that the perfume tree grew there" (al-Suddi); "Adam came down at al-Safa and Hawwa at al-Marwah" (Ibn Umar, via Ibn Abi Hatim); the Sarandib mountain named "Nud/Budh" (al-Qurtubi).
- **Source:** Tarikh al-Tabari (Ibn Abbas, Ibn Ishaq); Ibn Abi Hatim; collected in Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya 1/187 (read on shamela) and 1/231 (on his burial: "the well-known view is that he was buried at the mountain on which he descended in India; it is said at Abu Qubays in Makkah; and it is said Nuh carried him and Hawwa in a coffin at the time of the Flood and buried them in Bayt al-Maqdis").
- **Grade:** All are athar (statements of Companions or Tabi'in) or Ahl al-Kitab material; **no marfu' hadith, sahih or weak, names the place of descent.** The reports contradict one another (India / Safa and Marwah / Makkah / Jerusalem). Ibn Kathir himself, introducing this section (1/202): "much of this is taken from the Israiliyyat" (وإن كان كثير منه متلقى من الإسرائيليات).
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/186 (descent), https://shamela.ws/book/4445/230 (burial)
- **Notes:** The "Adam's Peak" (Sri Pada) tradition is medieval travel lore (Ibn Battuta describes it; Buddhists, Hindus and Christians assign the same footprint to the Buddha, Shiva and St Thomas). No Islamic textual authority attaches to it.

### 8.2 The Hinn and Binn (pre-Adamic creatures) and jinn inhabiting the earth before Adam

- **Text (summary):** On 2:30 "the angels said: will You place in it one who spreads corruption and sheds blood?": (a) Ibn Abbas: "The first to dwell on the earth were the jinn; they spread corruption and shed blood and killed one another; so Allah sent Iblis against them with an army of angels, who drove them to the islands of the seas and the edges of the mountains; then He created Adam and settled him there." (b) Qatadah: the angels knew this "from what they had seen of those who were before Adam, the Hinn and the Binn"; Abd Allah ibn Umar: "the Hinn and the Binn were two thousand years before Adam; they shed blood, so Allah sent an army of angels who drove them to the islands of the seas." (c) Abu al-Aliyah (via al-Rabi' ibn Anas): the angels were created on Wednesday, the jinn on Thursday, Adam on Friday; a people of the jinn disbelieved and the angels used to descend and fight them. Urdu gist: آدمؑ سے پہلے زمین پر جن (یا "حن اور بن") آباد تھے جنہوں نے فساد کیا تو فرشتوں نے انہیں جزیروں کی طرف بھگا دیا۔
- **Source:** Tafsir al-Tabari on 2:30, reports no. 601 (Ibn Abbas via al-Dahhak), 602 (al-Rabi' ibn Anas), 606 (Ibn Abbas: Iblis was of a tribe of angels called al-Hinn); Tarikh al-Tabari 1/43; al-Hakim, al-Mustadrak 2/261 (Ibn Abbas via Mujahid); Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/166 and Tafsir 1/127-128.
- **Grade:** All **mawquf / maqtu' athar, none marfu'.** Al-Tabari's isnad for no. 601 (Bishr ibn Umarah, from Abu Rawq, from al-Dahhak, from Ibn Abbas) is weak (editor Mahmud Shakir's note; al-Dahhak did not hear from Ibn Abbas and Bishr is weak). Al-Hakim's route via al-A'mash, from Bukayr ibn al-Akhnas, from Mujahid, from Ibn Abbas: "sahih al-isnad", al-Dhahabi agreed, but it is still Ibn Abbas's own statement, of the kind he is known to have taken from Ahl al-Kitab. The Hinn/Binn reports in Ibn Kathir are given **without any isnad** and are of Israiliyyat type.
- **Link:** https://tafsir.app/tabari/2/30 ; https://shamela.ws/book/4445/165
- **Notes:** The Quran itself (15:27 "and the jann We created before, from the fire of scorching wind") establishes only that jinn were created before Adam; the earth-dwelling, blood-shedding story is not from the Prophet. Any modern identification of "Hinn and Binn" or "pre-Adamic jinn" with Neanderthals or other hominins is speculation with no textual basis (see section 13).

### 8.3 Qabil married his sister; the sacrifice; the raven

- **Text (summary):** The Quran (5:27-31) gives only: two sons of Adam offered sacrifices, one was accepted, the other killed his brother, and a raven showed him how to bury the body; and Bukhari 3335 gives the sahih marfu' basis for the first murder ("no soul is killed unjustly but a share of it falls on the first son of Adam, for he was the first to institute killing"). Everything else (that each pregnancy produced a boy and a girl, that each son was to marry the twin sister of the other, that Qabil wanted his own more beautiful twin Iqlima/Qalima, that the fire consumed the accepted sacrifice, that Hawwa bore forty children in twenty pregnancies, that Adam saw 40,000 descendants before he died) is from Ibn Abbas, Ibn Mas'ud, Ibn Ishaq and Ahl al-Kitab via al-Tabari's Tarikh and Tafsir.
- **Source:** Tarikh al-Tabari; Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/215 (introduced with «وذكروا», "they mentioned", without isnad) and 1/224 (Ibn Ishaq via al-Tabari: forty children in twenty births; "it is said 120 births, the first Qabil and his sister Qalima, the last Abd al-Mughith and his sister Amat al-Mughith").
- **Grade:** Israiliyyat / historians' reports; **no marfu' sahih hadith** for the marriage of twins or the detail of the sacrifice. Sahih al-Bukhari 3335 (link: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3335 ) is the only sahih marfu' text beyond the Quran.
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/214 , https://shamela.ws/book/4445/223
- **Notes:** Ibn Kathir accepts the twin-marriage explanation as the standard tafsir of how humanity multiplied from one couple, but it remains an inference, not revelation.

### 8.4 "Adam was 6,000 years before the Prophet" / dates of Adam

- **Text (summary):** No hadith. The figures in the books come from: (a) Ahl al-Kitab genealogies (Genesis 5, quoted by Ibn Kathir 1/223: Adam 130 at Seth's birth... Lamech 182 at Nuh's birth, Nuh 500 at the birth of Sam, Ham and Yafith); (b) the Persian (Majus) claim of 3,139 years from Jayumart, "whom they claim is Adam", to the Hijrah (Tarikh al-Tabari 1/18); (c) the athar on the total age of the world (section 12).
- **Source:** Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/223-224; Tarikh al-Tabari 1/10-18.
- **Grade:** Not marfu'. Ibn Kathir on the Torah dates, verbatim: «وفي كون هذه التواريخ محفوظة فيما نزل من السماء نظر، كما ذكره غير واحد من العلماء طاعنين عليهم في ذلك، والظاهر أنها مقحمة فيها» ("that these dates are preserved from what came down from heaven is doubtful; more than one scholar has criticised them on this, and the apparent case is that they were inserted"). He adds that the Torah text contains "much error".
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/222 , https://shamela.ws/book/9783/16
- **Notes:** Ibn Kathir does compute Nuh's birth as 126 years after Adam's death (per Ibn Jarir) or 146 (per Ahl al-Kitab), but on the same page insists the gap was "ten qurun", which on the generation reading is "thousands of years" (see 3.4). There is therefore **no Islamic authority for "Adam lived about 6,000 years ago"** beyond borrowed Jewish and Persian chronologies.

## 9. Are non-mukallaf creatures judged? Animals on the Day of Judgment

### 9.1 The hornless sheep and the horned sheep

- **Text (summary):** "Rights will be restored to their owners on the Day of Resurrection, so that even the hornless sheep will be given retaliation against the horned sheep." Urdu gist: قیامت کے دن حقوق ادا کیے جائیں گے حتیٰ کہ بے سینگ بکری کا سینگ والی سے بدلہ لیا جائے گا۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2582 (Kitab al-Birr). Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2582
- **Notes:** Al-Nawawi: this is a retaliation of pure justice (قصاص مقابلة), not of accountability (تكليف), since animals are not mukallaf; it shows the completeness of divine justice, not that animals are rewarded or punished for religious obligation.

### 9.2 "Be dust" (kuni turaban): animals turned to dust after retaliation

- **Text (summary):** After Allah settles the animals' claims against one another, He says to them "be dust", at which the disbeliever says "would that I were dust" (78:40). Urdu gist: جانوروں کا حساب ہونے کے بعد اللہ کہے گا "مٹی ہو جاؤ" تو کافر کہے گا کاش میں مٹی ہوتا۔
- **Source:** Tafsir al-Tabari 24/418 (on 78:40) and al-Hakim 3231, from Abu Hurairah (mawquf-type tafsir report); a longer Abu Hurairah marfu' version in al-Bayhaqi, al-Ba'th wa al-Nushur 609, Ishaq ibn Rahawayh 10, Ibn Abi al-Dunya, al-Ahwal 55.
- **Grade:** The short Abu Hurairah wording: al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 4/609 (no. 1966, number from memory; volume/page per dorar): "qawi" (strong); Ahmad Shakir (Umdat al-Tafsir 1/772): isnad sahih. The **long** version (Bayhaqi 609 route): al-Bukhari: "did not prove sound"; Ahmad: "its men are unknown"; Ibn Hibban: "I do not rely on its isnad" (all in Tahdhib al-Tahdhib 9/524); Ibn Kathir, Tafsir 3/276: "very gharib"; Ahmad Shakir: "apparent nakarah". The Ibn Abbas / Yazid ibn al-Asamm tafsir statement: al-Hakim 3273, on Muslim's conditions.
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com. Grader entries: https://dorar.net/hadith/search?q=للبهائم+كوني+ترابا
- **Notes:** Usable as an established tafsir of 78:40 with a strong short route; the elaborate long narration should not be quoted as Prophetic.

## 10. Adam's language, navel, height in Paradise vs earth, and other details

### 10.1 Adam's language

- **Text (summary):** Claims that Adam spoke Arabic in Paradise and Syriac after the fall, or that "Arabic is the language of the people of Paradise".
- **Source / Grade:** No hadith on Adam's language, sahih or weak, is found in the primary collections. Ibn Kathir (al-Bidaya 1/283) records the historians' view that **Isma'il** was "the first to speak clear, eloquent Arabic", having learned Arabic from Jurhum. The famous "Love the Arabs for three reasons: I am an Arab, the Quran is Arabic, and the speech of the people of Paradise is Arabic" is graded **mawdu'** (fabricated) by al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Da'ifah 160, following earlier critics **(number from memory, not re-verified online)**. The "Syriac after the fall" story appears in al-Tha'labi-type qisas literature without isnad.
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/283
- **Notes:** Verdict: baseless as hadith.

### 10.2 Adam's navel

- **Text (summary):** Folk claim that Adam had no navel (because he was not born) or, conversely, that his navel was the first thing created.
- **Source / Grade:** No hadith or classical athar found. Baseless; a theological puzzle from medieval Christian art, not from Islamic sources.

### 10.3 Adam's height in Paradise vs on earth

- **Text (summary):** "When Adam was sent down his feet were on the earth and his head in the sky; so Allah reduced him to sixty cubits" (Abd al-Razzaq, from Hisham ibn Hassan, from Sawwar, from Ata' ibn Abi Rabah; a similar statement from Ibn Abbas).
- **Source:** Musannaf Abd al-Razzaq; cited by Ibn Hajar (Fath, Anbiya) and Ibn Kathir (al-Bidaya 1/214).
- **Grade:** Maqtu' (Ata's statement) / mawquf; Ibn Hajar: the sahih hadith's apparent meaning is that he was created at sixty cubits, "and that is the reliable position"; Ibn Kathir: «وفي هذا نظر» because the agreed sahih hadith says "Allah created Adam and his height was sixty cubits, and the creation has not stopped decreasing", which requires that he was never taller than sixty cubits.
- **Notes:** Verdict: not sahih; contradicts the muttafaq alayh wording.

### 10.4 "Sixty cubits tall and seven cubits wide"

- **Source:** Musnad Ahmad 10913; al-Bazzar 7844; Ibn Asakir 7/391 (Abu Hurairah).
- **Grade:** Al-Albani (Hidayat al-Ruwat 5668): sahih **except the width clause**; Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Musnad 10913): sahih except "seven cubits wide"; Ibn Hajar (Tuhfat al-Nubala' 132; al-Kafi al-Shaf 278): Ali ibn Zayd in it is weak; Ibn al-Qaysarani: Ali ibn Zayd "is nothing".
- **Notes:** Quote the sixty cubits only; the "seven cubits wide" is weak.

### 10.5 Hawwa's name

- See 5.2: the name is in Sahih al-Bukhari 3330 / Muslim 1470. Established. The etymology "from hayy" is a tafsir statement (Ibn Abbas via al-Razi), not hadith.

## 11. Nuh (AS): the flood and his age

### 11.1 Nuh the first messenger to the people of the earth (hadith of intercession)

- **Text (summary):** On the Day of Resurrection people go to Adam, who sends them to Nuh: "O Nuh, you are the first of the messengers to the people of the earth (أول الرسل إلى أهل الأرض), and Allah named you a thankful servant..." Urdu gist: شفاعت کی حدیث میں لوگ نوحؑ سے کہیں گے: آپ زمین والوں کی طرف پہلے رسول ہیں۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3340 (Ahadith al-Anbiya), also 4712, 7410; Sahih Muslim 194. Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3340 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:194
- **Notes:** "First messenger to the people of the earth" is reconciled with Adam's prophethood (3.1) by the nabi/rasul distinction: Adam was a prophet spoken to, Nuh the first sent with a law to a disbelieving people (Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/238: "he was the first messenger sent to the people of the earth, as the people of the standing-place will say to him"). Together with 37:77 "We made his descendants the survivors" and 71:26 "do not leave upon the earth a single dweller among the disbelievers", it is the textual basis on which the classical scholars held that **all present humanity descends from Nuh** and that the flood destroyed all mankind outside the ark.

### 11.2 Did the flood cover the whole earth? (classical statements and their status)

- **Text (summary):** Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/263-264, read verbatim: «قال جماعة من المفسرين: ارتفع الماء على أعلى جبل بالأرض خمسة عشر ذراعا، وهو الذي عند أهل الكتاب. وقيل: ثمانين ذراعا. وعم جميع الأرض؛ طولها والعرض، سهلها وحزنها، وجبالها، وقفارها ورمالها، ولم يبق على وجه الأرض ممن كان بها من الأحياء عين تطرف، ولا صغير ولا كبير» ("A group of the exegetes said: the water rose fifteen cubits above the highest mountain on earth, and that is what Ahl al-Kitab have; it is said eighty cubits; and it covered the whole earth, its length and breadth, plain and rugged, mountains, deserts and sands, and no living eye that blinks remained on the face of the earth, small or great"). He then quotes Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam: "the people of that time had filled the plain and the mountain", and Abd al-Rahman ibn Zayd: "there was no spot on earth without an owner" (both via Ibn Abi Hatim).
- **Source:** Al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya 1/263-264; the "fifteen cubits" is Genesis 7:20.
- **Grade:** **No marfu' hadith, sahih or hasan, states the geographical extent of the flood.** The "fifteen cubits above the highest mountain" is explicitly labelled by Ibn Kathir as Ahl al-Kitab material; the "eighty cubits" is an anonymous "qila". The universality of the flood in classical Islam is an **inference** from the Quranic verses (71:26, 37:77, 11:43 "no protector today from the command of Allah") and from Bukhari 3340, not from a hadith describing the waters.
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/263
- **Notes:** The classical consensus reading is universal destruction of mankind; whether "the earth" (الأرض) in 71:26 means the globe or the inhabited land of Nuh's people is a modern question, and a minority of modern writers have read it regionally. The podcast's geologist can be told honestly: the Islamic texts commit to the destruction of all of Nuh's people and the survival of humanity only through the ark; the "water above Everest" imagery is Genesis-derived.

### 11.3 Sam, Ham and Yafith as fathers of the Arabs, the Africans and the Romans

- **Text (summary):** "Sam is the father of the Arabs, Yafith the father of the Romans, and Ham the father of the Abyssinians." Urdu gist: سام عربوں کے، یافث رومیوں کے اور حام حبشیوں کے باپ ہیں۔
- **Source:** Jami' al-Tirmidhi 3931 (Chapters on Virtues), and 3231 in another numbering; Musnad Ahmad 20099/20111/20114. Narrator: Samurah ibn Jundub, via al-Hasan al-Basri.
- **Grade:** Al-Tirmidhi: «هذا حديث حسن» (read from the Arabic on sunnah.com). **Al-Albani: da'if** (Da'if al-Tirmidhi 3931; al-Silsilah al-Da'ifah 3683; Da'if al-Jami' 3214). Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut (Musnad 20114): isnad da'if. Darussalam: da'if. Al-Iraqi: hasan. Al-Shawkani and Siddiq Hasan Khan: "from al-Hasan's hearing from Samurah, about which there is a known dispute" (the weakness is al-Hasan al-Basri's hearing from Samurah).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3931
- **Notes:** Ibn Kathir (al-Bidaya 1/268-269) quotes it and adds Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib's mawquf: "Sam fathered the Arabs, Persians and Romans; Yafith the Turks, Slavs, and Gog and Magog; Ham the Copts, Sudan (Africans) and Berbers", and notes the Abu Hurairah marfu' version of that (al-Bazzar) has Yazid ibn Sinan, "very weak, not to be relied on" (1/270). The ethnographic table of Genesis 10 is the source of all of this. The story that Ham was cursed and his descendants blackened (1/270: "it has been mentioned that Ham had relations with his wife in the ark, so Nuh prayed his seed be deformed, and a black son Kan'an was born") is Israiliyyat with no isnad; Ibn Kathir cites it as "qad dhukira".

### 11.4 Nuh's age

- **Text (summary):** The Quran fixes only one number: he remained among his people "a thousand years less fifty" before the flood (29:14). Reports on his age at commissioning: 50, 350, or 480 years (the last attributed to Ibn Abbas by Ibn Jarir); Ahl al-Kitab: 600 at the flood and 350 after (total 950, Genesis 9:29); Ibn Abbas: 480 at commissioning + 950 of da'wah + 350 after the flood = 1,780. Urdu gist: قرآن میں صرف ساڑھے نو سو سال دعوت کا ذکر ہے؛ باقی اعداد اہلِ کتاب اور آثار سے ہیں۔
- **Source:** Al-Bidaya wa al-Nihaya 1/238-239 and 1/280-281 (read on shamela).
- **Grade:** **No marfu' hadith gives Nuh's total age.** Ibn Kathir on the Ibn Abbas 600 + 350 figure: «وفي هذا القول نظر، ثم إن لم يمكن الجمع بينه وبين دلالة القرآن فهو خطأ محض» ("this view is doubtful; and if it cannot be reconciled with the Quran it is pure error"), because the Quran requires 950 years of preaching before the flood; he concludes: "then Allah knows best how long he lived after that; if what is reported from Ibn Abbas is preserved (sent at 480, lived 350 after the flood), he lived 1,780 years."
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/280 , https://shamela.ws/book/4445/238
- **Notes:** Popular Urdu figures such as "1,450 years" are arithmetic combinations of these athar; none is Prophetic. The safe statement is: "the Quran gives 950 years of preaching; his total lifespan is not stated in any sahih source."

## 12. The age of the world: "7,000 years", "6,000 years", and the sources

### 12.1 Tarikh al-Tabari, introduction ("How long is the whole span of time?")

- **Text (summary):** Al-Tabari (Tarikh, Dar al-Turath ed., 1/10-17, read on shamela): "The salaf before us differed. Some said the whole of it is **seven thousand years**: Ibn Abbas: 'The world is a week of the weeks of the Hereafter, seven thousand years, of which six thousand two hundred have passed; and there will come upon it hundreds of years with no monotheist on it.' Others said **six thousand years**: Ka'b (al-Ahbar): 'The world is six thousand years'; Wahb ibn Munabbih: 'Five thousand six hundred years of the world have passed, and I know every era of it and its kings and prophets... the world is six thousand years.'" Al-Tabari then argues from the hadiths "I was sent with the Hour like these two (fingers)" and "this Ummah will not fall short of half a day (= 500 years)" that Ibn Abbas's 7,000-year figure is "the more correct of the two views" (1/16), and computes the total from the start of creation to the end as roughly 7,000 years, "a little more or less" (1/57).
- **Source:** Tarikh al-Rusul wa al-Muluk 1/10-17, 1/57. Chains: Ibn Abbas via Ibn Humayd, from Yahya ibn Wadih, from Yahya ibn Ya'qub, from Hammad, from Sa'id ibn Jubayr; Ka'b via al-A'mash from Abu Salih; Wahb via Abd al-Samad ibn Ma'qil.
- **Grade:** All **mawquf on Ibn Abbas or maqtu' on Ka'b al-Ahbar and Wahb ibn Munabbih**, the two Tabi'in most associated with Israiliyyat. The one marfu' report al-Tabari cites ("the huqub is eighty years, and a day of it is a sixth of the world", Abu Hurairah, via Zabban ibn Fa'id from Asim) he himself sets aside: «لو كان صحيحا سنده لم نعد القول به إلى غيره» ("had its isnad been sound we would not have gone beyond it to any other view", 1/17), i.e. al-Tabari did not consider it sound.
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/9783/8 , https://shamela.ws/book/9783/15 , https://shamela.ws/book/9783/55
- **Notes:** Al-Tabari also records (1/18) that "the Majus claim the span from the reign of Jayumart to the Hijrah of our Prophet is 3,139 years, and they claim Jayumart is Adam the father of mankind." This Persian reckoning and the Torah genealogies are the raw material of every "Adam lived X years ago" figure in the Islamic historical tradition.

### 12.2 "The six days of creation were 6,000 years" (Ibn Kathir's note)

- **Text (summary):** Ibn Kathir, al-Bidaya 1/214: some held that the six days of creation "were each a thousand years, as reported from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid and al-Dahhak, and chosen by Ibn Jarir", who then computed: Adam was created in the last hour of Friday; an hour is 83 years and 4 months; he lay as shaped clay 40 years before the rooh was breathed, and stayed in Paradise 43 years and 4 months before descending. CORRECTION 2026-09-17 (page re-read on shamela 4445/213): Ibn Kathir's «وفي هذا نظر» here falls on the ordinary-days / "part of a day in Paradise" option; the 6,000-year computation is merely reported and closed with «والله تعالى أعلم». The second «وفي هذا نظر» on the page is on the "head in the sky" report. Do not cite 1/214 as Ibn Kathir rejecting the 6,000-year computation.
- **Source:** Al-Bidaya 1/214; Tafsir al-Tabari on 7:54.
- **Grade:** Exegetical calculation on the athar; no marfu' basis.
- **Link:** https://shamela.ws/book/4445/213

### 12.3 Al-Suyuti, "al-Kashf 'an mujawazat hadhihi al-ummah al-alf"

- **Text (summary):** A short treatise (printed in al-Hawi lil-Fatawi) in which al-Suyuti, building on the same 7,000-year athar and the "half a day = 500 years" hadith, argues that the Ummah's span will exceed 1,000 years after the Hijrah but not reach 1,500. **(Content known from the standard editions; not re-verified online in this session.)**
- **Grade:** A juristic-historical opinion resting on mawquf reports; later scholars (including Ibn Hajar before him, in Fath al-Bari on the hadith "I was sent with the Hour like these two", and modern muftis at islamqa/islamweb) hold that no sahih text fixes the age of the world or the date of the Hour, and that all such calculations are rejected on the basis of 7:187 and 31:34. **(Ibn Hajar's page not re-verified online.)**
- **Notes:** For the podcast: the honest summary is that the "6,000 / 7,000 years" numbers are (a) Jewish chronology (Ka'b, Wahb), (b) a Persian regnal count, and (c) a symbolic "week of the Hereafter" reading of Ibn Abbas; the only marfu' report was rejected by al-Tabari himself, and Ibn Kathir called the Torah dates "inserted".

## 13. Fabricated or baseless statements circulating online about Adam

1. **"Giant human skeletons found, proving Adam's 60-cubit height" (photographs).** Verdict: the images are digital composites from a 2002 online Photoshop contest and were debunked by National Geographic (2007) and by every archaeological body that examined them. No skeleton of a 27-metre human has ever been recovered. Citing them harms the sahih hadith rather than supporting it; Ibn Hajar's own honesty about the Thamud dwellings (1.1) is the better model.

2. **"Adam's footprint is on Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) in Sri Lanka."** Verdict: no hadith, sahih or weak; the India/Sarandib descent is a set of conflicting Companion and Tabi'i reports (8.1) with no Prophetic isnad, and the footprint itself is a shared Buddhist, Hindu, Christian and Muslim legend recorded by Ibn Battuta. Baseless as religion, interesting as history.

3. **"Neanderthals / earlier hominins were the jinn (or the Hinn and Binn) who lived before Adam."** Verdict: the Hinn/Binn reports are isnad-less athar (8.2); the Ibn Abbas report about jinn on earth is mawquf and from Israiliyyat-type material; neither says anything about hominins, and no scholar of the classical period linked them to any physical remains. Speculation, not evidence.

4. **"According to hadith, Adam lived about 6,000 years ago."** Verdict: no marfu' hadith gives any date (3.4, 8.4, 12). The figures come from the Torah genealogies (which Ibn Kathir called "inserted"), Ka'b al-Ahbar and Wahb ibn Munabbih, and the Persian regnal list; al-Tabari rejected the isnad of the only marfu' report. Ibn Kathir explicitly allowed that the Adam-to-Nuh gap alone could be "thousands of years".

5. **"Adam spoke Arabic; Arabic is the language of Paradise."** Verdict: the "language of Paradise" hadith is graded mawdu' (fabricated); no report on Adam's language exists in the primary collections (10.1). Ibn Kathir's history makes Isma'il, not Adam, the first speaker of eloquent Arabic.

6. **"The hadith says Adam was 90 feet / 30 metres tall on earth, and science confirms it."** Verdict: the hadith says sixty cubits and is sahih (1.1); the metric conversion is a modern gloss; and the leading classical commentator recorded that archaeology of ancient nations does not fit a continuous decrease. Contemporary scholars offer three answers (accept as unseen; height refers to the form in Paradise; the decrease was not linear), none of which claims scientific confirmation.

7. **"Hawwa's tomb is in Jeddah (Maqbarat Hawwa)."** Verdict: the only textual basis is the Tabi'i report "Hawwa was sent down at Jeddah" (al-Hasan al-Basri, 8.1), which says nothing of burial; the Jeddah tomb is medieval local tradition, demolished in 1928. No hadith.

8. **"Adam was created without a navel / Adam's grave is at Mount Abu Qubays / at Jerusalem / in India."** Verdict: the navel claim has no source at all; the grave reports are three contradictory athar listed by Ibn Kathir (1/231) with the words "the well-known view... it is said... it is said". Nothing is established.

9. **"The Prophet said the world is 7,000 years old."** Verdict: the 7,000-year figure is Ibn Abbas's statement, not the Prophet's (12.1); Ibn Abbas himself framed it as "a week of the weeks of the Hereafter", i.e. symbolically.

## 14. Is knowledge a liability? (accountability after the message reaches)

### 14.1 "Convey from me, even one ayah"

- **Text (summary):** "Convey from me, even if it is one ayah; and narrate from Banu Isra'il, there is no harm; and whoever lies about me deliberately, let him take his seat in the Fire." Urdu gist: میری طرف سے پہنچاؤ چاہے ایک آیت ہو؛ جو میرے بارے میں جان بوجھ کر جھوٹ بولے وہ جہنم میں اپنا ٹھکانہ بنا لے۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3461 (Ahadith al-Anbiya). Narrator: Abd Allah ibn Amr.
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3461
- **Notes:** The command to convey is paired in the same sentence with the threat against fabrication; knowledge received creates a duty to transmit accurately, which is exactly the burden this file exists to serve.

### 14.2 "If Allah guides one man through you, it is better for you than red camels"

- **Text (summary):** At Khaybar the Prophet gave Ali the standard and said: "Advance calmly until you reach their courtyard, then invite them to Islam and tell them what is due to Allah from them; for by Allah, if Allah guides a single man through you, it is better for you than to own red camels." Urdu gist: اگر تمہارے ذریعے اللہ ایک آدمی کو ہدایت دے دے تو یہ تمہارے لیے سرخ اونٹوں سے بہتر ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 4210 (al-Maghazi); Sahih Muslim 2406 (Fada'il al-Sahabah). Narrator: Sahl ibn Sa'd.
- **Grade:** Sahih (muttafaq alayh).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4210 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2406
- **Notes:** Red camels were the most prized wealth of Arabia; the hadith values one person's guidance above it. Note the order: invite first, then inform of obligations. Guidance is not forced.

### 14.3 "Whoever hears of me and dies without believing is of the people of the Fire" (the word "hears")

- **Text (summary):** "By Him in Whose hand is Muhammad's soul, no one of this community, Jew or Christian, **hears of me** (يسمع بي) and then dies without believing in that with which I was sent, but he will be of the people of the Fire." Urdu gist: جو یہودی یا نصرانی میرے بارے میں سنے اور اس پر ایمان لائے بغیر مرے وہ جہنمی ہے۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 153 (Kitab al-Iman). Narrator: Abu Hurairah.
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:153
- **Notes:** The operative verb is "hears" (يسمع), not "is born after" or "lives in the time of". Accountability is triggered by the message reaching a person; ahl al-fatra (4.1) are by definition those it did not reach. Al-Ghazali (Faysal al-Tafriqah) and later Ibn Taymiyyah and Ibn al-Qayyim discuss whether a distorted or hostile report counts as "hearing"; the majority hold that the proof is established (بلوغ الحجة) when the message reaches in a form that a reasonable person could investigate, so "hearing" here is not satisfied by rumour alone but is satisfied by real access. For the podcast: knowledge is indeed a liability in this precise sense, and ignorance without access is not.

### 14.4 The hundred levels of Jannah; the people of the upper rooms seen like distant stars (ranks are earned)

- **Text (summary):** (a) "In Paradise are a hundred grades which Allah has prepared for those who strive in His cause; between every two grades is the distance between heaven and earth. So when you ask Allah, ask Him for al-Firdaws, for it is the middle and highest of Paradise, above it is the Throne of the Merciful, and from it the rivers of Paradise spring." (b) "The people of Paradise will look at the people of the rooms (الغرف) above them as you look at a brilliant star far away on the eastern or western horizon, because of the superiority (تفاضل) between them." They said: "O Messenger of Allah, are those the dwellings of the prophets that no one else reaches?" He said: "No, by Him in Whose hand is my soul, they are men who believed in Allah and affirmed the messengers." Urdu gist: جنت میں سو درجے ہیں، ہر دو درجوں کے بیچ زمین و آسمان کا فاصلہ؛ اہلِ جنت اوپر والے بالاخانوں کو دور کے چمکتے ستارے کی طرح دیکھیں گے؛ یہ صرف انبیاء کے نہیں بلکہ ایمان لانے والوں کے ہیں۔
- **Source:** (a) Sahih al-Bukhari 2790 (Kitab al-Jihad), Abu Hurairah. (b) Sahih al-Bukhari 3256 (Bad' al-Khalq), Abu Sa'id al-Khudri; Sahih Muslim 2831 (Kitab al-Jannah; on sunnah.com as 2830-2831a from Sahl ibn Sa'd and 2831b from Abu Sa'id).
- **Grade:** Sahih (al-Bukhari; muttafaq alayh for (b)).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2790 , https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3256 , https://sunnah.com/muslim:2831b
- **Notes:** The rooms are explicitly said to be reachable by ordinary believers, not reserved for prophets: rank in the Hereafter is earned by faith and deeds. This is the positive side of "knowledge as liability": greater knowledge and greater striving raise the rank; the same texts imply that those who knew and did nothing forfeit it.

### 14.5 The marfu' wording on the fitrah: "I created My servants all hunafa'"

- **Text (summary):** From the Prophet's sermon reporting Allah's words: "Every property I have granted a servant is lawful; and I created My servants all upon the right way (حنفاء كلهم), then the devils came to them and turned them away from their religion, forbade them what I made lawful, and commanded them to associate with Me that for which I sent down no authority. And Allah looked at the people of the earth and detested them, Arab and non-Arab, except remnants of the People of the Book..." Urdu gist: میں نے اپنے سب بندوں کو حنیف (سیدھی راہ پر) پیدا کیا، پھر شیطان آئے اور انہیں ان کے دین سے پھیر دیا۔
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2865 (Kitab al-Jannah). Narrator: Iyad ibn Himar al-Mujashi'i.
- **Grade:** Sahih (Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2865
- **Notes:** This is the marfu' companion to "every child is born on the fitrah" (4.4) and is the text Ibn Kathir uses (7:172) to read the Alast covenant as the fitrah. It also states that before the Prophet's mission most of mankind had drifted from that fitrah, "except remnants of the People of the Book": the pre-Islamic fatrah was real, and it is the reason the imtihan hadith (4.1) matters.

### 14.6 The test of ahl al-fatra: passing enters Jannah, refusing is dragged to the Fire (the exact clause)

- **Text (quoted precisely):** Al-Aswad ibn Sari' version (Musnad Ahmad 16301; Ibn Hibban 7357): «فيأخذ مواثيقهم ليطيعنه، فيرسل إليهم رسولا أن ادخلوا النار. قال: فوالذي نفس محمد بيده، لو دخلوها لكانت عليهم بردا وسلاما» ("He takes their pledges that they will obey Him, then sends to them a messenger: enter the Fire. By Him in Whose hand is Muhammad's soul, had they entered it, it would have been cool and safe for them"). Abu Hurairah version (Musnad Ahmad 16302; Ibn Abi Asim, al-Sunnah 404; al-Bayhaqi, al-I'tiqad p.169), the clause the podcast needs: «فمن دخلها كانت عليه بردا وسلاما، ومن لم يدخلها يُسحَب إليها» ("whoever enters it, it will be cool and safe for him; and **whoever does not enter it will be dragged into it**"). Urdu gist: جو حکم مان کر آگ میں داخل ہوگا اس کے لیے وہ ٹھنڈک اور سلامتی ہوگی، اور جو انکار کرے گا اسے کھینچ کر اس میں ڈالا جائے گا۔
- **Source:** Musnad Ahmad 16301 and 16302 (Shu'ayb ed.; 16344 and 16345 in the old pagination, 4/24); Sahih Ibn Hibban 7357; al-Bayhaqi, al-I'tiqad p.169 (with the "dragged" clause); Ibn Abi Asim, al-Sunnah 404; al-Diya al-Maqdisi, al-Mukhtarah 1454-1456. Quoted with both clauses by Ibn Kathir on 17:15.
- **Grade:** Al-Aswad route: sahih (al-Albani, al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 1434; al-Bayhaqi; Ibn Kathir; Ibn al-Qayyim), hasan (Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut on the Musnad, sahih on Ibn Hibban). Abu Hurairah route with the "dragged" clause: isnad hasan (Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut, Musnad 16302); sahih, men of the Sahih (al-Wadi'i, al-Sahih al-Musnad 1453); isnad sahih (al-Bayhaqi, al-Qada' wa al-Qadar 645); isnad jayyid (Ibn Kathir, Jami' al-Masanid 444); al-Albani, Raf' al-Astar 113: "its men are trustworthy were it not for al-Hasan al-Basri's 'an'anah" (he nonetheless grades the Ibn Abi Asim wording sahih in his takhrij of al-Sunnah 404). Al-Busiri alone calls this route weak.
- **Link:** none on sunnah.com. Grader entries: https://dorar.net/hadith/search?q=ومن+لم+يدخلها+يسحب+إليها
- **Notes:** The clause settles the point the podcast must make: the fatrah excuse earns a **test**, not an automatic pass. Ibn al-Qayyim (Tariq al-Hijratayn p.864): "some of them will be in Paradise and some in the Fire; and with this all the evidences are reconciled." The weaker Anas version via Abu Ya'la (Layth ibn Abi Sulaym; Abd al-Warith "munkar al-hadith" per al-Bukhari) spells out the psychology, "those written for wretchedness say: Lord, how can we enter it when we fled from it? and those written for felicity rush into it", but should be cited only as an illustration, not as proof.

### 14.7 "Actions are judged by their endings" / "Allah judges each by what he knew"

- **Text (summary):** (a) Sahih: "A man may do the deeds of the people of the Fire while he is of the people of Paradise, and do the deeds of the people of Paradise while he is of the people of the Fire; **and actions are only (judged) by their endings** (وإنما الأعمال بالخواتيم)." Urdu gist: اعمال کا اعتبار خاتمے پر ہے۔ (b) "Allah will judge each person by what he knew": **no such hadith exists** in the primary collections, sahih or weak, in this or similar wording; the idea is a paraphrase of Quranic principles (2:286 "Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity"; 17:15 "We never punish until We send a messenger"; 65:7 "Allah does not burden anyone except with what He has given him"; 6:131 "so that no people are destroyed for wrongdoing while unaware") and of the imtihan hadiths, not a Prophetic text.
- **Source:** (a) Sahih al-Bukhari 6607 (Kitab al-Qadar), narrator Sahl ibn Sa'd; the clause is read from the Arabic on sunnah.com. (b) none.
- **Grade:** (a) Sahih (al-Bukhari). (b) No text; do not attribute it to the Prophet.
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6607
- **Notes:** For "judged by what he knew", the podcast should quote the Quran and the imtihan hadith, and may add the scholarly maxim (not a hadith) that "the proof is established by the reaching of the message, not by its acceptance" (تقوم الحجة ببلوغ الرسالة لا بفهمها, as Ibn Taymiyyah and Ibn al-Qayyim phrase the principle in different words). Never present it as a hadith.
