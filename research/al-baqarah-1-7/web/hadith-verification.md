# Hadith Verification — Surah Al-Baqarah 1-7 Research

### Virtues of Surah Al-Baqarah
- **Satan Flees:** "Indeed, Satan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited." (Sahih Muslim 780)
- **Blessing and Protection:** "Recite Surah Al-Baqarah, for taking recourse to it is a blessing and abandoning it is a cause of grief, and the magicians (batala) cannot confront it." (Sahih Muslim 804a)
- **Hump of the Quran:** "Everything has a hump (pinnacle), and the hump of the Quran is Surah Al-Baqarah." (Jami` at-Tirmidhi)

### Alif Lam Mim (Verse 1)
- **Reward per Letter:** "I do not say that Alif-Lam-Mim is one letter, but Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter and Mim is a letter." (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2910) - *Each letter earns 10 rewards.*

### Guidance and Taqwa (Verse 2)
- **No Doubt:** "Having doubt in the Quran is disbelief (kufr)." (Abu Dawud 4603)
- **Straight Path Parable:** Parable of the straight path with walls and open doors (limits of Allah). (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2859)

### Belief and Disbelief (Verses 6-7)
- **Sealing of the Heart:** "When a believer commits a sin, a black spot is placed on his heart... if he increases [in sin], the spot increases until it covers his heart. That is the 'Ran' (stain)." (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3334)
- **Steadfastness Supplication:** "O Turner of the hearts, make my heart steadfast upon Your religion." (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2140)
