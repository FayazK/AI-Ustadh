## Key hooks
- An-Nahl 16:68-69: أَوْحَىٰ (wahi/ilham to the bee), مِن بُطُونِهَا, مُّخْتَلِفٌ أَلْوَانُهُ, فِيهِ شِفَاءٌ لِّلنَّاسِ.
- "Rivers of honey in Paradise" = Qur'an 47:15, NOT a hadith.
- Honey is "free sugar" (WHO) — diabetics must count & limit; "safe for diabetics" is a myth.
- Female worker bees (feminine verb forms in 16:68-69); NAK debunks the over-claimed Harun-Yahya "miracle."
- Waggle dance (von Frisch, 1973 Nobel). Manuka MGO / Medihoney. Cough: Cochrane (Oduwole 2018); no honey under 12 months (infant botulism).
- Pakistan = 4th-highest diabetes burden (~36M); EU: ~46% of imported honey suspected adulterated.
