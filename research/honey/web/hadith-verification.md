# Hadith Verification — Honey (شہد / عسل)
**Fetched:** 2026-05-29
**Sources verified against:** sunnah.com (each hadith page fetched directly), islamqa.info for grading of weak narrations.

---

## Authentic (صحیح / حسن)

### 1. Healing is in three things (honey, cupping, cautery)
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated Ibn 'Abbas — the Prophet ﷺ said: "Healing is in three things: a gulp of honey (شَرْبَةِ عَسَلٍ), cupping (شَرْطَةِ مِحْجَمٍ), and branding with fire / cautery (كَيَّةِ نَارٍ). But I forbid my followers (Ummah) to use cauterization."
- **Arabic:** الشِّفَاءُ فِي ثَلاَثَةٍ شَرْبَةِ عَسَلٍ وَشَرْطَةِ مِحْجَمٍ وَكَيَّةِ نَارٍ وَأَنْهَى أُمَّتِي عَنِ الْكَىِّ
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5680 — Kitab al-Tibb (Book of Medicine, كتاب الطب). In-book ref: Book 76, Hadith 9. USC-MSA: Vol. 7, Book 71, Hadith 584.
- **Grade:** صحیح (Sahih — it is in Sahih al-Bukhari).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5680
- **Establishes:** Honey is named first among the three primary means of healing in Prophetic medicine. Note the Prophet ﷺ disliked/discouraged cautery even while mentioning it — important nuance for any "Prophet endorsed cautery" claim.

### 2. The man whose brother had a stomach complaint (diarrhea) — "Allah has spoken the truth"
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated Abu Sa'id al-Khudri — A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said, "My brother has abdominal trouble (his stomach is loose / diarrhea — اسْتَطْلَقَ بَطْنُهُ)." The Prophet ﷺ said, "Let him drink honey." The man returned a second and a third time with the same complaint, each time told "Let him drink honey." He then said, "I have done that (but it did not help)." The Prophet ﷺ said: "Allah has spoken the truth, and your brother's stomach has lied (صَدَقَ اللَّهُ وَكَذَبَ بَطْنُ أَخِيكَ). Let him drink honey." So he gave him honey and he was cured.
- **Arabic (opening):** أَنَّ رَجُلاً أَتَى النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ إِنَّ أَخِي اسْتَطْلَقَ بَطْنُهُ‏.‏ فَقَالَ "‏ اسْقِهِ عَسَلاً ‏"
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5684 — Kitab al-Tibb (Book of Medicine). In-book ref: Book 76, Hadith 13. USC-MSA: Vol. 7, Book 71, Hadith 588.
- **Cross-reference:** Also reported in Sahih Muslim 2217a — Kitab al-Salam (The Book of Greetings, كتاب السلام), Book 39, Hadith 122, chapter "Treating Sickness With A Drink Of Honey." Link: https://sunnah.com/muslim:2217a (page fetched; in the Muslim wording the man comes back saying the honey only made the bowels looser, repeated, then "Allah has spoken the truth and your brother's bowels are in the wrong"). Bukhari numbers confirmed directly; Muslim cited as a Sahih parallel.
- **Grade:** صحیح (Muttafaq 'alayh — agreed upon by Bukhari and Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5684
- **Establishes:** Honey as a remedy works by Allah's truth even when initial trials seem to fail; persistence in the prescribed cure. The classic basis for honey as treatment for digestive/abdominal ailments.

### 3. Aisha (RA): the Prophet ﷺ loved sweets and honey
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated Aisha (RA): The Prophet ﷺ used to love sweet edible things (الْحَلْوَاء) and honey (الْعَسَل).
- **Arabic:** كَانَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يُحِبُّ الْحَلْوَاءَ وَالْعَسَلَ
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5431 — Kitab al-At'imah (Book of Food / Meals, كتاب الأطعمة), chapter "Sweet edible things and honey" (باب الْحَلْوَاءِ وَالْعَسَلِ). In-book ref: Book 70, Hadith 59 (confirmed from the fetched page). USC-MSA web reference: Vol. 7, Book 65, Hadith 342.
- **Grade:** صحیح (Sahih — Sahih al-Bukhari).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5431
- **Establishes:** The Prophet's ﷺ personal love of honey — a Sunnah of preference, not merely a medicine. Useful for "honey is a Sunnah food" framing.

### 4. Honey / rivers in Paradise
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated (via Abu Hurairah, on the authority chain shown): Allah's Messenger ﷺ said: "Saihan, Jaihan (Jaxartes and Oxus), the Euphrates and the Nile are all from the rivers of Paradise."
- **Arabic:** سَيْحَانُ وَجَيْحَانُ وَالْفُرَاتُ وَالنِّيلُ كُلٌّ مِنْ أَنْهَارِ الْجَنَّةِ
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2839 — Kitab al-Jannah (The Book of Paradise, كتاب الجنة). In-book ref: Book 53, Hadith 49.
- **Grade:** صحیح (Sahih — Sahih Muslim).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2839
- **Establishes:** Rivers of Paradise theme. NOTE: this specific hadith names rivers but does NOT itself mention honey. The honey-river of Paradise is established by the **Qur'an, not this hadith** — Surah Muhammad 47:15: "...rivers of honey purified (أَنْهَارٌ مِنْ عَسَلٍ مُصَفًّى)." Use the Qur'an (47:15) as the primary proof for honey in Paradise; do not attribute a "rivers of honey" hadith to this narration.

## Weak / Fabricated (ضعیف / موضوع) — for myth-busting

### 5. "Take advantage of the two cures: honey and the Qur'an" — WEAK
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated from Ibn Mas'ud: the Prophet ﷺ said, "Take advantage of the two cures (الشِّفَاءَيْنِ): honey and the Qur'an (الْعَسَلِ وَالْقُرْآنِ)."
- **Arabic:** عَلَيْكُمْ بِالشِّفَاءَيْنِ الْعَسَلِ وَالْقُرْآنِ
- **Source:** Sunan Ibn Majah 3452 — Kitab al-Tibb (Chapters on Medicine). In-book ref: Book 31, Hadith 17. Also reported by al-Hakim and al-Bayhaqi.
- **Grade:** ضعیف (Da'if / weak). Listed as **Da'if by Darussalam** on the sunnah.com page (fetched directly); also weakened by **Shaykh al-Albani** in *Da'if Ibn Majah*. Weakness is in the isnad (chain).
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3452
- **Establishes (myth-busting):** Extremely popular and widely quoted, but the chain is weak — it should NOT be presented as a sound Prophetic hadith. Its meaning is, however, supported by authentic texts: honey as "healing for people" (Qur'an 16:69, An-Nahl) and the Qur'an as "a healing" (Qur'an 17:82, Al-Isra). So the idea is sound; the specific wording/attribution is weak.

### 6. "Whoever licks honey three mornings each month will not be stricken by a great calamity" — WEAK
- **Matn (meaning):** Narrated from Abu Hurairah: the Prophet ﷺ said, "Whoever licks honey three mornings each month (ثَلاَثَ غَدَوَاتٍ كُلَّ شَهْرٍ) will not be stricken by any serious calamity (عَظِيمٌ مِنَ الْبَلاَءِ)."
- **Arabic:** مَنْ لَعِقَ الْعَسَلَ ثَلاَثَ غَدَوَاتٍ كُلَّ شَهْرٍ لَمْ يُصِبْهُ عَظِيمٌ مِنَ الْبَلاَءِ
- **Source:** Sunan Ibn Majah 3450 — Kitab al-Tibb. In-book ref: Book 31, Hadith 15.
- **Grade:** ضعیف (Da'if / weak). Listed **Da'if by Darussalam** on the sunnah.com page (fetched directly). Ibn Hajar and al-Busiri also declared the chain weak (per IslamQA citing them). Note: some have even classed versions as موضوع (fabricated) and it appears in al-Albani's *Silsilat al-Ahadith al-Da'ifa wa al-Mawdu'a* — but the sunnah.com grade for Ibn Majah 3450 specifically is Da'if.
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3450
- **Establishes (myth-busting):** Circulated as a guaranteed monthly "immunity" routine, and sometimes FALSELY attributed to Bukhari/Tirmidhi (it is in NEITHER). The chain is weak — must not be cited as an authentic promise of protection from all major illness. Honey's benefit is real and Qur'an-affirmed (16:69), but this specific cure-all formula is not authentically established.

## Notes
- **Verification method:** Each sunnah.com page above was fetched directly and the Arabic + reference numbers transcribed from the page; gradings for the weak narrations cross-checked against islamqa.info (al-Albani / Darussalam).
- **Strongest authentic basis for "honey heals":** Qur'an 16:69 (An-Nahl) — "...there comes from their bellies a drink of varying colors, in which there is healing for people (فِيهِ شِفَاءٌ لِلنَّاسِ)" — and the two Sahih al-Bukhari hadith (5680, 5684). These are the safe, authentic foundations.
- **Strongest authentic basis for "honey in Paradise":** Qur'an 47:15 (Surah Muhammad) — "rivers of honey purified." This is Qur'anic, not a hadith; do not invent a "rivers of honey" hadith.
- **Key myth-busting points:**
  1. The "honey + Qur'an two cures" hadith (Ibn Majah 3452) is **weak**, despite its popularity.
  2. The "lick honey three mornings a month = no calamity" hadith (Ibn Majah 3450) is **weak** — not an authentic guarantee.
  3. The Sahih "healing in three things" hadith (Bukhari 5680) does NOT make honey a literal cure for *every* disease; "shifa'" here is general/contextual, and even the Prophet ﷺ discouraged cautery in the same breath. Avoid over-literal "cure-all" claims.
  4. No authentic hadith specifies "eating honey on an empty stomach" as a Prophetic prescription; that is a folk/medical recommendation, not established Sunnah.
- **No fabricated (موضوع) honey hadith with a confirmed mawdu' grading was located in this pass** — the two problematic narrations found are graded weak (ضعیف), not fabricated. If a mawdu'-graded honey narration is needed later, it would require a dedicated search; do not upgrade a "weak" to "fabricated" without a source.
