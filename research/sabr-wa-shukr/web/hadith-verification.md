# Hadith Verification: Sabr (Patience) & Shukr (Gratitude)

**Verified:** 2026-03-18
**Sources:** sunnah.com, abuaminaelias.com

## Sabr (Patience) Hadith

### 1. No gift better than patience
**Narrator:** Abu Sa'id al-Khudri
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1469, Sahih Muslim 1053
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "Whoever seeks to be patient, Allah will make him patient. Nobody can be given a blessing better and greater than patience."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1469

### 2. True patience is at the first stroke
**Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 1302
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "The real patience is at the first stroke of a calamity."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1302

### 3. Victory comes with patience
**Narrator:** Ibn Abbas
**Collection:** Musnad Ahmad; also in Nawawi's 40 Hadith (#19)
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic — verified by Al-Albani)
**Text:** "Victory comes with patience, relief with affliction, and with hardship comes ease."

### 4. Patience with people earns greater reward
**Narrator:** Ibn Umar
**Collection:** Sunan Ibn Majah 4032
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic — verified by Al-Albani)
**Text:** "The believer who mixes with people and bears their annoyance with patience will have a greater reward than the believer who does not mix with people and does not put up with their annoyance."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:4032

### 5. Patient on loss of eyesight
**Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 5653
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "If I deprive my slave of his two beloved things (eyes) and he remains patient, I will let him enter Paradise in compensation for them."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5653

### 6. The affair of the believer is all good
**Narrator:** Suhaib ar-Rumi
**Collection:** Sahih Muslim 2999
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "How wonderful is the affair of the believer, for his affairs are all good. If something good happens to him, he is thankful for it and that is good for him. If something bad happens to him, he bears it with patience and that is good for him."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2999

### 7. Holding onto embers
**Narrator:** Anas ibn Malik
**Collection:** Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3058
**Grading:** حسن (Good — graded Hasan by Tirmidhi)
**Text:** "Ahead of you are the days in which patience is like holding onto an ember, for the doer (of righteous deeds) during them is the like of the reward of fifty of those who do the like of what you do."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3058

### 8. Allah's gift of patience
**Narrator:** Abu Sa'id al-Khudri
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6470
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "Whoever remains patient, Allah will make him patient."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6470

## Shukr (Gratitude) Hadith

### 9. Should I not be a grateful slave?
**Narrator:** Al-Mughirah ibn Shu'bah
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 4837, Sahih Muslim 2819
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** When asked why he prayed until his feet cracked despite being forgiven, the Prophet (PBUH) replied: "Should I not be a grateful slave of Allah?"
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4837

### 10. He who does not thank people
**Narrator:** Abu Hurairah
**Collection:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4811, Jami` at-Tirmidhi 1954
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic — verified by Al-Albani)
**Text:** "He who does not thank the people is not thankful to Allah."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:4811

### 11. Help me remember You and be grateful
**Narrator:** Mu'adh ibn Jabal
**Collection:** Sunan Abi Dawud 1522, Sunan an-Nasa'i 1303
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** The Prophet took Mu'adh's hand and said: "O Mu'adh, by Allah I love you. I advise you to never forget to say after every prayer: 'Allahumma a'inni ala dhikrika, wa shukrika, wa husni 'ibadatika' (O Allah, help me remember You, be grateful to You, and worship You in an excellent manner)."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1522

### 12. Two blessings people waste
**Narrator:** Ibn Abbas
**Collection:** Sahih al-Bukhari 6412
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "There are two blessings which many people waste: health and free time."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6412

### 13. Prostration of gratitude
**Narrator:** Abu Bakrah
**Collection:** Sunan Abi Dawud 2774
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic — verified by Al-Albani)
**Text:** "The Prophet, if he received good news or something pleasing, he would fall down in prostration from gratitude to Allah."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2774

### 14. Al-Hamdulillah fills the scales
**Narrator:** Abu Malik al-Ash'ari
**Collection:** Sahih Muslim 223
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic)
**Text:** "'Al-hamdu lillah' fills the scales, and 'SubhanAllah wal-hamdulillah' fills what is between the heavens and the earth."
**Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:223

### 15. Take advantage of five before five
**Narrator:** Ibn Abbas
**Collection:** Shu'ab al-Iman (Bayhaqi), Mustadrak al-Hakim 7846
**Grading:** صحیح (Authentic — verified by Al-Albani)
**Text:** "Take advantage of five before five: your youth before your old age, your health before your illness, your riches before your poverty, your free time before your work, and your life before your death."
