# Current-Events Snapshot — Women's Parda / Hijab and Dress

A factual, balanced snapshot of where hijab and women's dress are in the headlines (2025–2026, with essential earlier context). Grouped by region/theme. Includes both restrictive (bans) and pro-hijab / supportive developments.

---

## 1. Iran — Mandatory hijab, shifting enforcement, and unrest

- **Enforcement shifts to businesses (mid-2025):** Rather than arresting individual women, Iran's judiciary increasingly targets businesses serving unveiled women. At least ~50 establishments (cafés, restaurants, wedding halls, clothing boutiques) were sealed between late June and early October 2025 across Tehran, Borujerd, Kashan, Shiraz, Fasa, Qom, Ahvaz, and Dezful under "improper hijab" pretexts. — Oct 2025, Center for Human Rights in Iran. https://iranhumanrights.org/2025/10/irans-new-tactics-to-crush-hijab-resistance-business-raids-and-surveillance/
- **Tech surveillance ("Noor plan"):** Since April 2024 the state has used traffic cameras to identify unveiled women in cars and mail automated tickets to license-plate holders, plus mobile patrol vans, fines, and "vigilantism." — UN Human Rights Council probe, March 2025. https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/03/1161126
- **Growing defiance:** Reporters observed a marked rise in women appearing unveiled in Tehran, including in traditionally conservative neighborhoods. — Oct 2025. https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/10/26/iran-women-hijab-veil-arrests/ ; https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/iran-women-hijab-dress-code-protests-9.7241566
- **Government division:** President Masoud Pezeshkian publicly conceded "coercion doesn't work" and that hijab "cannot be restored by force," while parliamentary hardliners pushed for new crackdowns. — Oct 2025, Iran International. https://www.iranintl.com/en/202510217391
- **Pro-hijab counter-protests:** Police dispersed weeks-long pro-hijab (pro-enforcement) demonstrations outside parliament in Tehran amid security concerns — showing the issue cuts both ways domestically. — March 2025, Al Jazeera. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/3/30/irans-police-disperses-pro-hijab-protest-amid-security-concerns
- **Late-2025/2026 protests (context):** Mass protests began ~28 Dec 2025 (shopkeeper strikes amid currency collapse, ~42% inflation), resurging Feb 2026. These were driven primarily by economic crisis, NOT hijab — but mandatory hijab features as a background grievance, and some women said the deadly unrest pushed them to defy the law further. — Wikipedia "2025–2026 Iranian protests"; Al Jazeera timeline Jan 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025%E2%80%932026_Iranian_protests ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/5/a-timeline-of-protests-in-iran-after-the-1979-islamic-revolution
- **Fact-check note:** Despite relaxed enforcement at times, Iran has NOT formally repealed the mandatory hijab law. — Outlook India fact-check. https://www.outlookindia.com/international/fact-check-no-iran-has-not-repealed-its-hijab-ban

> Note: Iran is the inverse of the European cases — here the state *compels* the hijab and women resist; in Europe the state *bans* it and women resist the ban. Both are coercion-of-dress stories.

## 2. Europe — Face-veil and headscarf bans expanding

- **Switzerland — national "burqa ban" in force:** A face-covering ban (implementing the 2021 referendum) took effect **1 January 2025**; fines up to CHF 1,000 (~$1,144). Exceptions for worship, health, safety, weather. — Library of Congress. https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2025-01-15/switzerland-burqa-ban-enters-into-force/
- **Portugal — face-veil ban passed:** Parliament approved a far-right bill banning face veils (for "gender or religious" reasons) on **17 Oct 2025**, with fines up to €2,000; veils still allowed on planes, in diplomatic premises, and places of worship. — Al Jazeera. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/17/portugals-parliament-approves-far-right-partys-bill-to-ban-face-veils
- **Austria — under-14 headscarf ban:** In 2025 Austria banned girls under 14 from wearing headscarves during school lessons and recess; law takes effect Sept 2026. — Wikipedia "Hijab and burqa controversies in Europe." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hijab_and_burqa_controversies_in_Europe
- **Denmark — bill to ban burqa/niqab in schools/universities:** Slated for parliament Feb 2026. — Canadian Affairs, Dec 2025. https://www.canadianaffairs.news/2025/12/24/denmark-wants-to-ban-burqas-niqabs-in-schools-and-universities/
- **Germany — CDU pushes burqa/niqab ban:** On 21 Feb 2026 the CDU advocated a public-space ban. — Wikipedia (Europe controversies, above).
- **Italy — Brothers of Italy plans niqab/burqa ban:** Announced Oct 2025. — Wikipedia (Europe controversies, above).
- **Belgium (context):** Face-veil ban since 2015; upheld by the European Court of Human Rights on 11 July 2017. — Wikipedia "Burqa by country."

## 3. France — Laïcité bans on headscarf, abaya, and sport

- **School abaya ban upheld:** Building on the 2004 ban on "ostentatious religious symbols" in state schools, France banned the abaya in 2023; the Council of State (highest administrative court) upheld it in Sept 2023. — US State Dept 2023 IRF report. https://2021-2025.state.gov/reports/2023-report-on-international-religious-freedom/france/
- **2024 Olympics hijab ban:** France barred its own Muslim athletes (as civil servants bound by laïcité) from wearing hijab at the Paris 2024 Games — uniquely among nations. UN experts called the sport bans "disproportionate and discriminatory" (Oct 2024). — Time; Amnesty. https://time.com/7000437/france-sporting-hijab-ban-olympics/ ; https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2024/07/france-hijab-bans-olympic-and-paralympic/
- **Move toward total sports ban (2025):** France is the only one of 38 European countries studied that bans religious headwear in sport (football, basketball, volleyball at all levels, including amateur). In 2025 authorities pushed a bill to extend hijab bans to all sports. Amnesty (Feb 2025) said this "would violate human rights and target Muslim women and girls." — Amnesty; Hyphen. https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/02/france-hijab-ban-in-all-sports-would-violate-human-rights-and-target-muslim-women-and-girls/ ; https://hyphenonline.com/2025/04/15/france-hijab-muslim-headscarves-total-ban-sport/

## 4. India — Karnataka hijab case still unresolved

- **Background:** Dispute began Jan 2022 (Udupi college barred hijab-wearing students). Karnataka govt order (5 Feb 2022) banned religious attire under the Karnataka Education Act 1983.
- **High Court (March 2022):** Ruled hijab is "not an essential religious practice" of Islam and upheld the ban.
- **Supreme Court:** A two-judge bench gave a split verdict (Oct 2022); the case remains pending before a larger bench.
- **Status (Feb 2025):** The ban in Karnataka educational institutions remains in effect until the Supreme Court delivers a final verdict; the state's education minister confirmed the dress code prohibiting headscarves continues. — Outlook India; Education Post. https://www.outlookindia.com/national/karnataka-hijab-case-supreme-court-reserves-verdict-on-plea-against-hc-judgement-upholding-hijab-ban-in-classes-news-225090 ; https://educationpost.in/news/education/hijab-ban-will-continue-in-karnataka-schools-until-supreme-court-ruling-says-education-minister

## 5. Tajikistan / Central Asia — State campaign against "foreign clothing"

- **Hijab ban law (June 2024):** Amendments to the 2007 Law on Regulation of Traditions and Rites (adopted 8 May 2024, in force 20 June 2024) ban importing, selling, and wearing clothes deemed "alien to national culture" — explicitly targeting the hijab. Fines: 8,000–54,000 Somoni (~€680–€4,600). — Euronews; IPHR. https://www.euronews.com/2024/06/24/why-did-muslim-majority-tajikistan-ban-the-hijab ; https://iphronline.org/articles/tajikistan-campaign-against-foreign-clothing-violates-human-rights/
- **Black clothing targeted:** Authorities have specifically discouraged black headscarves/hijab, associating them with "Arab influence." — IPHR (above).
- **Wider crackdown:** Police shaving men's beards at checkpoints, questioning headscarf-wearing women, demolishing mosques, monitoring markets for "foreign dress." Part of President Rahmon's decades-long secular-identity project. — IPHR; RFE/RL. https://www.rferl.org/a/tajikistan-islamic-hijab-ban-head-scarf/32979116.html
- **Ongoing (late 2025):** Rahmon reiterated concern over "clothing alien to national culture" on 16 Dec 2025; reports of stricter measures for women's clothing. — Asia-Plus; Newsgram. https://www.asiaplustj.info/en/news/tajikistan/power/20251216/emomali-rahmon-reiterates-concern-over-wearing-of-clothing-alien-to-the-national-culture ; https://www.newsgram.com/human-rights/2025/11/22/tajikistan-ban-hijabs-beards-miniskirts
- **Regional pattern:** Across Soviet-shaped Central Asia, governments are tightening control on overt religiosity.

## 6. Western Muslim women — discrimination, legal protections, and World Hijab Day

- **Workplace discrimination persists:** Hijabi women report bias at every employment stage (hiring, accommodation, harassment) across law enforcement, security, retail, and offices. On US college campuses, CAIR-CA reported 49% of Muslim students faced harassment/discrimination in 2024. — UA Little Rock Journal; NEA. https://ualr.edu/socialchange/2025/02/21/veiled-bias-the-complex-reality-of-hijab-in-the-american-workplace/
- **Legal win (Feb 2024):** The 2nd US Circuit Court of Appeals ruled a Muslim corrections officer can sue NY DOCCS under Title VII; forcing her to remove her hijab in front of a male supervisor was an "adverse employment action." — HR Dive. https://www.hrdive.com/news/2nd-circuit-muslim-officer-forced-to-remove-hijab-title-vii/707197/
- **Other religious-accommodation precedents:** United Airlines paid $305,000 (EEOC settlement) and a Southwest worker won a $1M judgment in religious-discrimination cases — reinforcing US protections for religious dress/grooming. — EEOC; The Center Square. https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/united-airlines-pay-305000-settle-eeoc-religious-discrimination-lawsuit
- **World Hijab Day (annual, 1 Feb):** Founded 2013 by Nazma Khan; observed in 150+ countries. 13th edition (1 Feb 2025) theme #HijabisUnsilenced; 14th edition (1 Feb 2026) theme #UnityInHijab — solidarity against prejudice and misconceptions. — World Hijab Day; Islamic Relief UK. https://worldhijabday.com/14th-annual-whd-press-release/ ; https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/world-hijab-day-2026-unityinhijab/

## 7. Modest fashion industry — strong commercial growth

- **Market size & growth:** Modest-clothing market valued ~$12.98B in 2025; projected ~14.12% CAGR (2026–2033), reaching ~$37.34B by 2033. (Broader "modest fashion" figures cited up to $402B globally in 2025 across regions.) Middle East and Europe forecast to drive ~60% of growth through 2030. — Accio; industry trend reports. https://www.accio.com/business/trending-high-quality-hijabs-2025
- **Mainstreaming:** European brands (Mango, H&M) launching hijab-friendly lines; sportswear brands offering performance sport hijabs.
- **2025–2026 trends:** Sustainability (eco-fabrics ~30% of new launches by 2026), jewel tones (emerald, sapphire, ruby, amethyst), chiffon/modal/bamboo blends, tech-enhanced fabrics. — Minnaba; Jinlaifabric. https://minnaba.com/blogs/article/hijab-fashion-trends-2026-colors-fabrics-and-new-styling-ideas

> Note: The commercial boom directly rebuts the "hijab = drab/oppressive uniform" stereotype and the "black abaya is mandatory" misconception — see misconceptions.md.
