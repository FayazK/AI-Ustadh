# Scientific Research — Ant Communication and Colony Intelligence

**Fetched:** 2026-04-01

## Ant Communication Systems

### Pheromone-Based Communication
- Ants communicate via complex chemical signals (pheromones) for colony defense, foraging, and alarm
- **Alarm pheromones:** Information about formic acid and n-undecane processed in specific glomeruli in the antennal lobe (Camponotus obscuripes) — ants have dedicated brain structures for processing warnings
- **Source:** Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience (PMC2912167)

### E.O. Wilson's Foundational Research (1962)
- Demonstrated that pheromone trails provide positive AND negative feedback to organize colony-level foraging
- Multiple pheromones reveal "remarkable sophistication" in ant trail networks
- **Source:** Current Biology, Cell Press

### Colony Intelligence — Collective Problem Solving
- When confronted with complex tasks, ants decompose them into smaller sub-tasks
- Each ant focuses on a particular aspect and conveys information via pheromone trails
- The colony collaborates cohesively toward shared objectives — true distributed intelligence
- **Source:** Swarm Intelligence, Springer Nature

### Alarm Communication Specifics
- Ants can warn colony members of danger using specific chemical signals
- Warning behavior includes physical gestures (antennation, stridulation) combined with chemical signals
- Some species use acoustic communication (stridulation) in addition to chemical signals
- **Source:** Alarm Pheromone Processing study, PMC2912167
