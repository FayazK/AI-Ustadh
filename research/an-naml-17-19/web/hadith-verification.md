# Hadith Verification — Sulaiman and the Ant (An-Naml 27:17-19)

**Fetched:** 2026-04-01

## Key Hadith

### 1. The Prophet and the Ant Colony (Prohibition of Burning Ants)
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 2241a
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:2241a
- **Grade:** Sahih (Authentic)
- **Text:** "An ant had bitten a Prophet (one amongst the earlier Prophets) and he ordered that the colony of the ants should be burnt. And Allah revealed to him: 'Because of an ant's bite you have burnt a community from amongst the communities which sings My glory.'"
- **Chapter:** The Prohibition Of Killing Ants
- **Narrator:** Abu Huraira → Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
- **Relevance:** Establishes ants as communities that glorify Allah — directly connects to why the Quran dignifies the ant's speech in Surah An-Naml

### 2. Solomon's Ambition and the Importance of "In sha Allah"
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3424
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3424
- **Grade:** Sahih (Authentic)
- **Text:** Solomon said "Tonight I will sleep with seventy ladies each of whom will conceive a child who will be a knight fighting for Allah's Cause." His companion said "If Allah will" but Solomon did not say so; therefore none of those women got pregnant except one who gave birth to a half child.
- **Narrator:** Abu Huraira → Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
- **Relevance:** Shows Solomon's human moments — his ambition could overstep, making his dua in 27:19 for restraint and gratitude deeply personal

### 3. Prohibition of Killing Four Creatures
- **Source:** Sunan Abu Dawud 5267, Sahih Muslim
- **Link:** https://sunnah.com/abudawud:5267
- **Grade:** Sahih
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ forbade the killing of four creatures: ants, bees, hoopoes, and shrikes.
- **Relevance:** Ants and hoopoes — both appear in Surah An-Naml as speaking creatures in Sulaiman's court

### 4. Solomon and Rain Prayer — Ant's Supplication
- **Source:** Referenced in various collections, narration strength debated
- **Text:** Solomon went out to pray for rain and saw an ant lying on its back raising its legs to the sky. He told his companions "Go back, for you have been given water through the supplication of others."
- **Relevance:** Shows Solomon recognizing the ant's worship and supplication — consistent with his ability to understand their speech in 27:18-19

## Tafseer Sources Verified

### Tafheem ul-Quran (Maududi) on 27:17-19
- **Source:** islamicstudies.info
- **Link:** https://www.islamicstudies.info/tafheem.php?sura=27&verse=15&to=19
- **Key linguistic points:**
  - Using "qaalat namlatun" (an ant said) for a human tribal member would be "altogether against Arabic usage" — no Arab would say "a dog said" for a member of Banu Kalb
  - The "al" article before jinn, ins, and tayr indicates three separate categories, not metaphorical groupings
  - "Awzi'ni" (أوزعني) root meaning is "to restrain/keep under control" — Solomon prays for restraint over his powers
  - The prayer for inclusion among "righteous servants" shows that righteous conduct alone doesn't guarantee Paradise — only Allah's mercy
