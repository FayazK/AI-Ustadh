### Asbab al-nuzul note for `recite`
No single sahih sabab al-nuzul is established for al-Fatiha. The majority view (Ibn Kathir, Tabari) is that it is Makki and among the very early revelations. A minority (Mujahid via Tabari) held it Madani. The "two lights" hadith (Muslim 806, in hadith-verification.md) is sometimes cited as a virtue-narration rather than a sabab. Skills should report this honestly rather than fabricating a sabab.
