# Hadith Verification — Salah (Namaz)

**Fetched:** 2026-05-30
**Sources:** sunnah.com, islamqa.info, islamweb.net (incl. fiqh.islamonline.net), dorar.net, abuaminaelias.com (Daily Hadith Online), seekersguidance.org, hadithanswers.com

> **Authenticity discipline applied:** Every entry below carries collection + number, a sunnah.com link where the text lives there, and a grade. Where a grade is disputed among scholars, the disagreement is named. Nothing here is fabricated; for the weak/fabricated narrations in Group B the scholarly ruling that condemns them is quoted with its citation.

## Group A — Authentic Foundational Hadith

### 1. Hadith of Jibril (Islam, Iman, Ihsan)
- **Arabic name / gist:** Jibril (Gabriel) comes in human form and asks the Prophet ﷺ about Islam, Iman, Ihsan, and the Hour. Islam is defined by the five pillars, with **salah** named as the second pillar after the shahadah.
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 8 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:8 (also Sahih al-Bukhari 50 — https://sunnah.com/bukhari:50). Narrated by Umar ibn al-Khattab (via Yahya ibn Ya'mur).
- **Grade:** **Sahih** (agreed upon — Bukhari & Muslim).
- **Meaning / relevance:** The "Mother of the Sunnah." Establishes salah as a constitutive pillar of Islam and frames Ihsan ("worship Allah as if you see Him; if you do not see Him, He sees you") — the inner spirit of prayer.

### 2. "بُنِيَ الإسلامُ على خمسٍ" — Islam is built on five pillars
- **Arabic gist:** "Islam is built upon five: testifying lā ilāha illā Allāh and that Muhammad is His Messenger, **establishing the salah**, paying zakah, Hajj, and fasting Ramadan."
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 8 — https://sunnah.com/bukhari:8 ; Sahih Muslim 16 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:16 (also Nawawi 40 #3 — https://sunnah.com/nawawi40:3). Narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar.
- **Grade:** **Sahih** (agreed upon).
- **Meaning / relevance:** Salah is one of the five structural pillars; the building stands on it.

### 3. "صَلُّوا كما رأيتُموني أصلِّي" — Pray as you have seen me pray
- **Arabic:** صَلُّوا كَمَا رَأَيْتُمُونِي أُصَلِّي
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 631 — https://sunnah.com/bukhari:631 . Narrated by Malik ibn al-Huwayrith.
- **Grade:** **Sahih.**
- **Meaning / relevance:** The textual basis that the *form* of prayer is taken from the Prophet's practice, not invented — foundational for fiqh of salah.

### 4. The first deed judged on the Day of Judgment is the salah
- **Arabic:** إِنَّ أَوَّلَ مَا يُحَاسَبُ بِهِ العَبْدُ يَوْمَ القِيَامَةِ مِنْ عَمَلِهِ صَلَاتُهُ … "If it is sound he has succeeded; if it is defective he has failed," with voluntary prayers making up deficiencies.
- **Source:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 413 — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:413 (also Sunan an-Nasa'i 465, Sunan Abi Dawud 864/866). Narrated by Abu Hurayrah.
- **Grade:** **Hasan / Sahih.** Tirmidhi himself graded it *hasan*; Darussalam and al-Albani graded it **sahih** (it has corroborating chains and is widely accepted as authentic).
- **Meaning / relevance:** The first matter of accountability — the centerpiece of the "weight of salah" theme. (Note: this is the authentic narration that the fabricated "rewards/punishments pamphlets" try to imitate — see Group B #3.)

### 5. "العهدُ الذي بيننا وبينهم الصلاةُ فمن تركها فقد كفر" — The covenant is the salah
- **Arabic:** العَهْدُ الَّذِي بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَهُمُ الصَّلَاةُ فَمَنْ تَرَكَهَا فَقَدْ كَفَرَ — "The covenant between us and them is the prayer; whoever abandons it has committed disbelief."
- **Source:** Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2621 — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2621 (also Nasa'i 463, Ibn Majah 1079, Ahmad). Narrated by Buraydah ibn al-Husayb.
- **Grade:** **Sahih.** Graded *hasan sahih* by Tirmidhi; authenticated by al-Albani; Daily Hadith Online lists it as **sahih** (https://www.abuaminaelias.com/dailyhadithonline/2021/12/16/abandons-salah/).
- **Meaning / relevance:** The central authentic text in the kufr-of-abandoning-salah debate (see Group C). This is a *real, sourced* text — not to be confused with the fabricated "15 punishments" list.

### 6. "بين الرجلِ وبين الشركِ والكفرِ تركُ الصلاة" — Between a man and shirk/kufr is leaving prayer
- **Arabic:** إِنَّ بَيْنَ الرَّجُلِ وَبَيْنَ الشِّرْكِ وَالْكُفْرِ تَرْكَ الصَّلَاةِ
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 82 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:82 . Narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah (via Abu Sufyan ← al-A'mash ← Jarir).
- **Grade:** **Sahih.**
- **Meaning / relevance:** The strongest (Muslim-grade) text in the kufr debate; companions to Group A #5.

### 7. The five prayers as a river one bathes in five times a day
- **Arabic gist:** أَرَأَيْتُمْ لَوْ أَنَّ نَهَرًا بِبَابِ أَحَدِكُمْ … "If there were a river at one of your doors and he bathed in it five times a day, would any dirt remain? … That is the likeness of the five prayers — Allah wipes out sins by them."
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 528 — https://sunnah.com/bukhari:528 ; Sahih Muslim 667. Narrated by Abu Hurayrah.
- **Grade:** **Sahih** (agreed upon).
- **Meaning / relevance:** Prayer as continuous purification — the expiation-of-sins dimension.

### 8. Witr — its virtue and ruling (cluster, with the disagreement noted)
The *obligation/recommendation* of witr is established; the question is the **grade of specific wordings**:
- **(a) "أمدَّكم بصلاةٍ … خيرٌ لكم من حُمر النَّعَم: الوتر"** ("Allah has added a prayer better for you than red camels — the witr"). Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1418 — https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1418 (also Tirmidhi 452, Ibn Majah 1168). Narrated by Kharijah ibn Hudhafah. **Grade: Da'if (weak)** — graded *da'if* by al-Albani on the sunnah.com page (chain weakness). So this *specific famous wording* is weak.
- **(b) "أوتروا يا أهل القرآن"** ("O people of the Qur'an, perform witr, for Allah is Witr and loves the witr"). Source: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 453 — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:453 (also Abu Dawud 1416, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah, Ahmad). Narrated by Ali ibn Abi Talib. **Grade: Disputed** — Tirmidhi himself: *hasan*; Darussalam/al-Albani: *da'if*; Ibn Khuzaymah authenticated it. **Note:** A closely related authentic core — "Allah is Witr (One) and loves the witr, so perform witr" — appears at Sunan Abi Dawud 1416 (https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1416), graded **sahih** by al-Albani.
- **(c) "الوترُ حقٌّ فمن لم يوتر فليس منا"** ("Witr is a duty; whoever does not perform it is not of us," said thrice). Source: Sunan Abi Dawud 1419 — https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1419 . Narrated by Buraydah. **Grade: Da'if** — al-Albani placed it in *Da'if Abi Dawud*; Ibn Hajar noted ʿUbaydullah al-ʿAtaki in the chain (whom al-Bukhari weakened). Some (e.g. al-Hakim) strengthened it and read "ḥaqq" as "an emphasized sunnah," not literal obligation.
- **Ruling / ikhtilaf:** Abu Hanifah held witr **wajib** (obligatory, one rank below fard); the majority (Malik, al-Shafi'i, Ahmad, and Abu Hanifah's own companions) held it **sunnah mu'akkadah** (strongly emphasized sunnah). See islamweb fatwa 469702 (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/469702/ruling-on-witr-prayer) and Daily Hadith Online (https://www.abuaminaelias.com/dailyhadithonline/2019/04/26/salat-al-witr-ahl-quran/).
- **Bottom line:** Witr is firmly established as worship; several of its *most-quoted single wordings* are weak/disputed, while authentic witr texts exist (e.g. Bukhari/Muslim narrations of the Prophet's regular witr). When quoting witr in the episode, prefer the established ruling over the weak "red camels" wording, and flag the grading.

### 9. The Night Journey (Isra/Mi'raj) — 50 prayers reduced to 5
- **Arabic gist:** Fifty prayers were enjoined; on Musa's repeated advice the Prophet ﷺ requested reduction until they became five — "they are five, and they are fifty (in reward); My word does not change."
- **Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3887 — https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3887 (Malik ibn Sa'sa'a ← Anas); also Sahih al-Bukhari 349 (Abu Dharr) and Sahih Muslim 162/163. 
- **Grade:** **Sahih** (agreed upon).
- **Meaning / relevance:** Origin of the five daily prayers and the "fivefold count, fiftyfold reward" point — a key foundation segment.

### 10. The two rak'ah sunnah of Fajr better than the world
- **Arabic:** رَكْعَتَا الفَجْرِ خَيْرٌ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا وَمَا فِيهَا
- **Source:** Sahih Muslim 725 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:725 . Narrated by Aisha.
- **Grade:** **Sahih.**
- **Meaning / relevance:** Virtue of the sunan rawatib (regular voluntary prayers); the Fajr sunnah specifically.

### 11. Sunan rawatib: 4 before Dhuhr, and the 12 rak'ah → house in Paradise
- **(a) "من حافظ على أربعٍ قبل الظهر وأربعٍ بعدها حرَّمه الله على النار"** ("Whoever maintains four before Dhuhr and four after, Allah forbids him to the Fire"). Source: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 427 — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:427 (also Abu Dawud 1269, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah). Narrated by Umm Habibah. **Grade:** Tirmidhi: *hasan gharib*; Darussalam/al-Albani: **sahih**.
- **(b) Umm Habibah — 12 rak'ah → a house in Paradise:** "Whoever prays twelve rak'ah in a day and night, a house will be built for him in Paradise." Source: Sahih Muslim 728 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:728 . **Grade: Sahih.** (The twelve = 2 before Fajr, 4 before + 2 after Dhuhr, 2 after Maghrib, 2 after Isha, per the Tirmidhi/Nasa'i breakdown.)
- **Meaning / relevance:** The regular sunnah prayers and their reward.

### 12. The times of the five prayers
- **(a) Jibril leading the Prophet at the start and end of each time:** Over two days Jibril prayed each prayer at its earliest, then at its latest, saying "the time is what is between these two." Source: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 149 — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:149 (Ibn Abbas); also Abu Dawud 393. **Grade: Hasan.**
- **(b) Abdullah ibn Amr — the time-windows of the five prayers** (Dhuhr until Asr; Asr until the sun yellows; Maghrib until the twilight/shafaq fades; Isha until midnight; Fajr until sunrise). Source: Sahih Muslim 612 — https://sunnah.com/muslim:612 . **Grade: Sahih.**
- **Meaning / relevance:** Scriptural basis for the prescribed prayer-time windows.

## Group B — Weak / Fabricated Narrations (Myth-Busting)

### 1. The "15 punishments for abandoning salah" list — **FABRICATED (موضوع)**
- **Popular wording (as circulated):** A list of fifteen punishments for the one negligent in prayer — **6 in this world** (e.g. blessing removed from his life, mark of the righteous wiped from his face, no reward for deeds, dua not raised to heaven, no share in the dua of the righteous, dies humiliated), **3 at death** (dies humiliated / hungry / thirsty), **3 in the grave** (grave constricts, fire, a serpent named "al-Shujā' al-Aqra'"), and **3 on the Day of Judgment** (severe reckoning, Allah's anger, entry into Hell). Often peddled as "narrated from Ibn Abbas" or "from Ali" or vaguely "in a hadith."
- **Grade / Status:** **Fabricated (موضوع / mawḍūʿ).** Not a hadith of the Prophet ﷺ at all.
- **Scholarly verdict + citation:**
  - **Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani** (*Lisan al-Mizan*): "This hadith is clearly false (bāṭil), and it is fabricated among the Sufi groups." Its forgery is traced to **Muhammad ibn Ali ibn al-Abbas al-Baghdadi** (per al-Dhahabi, *Mizan al-Iʿtidal*). — islamweb fatwa 83984: https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/83984/hadith-about-15-punishments-for-leaving-prayers
  - **Shaykh Ibn Baz:** "This is one of the fabricated hadiths falsely attributed to the Prophet," citing al-Dhahabi and Ibn Hajar. **The Standing Committee** (Fatwa no. 8689, *Fatawa al-Lajnah* 4/468) formally declared it false. **Ibn Uthaymeen:** "This hadith is mawḍūʿ (fabricated)… it is not permissible to propagate it unless accompanied by a statement that it is fabricated." — IslamQA 20897: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/20897 ; also fiqh.islamonline.net: https://fiqh.islamonline.net/en/are-there-15-punishments-for-one-who-neglects-prayer/
- **The AUTHENTIC position instead:** The danger of abandoning prayer is real and needs no forgery — Qur'an 74:42–43 ("What put you in Hell? They will say: We were not of those who prayed"), Qur'an 19:59, and the **authentic** texts in Group A #5 (Tirmidhi 2621) and #6 (Muslim 82). Scholars insist: do not circulate the fabricated list; cite the sound texts.

### 2. "الصلاةُ عمادُ الدين" — "Prayer is the pillar of religion"
- **Popular wording:** الصلاةُ عمادُ الدين، فمن أقامها فقد أقام الدين، ومن هدمها فقد هدم الدين — "Prayer is the pillar of the religion; whoever establishes it establishes the religion, and whoever destroys it destroys the religion."
- **Grade / Status:** **Da'if (weak) as a marfu' (Prophetic) statement** — and the *full* compound wording above is not established as a single authentic hadith. The first clause circulates with weak/broken chains.
- **Scholarly verdict + citation:** Al-Bayhaqi (*Shu'ab al-Iman*) records it via ʿIkrimah ← Umar with a **weak** chain (ʿIkrimah did not hear from Umar). **Ibn al-Salah** said it is "not well known (lā yuʿraf)"; **al-Nawawi** (in *al-Tanqih*) called it "munkar bāṭil" (rejected/false) as a marfu'. Abu Nuʿaym narrates a **mursal** (disconnected) version with reliable narrators. — hadithanswers.com: https://hadithanswers.com/salah-is-the-pillar-of-din/ ; islamweb (Arabic) fatwa 397345: https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/397345
- **The AUTHENTIC position instead:** The **meaning** is sound and is supported by an authentic hadith with different wording — **Tirmidhi (via Mu'adh ibn Jabal): "the head of the matter is Islam, its pillar (ʿamūd) is the salah, and its peak is jihad"** (graded *hasan sahih* by Tirmidhi). So: quote the Mu'adh hadith for the "pillar" idea, and flag the popular "ʿimād al-dīn" wording as weak-as-marfu'.

### 3. Fabricated "rewards/punishments for each prayer" pamphlet narrations
- **Popular wording:** Pamphlets/forwards listing a fixed reward or punishment "for each of the five prayers" (e.g. "whoever guards Fajr gets X, whoever misses Asr loses Y…") presented as one hadith, and misquotes of the "first thing reckoned" hadith embellished with invented specifics.
- **Grade / Status:** The **specific tabulated lists are unverifiable / baseless** as single Prophetic hadith; they are typically stitched together from disparate, often weak material.
- **Scholarly verdict + citation:** Same principle as #1 — scholars (Ibn Baz, Ibn Uthaymeen) warn against circulating unverified prayer-virtue lists; what is authentic is the **"first deed reckoned is the salah"** hadith (Tirmidhi 413, Group A #4, *hasan/sahih*) and the **river-of-purification** hadith (Bukhari 528, Group A #7, *sahih*). Use those instead of pamphlet tables. (IslamQA / islamweb consistently redirect to the authentic texts: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/20897)

### 4. "Missing one prayer = 80 huqub / a fixed huge span in Hell"
- **Popular wording:** "Whoever misses a single prayer (even if he makes it up) will be in Hell for **one ḥuqb** = eighty years, each day of which equals 1,000 worldly years" — and a separate misframe: "missing one of the five = 80 years in Hell."
- **Grade / Status:** **Weak (ḍaʿīf)** — *not* established as authentic, but **not graded "fabricated" by the scholars who treated it** (it appears in some tafsir/hadith books). The "80 years for missing *one of the five daily prayers*" framing in particular has **no authentic basis**.
- **Scholarly verdict + citation:** **SeekersGuidance (Shaykh Faraz Khan):** "This particular hadith is weak, despite being found in some well-known books of tafsir and hadith." It may be used only for *targhib/tarhib* (encouragement/warning) since it does not contradict established principles — but must not be presented as definitively authentic. — https://seekersguidance.org/answers/general-counsel/hadith-of-staying-in-hell-for-one-haqb-for-missing-a-single-prayer-intentionally/ . On the broader claim, scholars note there is **no sound text** fixing "eighty years of Hell" as the penalty for one missed prayer.
- **The AUTHENTIC position instead:** Deliberately neglecting prayer is a grave sin and (per the kufr debate, Group C) potentially disbelief — but the **specific numeric punishments are not authentically fixed**. Any sin including missed prayers is forgivable through sincere repentance and making them up.

### 5. Salat al-Tasbih — **DISPUTED, not simply fabricated**
- **Popular wording:** A four-rak'ah prayer in which one says the tasbih (subhanallah, alhamdulillah, lā ilāha illā Allah, Allahu akbar) 300 times total in a prescribed distribution, narrated to the Prophet's uncle al-Abbas with great reward.
- **Grade / Status:** **Disputed** — graders range from sahih/hasan to weak to fabricated. *This is a genuine ikhtilaf, NOT a clear-cut fabrication.*
- **Scholarly verdict + citation:** Source hadith at Sunan Abi Dawud 1297 (https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1297) and Ibn Majah 1387. **Ibn Hajar** accepted it as **sahih or hasan** (not weak), noting different chains raise it; **al-Albani** authenticated it. On the other side, **Ibn al-Jawzi** listed it among fabrications, and **Ibn al-Arabi** said "there is no sound or hasan hadith on it"; **Ibn Taymiyyah** rejected the text. — aboutislam.net summary: https://aboutislam.net/counseling/ask-the-scholar/prayer/is-offering-salat-al-tasbih-recommended/ ; islamweb fatwa 83877: https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/83877/the-tasbeeh-prayer
- **Honest framing for the episode:** Present it as a *scholarly disagreement* — some major hadith masters authenticated it, others rejected it — not as a settled forgery.

## Group C — Kufr of Abandoning Salah (Authentic Texts, Real Ikhtilaf)

This is a **genuine, sourced scholarly disagreement** — the episode must clearly separate these *authentic* texts from the *fabricated* "15 punishments" list (Group B #1).

**The authentic texts in play:**
- **Muslim 82** (Jabir): "Between a man and shirk and kufr is abandoning the prayer." — https://sunnah.com/muslim:82 — **Sahih.**
- **Tirmidhi 2621** (Buraydah): "The covenant between us and them is the salah; whoever abandons it has disbelieved." — https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2621 — **Sahih** (hasan sahih; al-Albani: sahih).
- **Qur'an 9:11** (brotherhood conditioned on establishing prayer) and **74:42–43**, **19:59** (the negligent of prayer threatened with Hell).
- **Reported consensus of the Companions** — Abdullah ibn Shaqiq: "The Companions of the Prophet ﷺ did not consider the abandonment of any deed to be disbelief except the prayer." (Tirmidhi)

**The disagreement (real, not a myth):**
- **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (and a body of the Salaf — Ishaq ibn Rahawayh, Ibn al-Qayyim, Ibn Kathir, and per several reports the majority of the Companions): one who **deliberately** abandons prayer out of laziness commits **major kufr** that takes him outside Islam. Ibn Uthaymeen and the IslamQA position lean to this, reading the texts literally.
- **The majority — Abu Hanifah, Malik, al-Shafi'i (the jumhur):** he is a **grave sinner (fāsiq), not a disbeliever**, so long as he does not deny prayer's obligation; the "kufr" in the texts is read as severe rebuke / kufr dūna kufr (a lesser kufr) or as applying to one who rejects its obligation.
- **Sources:** IslamQA 5208 — https://islamqa.info/en/answers/5208 ; Daily Hadith Online "Between faith and disbelief is leaving prayer" — https://www.abuaminaelias.com/dailyhadithonline/2012/03/27/iman-kufr-leaving-salat/ ; Ibn Uthaymeen, *A Treatise on the Ruling Regarding the Abandoner of Prayer*.

**Key distinction for the myth-busting segment:** The *threat* against abandoning salah is grounded in **sahih hadith and the Qur'an**, and scholars genuinely differ on its exact ruling. The *fabricated "15 punishments" list* is a forgery layered on top of that real threat — and using the forgery actually *weakens* the case that the authentic texts already make powerfully.
