## Section 1 — The categories of prayer units (Hanafi terminology)

The Hanafi school recognizes a **five-tier** classification (fard / wajib / sunnah muʾakkadah / sunnah ghayr muʾakkadah / nafl). The other three schools collapse this into roughly three tiers (fard / sunnah-muʾakkadah / nafl) — **they do not carve out a separate "wajib" category between fard and sunnah.** This single structural difference is the root of most of the ikhtilaf in this document.

### فرض (Fard) — obligatory, by definitive (qaṭʿī) evidence
- Established by absolute, decisive proof (Qur'an + mutawatir Sunnah + ijmaʿ).
- **Denial (juhūd) of a fard = kufr.** Deliberate omission = a major sin requiring repentance and qadāʾ (making it up).
- A prayer is invalid if a fard *act within it* (an arkān/rukn) is omitted — it cannot be fixed by sajdah al-sahw; the prayer must be repeated.
- **Farḍ ʿayn** (individually obligatory on every accountable Muslim — e.g., the five daily prayers) vs **farḍ kifāyah** (communal — discharged on behalf of all if some perform it; e.g., Salat al-Janazah / the funeral prayer). [All four]
- Source: [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham — Faraidh, Sunnah, Wajib of Salah](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171193/what-are-the-faraaidh-and-sunnah-and-wajib-of-salah/)

### واجب (Wajib) — binding, by probable (ẓannī) evidence **[Hanafi-specific category]**
- A distinct Hanafi rank **just below fard**: established by strong but not decisive proof.
- **Denial of a wajib is NOT kufr** (because the evidence is ẓannī), but deliberate omission **is sinful**; forgetful omission within a prayer is rectified by **sajdah al-sahw** (prostration of forgetfulness).
- Examples of independent wajib prayers: **Witr**, the **two Eid prayers**, and **Sajdah al-Tilawah** (the prostration of recitation).
- **The other three madhahib do not distinguish wajib from fard or from sunnah muʾakkadah** — for them an act is either fard, sunnah, or nafl. So what a Hanafi calls "wajib," the Majority will classify either as fard or as a confirmed sunnah depending on the act.
- Source: [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171193/what-are-the-faraaidh-and-sunnah-and-wajib-of-salah/) · [islamweb — supererogatory prayers per Hanafi madhhab](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/108251/the-supererogatory-prayers-according-to-the-hanafi-mathhab)

### سنّتِ مؤکدہ (Sunnah Muʾakkadah) — stressed / confirmed sunnah
- Acts the Prophet ﷺ **regularly and consistently performed** and rarely left. The confirmed rawatib (the 12 rakʿah, see Section 5) fall here.
- Omitting them occasionally is not sinful; **persistently abandoning** them is blameworthy and (per Hanafi jurists) deserving of reproach, but does **not** require qadāʾ and does not invalidate the obligatory prayer.
- Source: [SeekersGuidance (Hanafi) — Confirmed and Non-Confirmed Sunna Prayers](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/what-are-the-confirmed-sunna-and-non-confirmed-sunna-prayers-associated-with-the-obligatory-prayers/)

### سنّتِ غیر مؤکدہ (Sunnah Ghayr Muʾakkadah / mustaḥabb / nafl) — non-stressed
- Acts the Prophet ﷺ did **sometimes but not consistently**. Doing them is rewarded; leaving them carries no blame at all. Often termed *mandūb* / *mustaḥabb*.
- Source: [SeekersGuidance (Hanafi)](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/what-are-the-confirmed-sunna-and-non-confirmed-sunna-prayers-associated-with-the-obligatory-prayers/)

### نفل (Nafl) — voluntary / supererogatory
- Purely optional extra prayer (e.g., Tahajjud, Duha, Tahiyyat al-Masjid). Great reward in performing; **no sin whatsoever** in omitting. May be prayed any time except the makrūh windows (Section 3).
- Source: [islamweb — supererogatory prayers per Hanafi madhhab](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/108251/the-supererogatory-prayers-according-to-the-hanafi-mathhab)

### وتر (Witr) — special status, the headline ikhtilaf
- **[Hanafi] Witr is WAJIB** — binding every night between Isha and Fajr. This is the position of **Imam Abu Hanifa** (his two companions, Abu Yusuf and Muhammad, held it to be sunnah muʾakkadah, but the relied-upon Hanafi madhhab follows Abu Hanifa: wajib). Deliberately leaving it is sinful, and if missed it must be made up (qadāʾ). Importantly it is **wajib, not fard** — its evidence is ẓannī, so denial of it is not kufr.
  - Basis: the Prophet ﷺ said *"Al-Witr is a ḥaqq (a due/right), so whoever does not pray Witr is not of us"* (narrated thrice), and *"Allah has added a prayer for you, so observe it between Isha and Fajr — the Witr."* The imperative wording is read by Abu Hanifa as establishing bindingness.
- **[Majority — Shafiʿi / Maliki / Hanbali] Witr is Sunnah Muʾakkadah** — strongly emphasized, but not obligatory.
  - Basis: the hadith of the Bedouin who asked what was obligatory upon him; the Prophet ﷺ named *"five prayers in the day and night,"* and when asked if anything else was due, said *"No, unless you volunteer."* The Majority read this as excluding Witr from obligation.
- Source: [islamweb — Ruling on Witr Prayer](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/469702/ruling-on-witr-prayer) · [SeekersGuidance — Hadiths on Witr (Hanafi)](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/prayer/the-hadiths-on-the-witr-prayer-according-to-the-hanafi-legal-school/)

### Correction / clarification to the draft — Jumuʿah
The draft listed Jumuʿah as *"4 sunnah before + 2 fard + 4 sunnah after + 2 sunnah + (2 nafl)."* The verified Hanafi sequence is:
> **4 rakʿah sunnah before** (after the first adhan) → **2 rakʿah fard** (in congregation, after the khutbah) → **4 rakʿah sunnah after** → **2 rakʿah sunnah** → **(2 nafl, optional)**, for a commonly cited **14 rakʿah** total.
So the draft is essentially correct; the only refinement is that the "2 sunnah" after the 4-sunnah is itself sunnah muʾakkadah (and the final 2 are nafl). The exact wording differs slightly across Hanafi muftis, but the agreed core is **4 sunnah → 2 fard → 4 sunnah → 2 sunnah**.
Source: [islamweb — Sunnah Prayer Before and After Jumuʿah (Hanafi)](https://islamweb.net/en/fatwa/86758/sunnah-prayer-before-and-after-jumuah-according-to-hanafi-school) · [IslamQA / Qibla — How many rakat is Salat al-Jumuʿah](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/qibla-hanafi/34764/how-many-rakat-is-salat-ul-jumah/)

### Fard counts are agreed by all four schools
The **fard rakʿah counts — 2 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4** (Fajr / Zuhr / Asr / Maghrib / Isha) — are **agreed upon by all four madhahib [All four]**. The schools differ **only** in:
1. the **framing of the sunnah/nafl** around the fard (how many are confirmed vs optional, before vs after), and
2. the **status of Witr** (wajib for Hanafi vs sunnah muʾakkadah for the Majority — §1).

### Traveler's qaṣr (shortening)
A traveler (musāfir) shortens the **4-rakʿah fard prayers (Zuhr, Asr, Isha) to 2 rakʿah** each. Fajr (2) and Maghrib (3) are **not** shortened. **[Hanafi]** holds qaṣr is **wajib** for the traveler (binding, not merely permitted); the **[Majority]** generally regards it as a sunnah/rukhṣah (recommended concession). Witr is still prayed; the confirmed sunnah rawatib may be dropped while traveling (except the 2 sunnah of Fajr, which are kept).
Source: [IslamQA / Askimam — rakats and travel](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/75737/what-are-the-number-of-rakats-for-each-salat-what-are-the-times-of-the-times-of-each-salat-what-is-to-recited-in-each-salat-2/)

## Section 3 — The five prayer timings (awqāt) with evidence

### Qur'anic basis (prayer has fixed times)
- **4:103** — *"Indeed, prayer is upon the believers a decree of specified/fixed times" (kitāban mawqūtā).* The foundational proof that salah is time-bound.
- **17:78** — *"Establish prayer at the decline of the sun (dulūk al-shams) until the darkness of the night (ghasaq al-layl), and [recite] the Qur'an at dawn (qur'ān al-fajr)."* Classical tafsir (Maʿariful Qur'an) reads *dulūk al-shams* (the sun passing the zenith) as covering **Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib**; *ghasaq al-layl* (deep night) as **Isha**; and *qur'ān al-fajr* as **Fajr** — i.e. all five times in one verse. Source: [Tafsir Maʿariful Qur'an on 17:78 (Quran.com)](https://quran.com/en/17:78/tafsirs/en-tafsir-maarif-ul-quran)
- **11:114** — *"Establish prayer at the two ends of the day (ṭarafay al-nahār) and in the early hours of the night."* Read as Fajr + (Zuhr/Asr) + (Maghrib/Isha).
- **2:238** — *"Guard strictly the prayers, especially the middle prayer (al-ṣalāt al-wusṭā)."* The **predominant scholarly view identifies al-ṣalāt al-wusṭā with ʿAsr**, supported by the Prophet's ﷺ statement at the Battle of the Trench cursing those who kept him from "the middle prayer, the ʿAsr prayer." (Other minority views named Fajr or Zuhr — noted as disputed.)
- **20:130** — *"Glorify... before the rising of the sun and before its setting, and during the hours of the night and at the ends of the day."*
- **50:39–40** — *"Glorify... before the rising of the sun and before its setting, and during the night glorify Him, and after prostration."*

### The Hadith of Jibril (two-day demonstration) — the structural proof of the windows
Jibril ʿalayhi al-salām led the Prophet ﷺ on two consecutive days, praying each prayer at the **beginning** of its time on day one and at the **end** of its time on day two, then said: *"Between these two times is the time [of prayer]."*
- Day 1 (early edge): Zuhr when the shadow ≈ a sandal-strap (just past zenith); Asr when each thing's shadow = its length; Maghrib at sunset; Isha when twilight disappeared; Fajr at the break of dawn.
- Day 2 (late edge): Zuhr when shadow = object's length; Asr when shadow ≈ twice the object's length; Maghrib still at sunset; Isha at the end of the first third of the night; Fajr when the sky brightened.
- Grading: **Hasan** (Tirmidhi). Source: [Jami at-Tirmidhi 149 (Sunnah.com)](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:149)

### Sahih Muslim — the window definitions (hadith of Abdullah ibn Amr)
The Prophet ﷺ defined each window's start-to-end: *Zuhr* from the sun's decline until a thing's shadow equals its length (then Asr begins); *Asr* until the sun yellows; *Maghrib* until the twilight (shafaq) disappears; *Isha* until midnight (half the night); *Fajr* from the break of dawn until just before sunrise.
Source: [Sahih Muslim 612a (Sunnah.com)](https://sunnah.com/muslim:612a)

### The five windows (Hanafi reckoning)
| Prayer | Start | End | Hanafi note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fajr** | True dawn (al-fajr al-ṣādiq, white light spreading across the horizon) | Just before sunrise | Hanafis recommend **delaying (isfār)** Fajr until it is light, before sunrise. |
| **Zuhr** | Sun passes the zenith (zawāl) | When an object's shadow = **twice** its length (plus the noon shadow) | **[Hanafi]** uses the *double-shadow* end-point — distinctively later than the Majority. |
| **Asr** | When Zuhr ends (double-shadow per Hanafi) | Sunset | **[Majority]** Asr begins at single-shadow; **[Hanafi]** at double-shadow — the main timing ikhtilaf. |
| **Maghrib** | Immediately after sunset | When the twilight (shafaq) disappears | Hanafis use the *white* twilight (shafaq abyaḍ) as the end-marker (Abu Hanifa's view). |
| **Isha** | When Maghrib's time ends (shafaq gone) | Until true dawn (Fajr); preferred before midnight | Preferred (Hanafi) to delay Isha to the first third of the night. |
Source: [IslamQA / Askimam — times of each salah](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/75737/what-are-the-number-of-rakats-for-each-salat-what-are-the-times-of-the-times-of-each-salat-what-is-to-recited-in-each-salat-2/)

### Makruh / forbidden times (Hanafi)
No prayer (and notably no nafl or qadāʾ) is to be performed at three solar moments:
1. **At sunrise** — from the sun beginning to rise until it has fully risen above the horizon (≈ a spear's length, ~15–20 min).
2. **At the zenith / midday (istiwāʾ)** — when the sun is at its highest point, until it begins to decline.
3. **At sunset** — from the sun beginning to set (turning pale, lookable-at) until it has fully set.
Basis: hadith of **ʿUqba ibn ʿĀmir al-Juhanī**: *"There are three times at which the Messenger of Allah ﷺ forbade us to pray or to bury our dead: when the sun begins to rise until it is fully up; when the sun is at its zenith until it passes; and when the sun starts to set until it has fully set."* — **Sahih Muslim 831**.
**[Hanafi]** also dislikes praying nafl after Fajr-fard until sunrise, and after Asr-fard until sunset; missed obligatory prayers must NOT be made up in the three solar windows above.
Source: [Sahih Muslim 831 / hadeethenc summary](https://hadeethenc.com/en/browse/hadith/10604) · [SeekersGuidance (Hanafi) — Prohibited times for prayer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/prohibited-times-for-prayers-and-makeup-prayers%E2%80%8F/) · [IslamQA.info — Forbidden prayer times](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/48998)

## Section 4 — The method / arkān of salah ("صلوا كما رأيتموني أصلي")

Grounding hadith: the Prophet ﷺ said **"Pray as you have seen me pray" (ṣallū kamā ra'aytumūnī uṣallī)** — Sahih al-Bukhari. The Hanafi school distinguishes **shurūṭ** (pre-conditions, before the prayer) from **arkān/farāʾiḍ** (pillars, within the prayer) from **wājibāt** (binding acts whose forgetful omission is fixed by sajdah al-sahw) from **sunan** (recommended acts).

### Shurūṭ (conditions — must be met *before* the prayer) **[All four, with detail variations]**
1. **Ṭahārah** — ritual purity of body, clothing, and place (wuḍūʾ / ghusl as required).
2. **Satr al-ʿawrah** — covering the ʿawrah (for men: navel to knee; for women: all but face, hands, and — Hanafi — feet).
3. **Istiqbāl al-qiblah** — facing the Kaʿbah.
4. **Waqt** — the prayer's time has entered.
5. **Niyyah** — intention.
Source: [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham — Faraidh, Sunnah, Wajib of Salah](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171193/what-are-the-faraaidh-and-sunnah-and-wajib-of-salah/)

### Arkān / Farāʾiḍ (the pillars — within the prayer; omission of any invalidates the salah) **[Hanafi: 6 core]**
1. **Takbīr al-taḥrīmah** — the opening "Allahu Akbar" (Hanafi: technically a condition of entry, treated among the farāʾiḍ).
2. **Qiyām** — standing (for those able), in the obligatory rakʿah.
3. **Qirāʾah** — reciting *some* of the Qur'an (at least one verse) — see the Fatihah note below.
4. **Rukūʿ** — bowing.
5. **Sujūd** — prostrating (on the seven limbs).
6. **Qaʿdah akhīrah** — the final sitting for the length of the tashahhud.
Omitting an arkān ⇒ **prayer invalid, must be repeated** (sajdah al-sahw does NOT fix it).
Source: [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171193/what-are-the-faraaidh-and-sunnah-and-wajib-of-salah/)

### Key wājibāt (binding acts; forgetful omission ⇒ **sajdah al-sahw**) **[Hanafi]**
- **Reciting Surah al-Fātiḥah** in the obligatory rakʿah — see ikhtilaf below.
- Adding a sūrah/verses **after** al-Fātiḥah in the first two rakʿahs.
- Reciting the **tashahhud** in both sittings; maintaining **tartīb** (sequence) and **taʿdīl al-arkān** (tranquility/stillness in each posture); concluding with the two **salāms**.
Source: [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171193/what-are-the-faraaidh-and-sunnah-and-wajib-of-salah/)

### Hanafi-specific points and the ikhtilaf with the Majority (stated without polemic)

- **Reciting al-Fātiḥah — wajib, not fard [Hanafi].** For the Hanafis the *rukn* is "reciting some Qur'an" (min al-Qur'an); reciting al-Fātiḥah specifically is **wājib**, so omitting it forgetfully is repaired by sajdah al-sahw and the prayer stays valid. **[Majority — esp. Shafiʿi/Hanbali]** hold al-Fātiḥah is a **rukn** — *"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book"* (Bukhari/Muslim) — and the prayer is invalid without it.
  Source: [IslamQA / muftionline — reciting al-Fātiḥah behind the imam](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/95388/do-you-need-to-recite-surah-fatihah-when-praying-behind-an-imam/) · [IlmGate — Recitation Behind the Imam: the Four Madhhabs](https://www.ilmgate.org/recitation-behind-the-imam/)

- **Following the imam's recitation [Hanafi].** Behind an imam the muqtadī (follower) does **not** recite at all (neither in loud nor silent prayers) — the imam's recitation suffices, on the basis of **16:204** *("When the Qur'an is recited, listen to it and be silent")* and the hadith *"Whoever has an imam, the imam's recitation is his recitation."* **[Majority — later Shafiʿi]** require the follower to recite al-Fātiḥah even behind the imam (at least in the silent prayers; the matured Shafiʿi view extends it to loud prayers too).
  Source: [IslamQA / muftionline](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/95388/do-you-need-to-recite-surah-fatihah-when-praying-behind-an-imam/) · [IlmGate](https://www.ilmgate.org/recitation-behind-the-imam/)

- **Placement of the hands [Hanafi].** Hands raised to the **earlobes** at the opening takbīr (women: to the shoulders), then folded **below the navel for men** (women: on the chest). **[Maliki]** lets the hands hang at the sides (sadl) in fard; **[Shafiʿi]** folds them on/just below the chest above the navel; **[Hanbali]** below the navel similar to Hanafi.
  Source: [IslamQA / Askimam — hand positions across the four madhahib](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/12031/can-you-give-me-in-detail-where-to-place-the-hand-positions-in-all-the-four-rakaat-of-four-madhab-during-takbir-qiyam-ruku-sujud-and-tahiyat/)

- **Rafʿ al-yadayn (raising the hands) [Hanafi].** Hands are raised **only at the opening takbīr** and not again before/after rukūʿ. The Hanafis hold the evidence that the Prophet ﷺ ceased raising the hands (narrations via Ibn Masʿūd) to be stronger. **[Majority — Shafiʿi/Hanbali, and Maliki in part]** raise the hands also when going into and rising from rukūʿ (rafʿ al-yadayn), on the hadith of Ibn ʿUmar. Both sides have authentic proofs; this is a recognized, non-divisive ikhtilaf.
  Source: [Jamiatul Ulama KZN — why Hanafis don't raise hands at rukuʿ](https://jamiat.org.za/why-dont-hanafis-raise-their-hands-when-going-into-ruku-or-raising-their-heads-from-ruku/) · [SeekersGuidance — Should I raise my hands during prayer](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/general-answers-feeds/should-i-raise-my-hands-during-the-prayer/)

- **Saying Āmīn [Hanafi].** "Āmīn" is said **silently (sirran)** after al-Fātiḥah, even in loud prayers. **[Majority — Shafiʿi/Hanbali]** say it **aloud (jahran)** in the loud prayers, on the hadith reports of the Prophet ﷺ and companions raising the voice with āmīn. Disputed; both positions are evidenced.
  Source: [IlmGate — Recitation Behind the Imam (covers āmīn)](https://www.ilmgate.org/recitation-behind-the-imam/)

## Section 5 — Role / rationale of Witr and the Sunan Rawatib

### Witr — why it exists, its timing, and its form
- **Why:** Witr ("the odd-numbered") seals the day's worship on an odd number, fulfilling *"Allah is Witr (One/odd) and loves the witr"* (Bukhari/Muslim). The Prophet ﷺ never left it, in residence or travel. It is the believer's nightly closing prayer.
- **Timing:** after the Isha prayer **until the break of Fajr** — *"Allah has added for you a prayer, so observe it between Isha and Fajr — the Witr."* It is recommended to delay it to the **end of the night** for one confident of waking; otherwise to pray it before sleeping.
  Source: [islamweb — Ruling on Witr Prayer](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/469702/ruling-on-witr-prayer)
- **Form ikhtilaf:**
  - **[Hanafi] 3 rakʿah, joined with a single salām**, with a sitting (tashahhud) after the 2nd rakʿah, then standing for the 3rd: recite al-Fātiḥah + a sūrah, then **takbīr → raise hands → recite Duʿāʾ al-Qunūt while standing (before rukūʿ)** → then rukūʿ → complete the prayer. **Qunūt is recited every night, all year**, not only in Ramadan.
    Source: [SeekersGuidance — Hadith proofs / procedure of Witr (Hanafi)](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/prayer/the-hadiths-on-the-witr-prayer-according-to-the-hanafi-legal-school/) · [IslamQA / Daruliftaa Birmingham — Qunut then takbir then ruku](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/172343/after-reciting-dua-qunoot-do-i-say-allahu-akbar-and-then-go-into-ruku/)
  - **[Maliki]** prefers **2 + 1**: two rakʿah with salām, a brief pause, then **1 separate rakʿah** (the actual Witr) with salām; Qunūt is not part of their Witr (Maliki Qunūt is in Fajr).
  - **[Shafiʿi]** flexible — Witr may be **1, 3, 5, 7, 9, or 11** rakʿah; commonly 2 + 1 with separate salāms. Qunūt is recited in Witr in the **second half of Ramadan** (and they have Qunūt in Fajr year-round).
  - **[Hanbali]** typically **2 + 1** with Qunūt in the final rakʿah (after rukūʿ), raising the hands for the duʿāʾ.
    Source: [About Islam — Different ways to pray Witr](https://aboutislam.net/counseling/ask-the-scholar/prayer/modes-performing-witr-prayer/) · [IslamOnline Fiqh — Modes of performing Witr](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/en/modes-of-performing-witr-prayer/)

### The Sunan Rawatib (the 12 rakʿah) — wisdom and a key misconception to flag
- **The 12 confirmed rakʿah** (the rawatib): **2 before Fajr, 4 before + 2 after Zuhr, 2 after Maghrib, 2 after Isha**.
- **The "house in Paradise" hadith (Umm Ḥabībah):** *"Whoever prays twelve rakʿah in a day and night [voluntarily, besides the obligatory], a house will be built for him in Paradise."* — **Sahih Muslim 728**; also Tirmidhi 414–415 and an-Nasāʾi 1807.
  Source: [Umm Habibah hadith — Muslim 728 / Tirmidhi (abuaminaelias, IslamQA)](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/1048/al-sunnan-al-rawatib-sunnah-prayers-associated-with-fardh-prayers)
- **Wisdom / role:** the rawatib act as a **protective fence** around the obligatory prayers and, crucially, **make up the deficiencies in the fard.** A hadith (Abu Dawud / Tirmidhi) states that on the Day of Judgment a servant's obligatory prayers are checked first, and **if found deficient, Allah says: "Look whether My servant has any voluntary (nafl) prayers,"** and the shortfall in the fard is completed from the nafl. The rawatib are the believer's reserve.
- **The misconception to flag for the episode:** that the rawatib/sunnah are **equal in weight to the fard**, or that praying lots of sunnah can substitute for a missed fard. **They cannot.** The fard is the obligation and a missed fard must be made up by qadāʾ of *that fard* — nafl only **supplements** the fard's reward and patches *minor* deficiencies (lack of khushūʿ etc.); it does not discharge an abandoned obligation. Conversely, neither should the sunnah be dismissed as trivial: persistently abandoning the muʾakkadah rawatib is blameworthy. The correct framing: **fard is the floor (binding), the rawatib are the believer's safety margin above it.**
  Source: [IslamQA.info — al-Sunan al-Rawatib (10 or 12)](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/175137) · [IslamQA.info — sunnah prayers associated with fard](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/1048/al-sunnan-al-rawatib-sunnah-prayers-associated-with-fardh-prayers)
