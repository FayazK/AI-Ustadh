## Scope of this research
Fundamentals of salah for a comprehensive podcast episode: what namaz IS; the categories
(فرض / واجب / سنّتِ مؤکدہ / سنّتِ غیر مؤکدہ / نفل / وتر); rakʿah counts per prayer; the five
timings; the method/arkān; the role of witr and the rawatib; and a rigorous myth-busting pass on
fabricated/weak narrations (the "15 punishments" forgery, "عماد الدین" wording, etc.). Fiqh is
**Hanafi-primary** with the four-madhhab ikhtilaf clearly labelled.

## Key research highlights
- **Rakʿah table (Hanafi) confirmed** against Hanafi fatwa sources; fard counts **2/4/4/3/4** agreed
  by all four schools; differences are in the sunnah/nafl framing and the status of Witr.
- **Witr = Wajib [Hanafi, Abu Hanifa]** vs **Sunnah Muʾakkadah [Majority]** — both hadith bases documented.
- **Myth-bust headline:** the "**15 punishments for abandoning salah**" list is **fabricated (موضوع)** —
  Ibn Hajar (*Lisan al-Mizan*: bāṭil), al-Dhahabi (traced the forger), Standing Committee Fatwa 8689,
  Ibn Baz, Ibn Uthaymeen. The authentic threat (Qur'an 74:42–43; Muslim 82; Tirmidhi 2621) needs no forgery.
- **"الصلاۃ عماد الدین"** — weak as a marfūʿ wording (al-Nawawi: munkar bāṭil); sound *meaning* via the
  Muʿadh hadith (Tirmidhi, hasan sahih).
- **Kufr of abandoning salah** — a real, sourced ikhtilaf (Ahmad: major kufr; jumhur: fāsiq not kāfir),
  distinct from the fabricated punishment list.
