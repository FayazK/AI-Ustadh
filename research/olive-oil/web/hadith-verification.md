## 1. "Eat Olive Oil and Anoint Yourselves With It" (Primary Hadith)

### Version A — Sunan Ibn Majah 3319

**Arabic:**
> ائْتَدِمُوا بِالزَّيْتِ وَادَّهِنُوا بِهِ فَإِنَّهُ مِنْ شَجَرَةٍ مُبَارَكَةٍ

**English Translation:**
> "Season (your food) with olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it comes from a blessed tree."

**Narrator:** 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Sunan Ibn Majah |
| **Book** | Chapters on Food (Kitab al-At'imah) |
| **Hadith Number** | 3319 |
| **Grading** | Sahih (al-Albani) |
| **Link** | [sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3319](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3319) |

**Notes:** This is one of the most widely cited narrations on olive oil. The word "ائتدموا" (i'tadimoo) specifically means "use as a condiment/seasoning for food," indicating that olive oil was recommended as an accompaniment to bread and other foods. The phrase "شجرة مباركة" (shajarah mubarakah — blessed tree) is understood by scholars to be a reference to the Quranic description of the olive tree in Surah An-Nur (24:35) and Surah At-Tin (95:1).

---

### Version B — Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1851

**Arabic:**
> كُلُوا الزَّيْتَ وَادَّهِنُوا بِهِ فَإِنَّهُ مِنْ شَجَرَةٍ مُبَارَكَةٍ

**English Translation:**
> "Eat the oil and anoint yourselves with it, for indeed it is from a blessed tree."

**Narrator:** 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Jami' at-Tirmidhi |
| **Book** | The Book on Food (Kitab al-At'imah) |
| **Hadith Number** | 1851 |
| **Grading** | Sahih (al-Albani); Tirmidhi noted it as Gharib from this route |
| **Link** | [sunnah.com/tirmidhi:1851](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:1851) |

**Chain of Transmission Notes:** Abu 'Eisa (Imam al-Tirmidhi) noted that this hadith is only known through the narration of 'Abdur-Razzaq from Ma'mar, and that 'Abdur-Razzaq would narrate this with idtirab (inconsistency) — sometimes attributing it to 'Umar from the Prophet, sometimes indicating doubt, and sometimes reporting it in a mursal form from Zaid bin Aslam from his father from the Prophet. Despite this, al-Albani graded it Sahih, likely considering the combined strength of multiple chains (sahih li-ghayrihi).

## 4. Olive Oil as Medicine: Black Seed with Olive Oil

### Sunan Ibn Majah 3449

**English Translation:**
> Khalid bin Sa'd narrated that they went out, and Ghalib bin Abjar was with them, and he fell ill on the way. When they came to Al-Madinah, Ibn Abu 'Atiq visited him and said: "You should use this black seed. Take five or seven (seeds) and grind them to a powder, then drop them into his nose with drops of olive oil, on this side and on this side." He cited 'A'ishah who narrated that she heard the Prophet say: "This black seed is a healing for every disease, except the Sam." (Sam meaning death.)

**Narrator:** 'A'ishah (may Allah be pleased with her), through Khalid bin Sa'd

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Sunan Ibn Majah |
| **Book** | Chapters on Medicine (Kitab al-Tibb) |
| **Hadith Number** | 3449 |
| **Grading** | Sahih |
| **Link** | [sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3449](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3449) |

**Notes:** While the primary hadith is about black seed (habbatus sauda), olive oil is used as the carrier/vehicle for administering the medicine nasally. This demonstrates the practical medicinal use of olive oil in the Prophetic tradition.

## 8. Gharqad Tree Hadith (End Times)

### Sahih Muslim 2922

**English Translation:**
> "The last hour would not come unless the Muslims will fight against the Jews and the Muslims would kill them until the Jews would hide themselves behind a stone or a tree and a stone or a tree would say: Muslim, or the servant of Allah, there is a Jew behind me; come and kill him; but the tree Gharqad would not say, for it is the tree of the Jews."

**Narrator:** Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Sahih Muslim |
| **Book** | The Book of Tribulations and Portents of the Last Hour |
| **Hadith Number** | 2922 |
| **Grading** | Sahih |
| **Link** | [sunnah.com/muslim:2922](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2922) |

**Relevance to Olive Trees:** While this hadith mentions trees generally and the Gharqad tree specifically, classical scholars have noted the contrast: the olive tree is described as "blessed" (mubarakah) in the Quran and Sunnah, while the Gharqad (boxthorn/Lycium) is mentioned as an exception. Some scholars have pointed out that among all trees that will speak, the Gharqad is singled out as the exception, while the olive tree — being blessed — would presumably be among those that testify to the truth.

## 9. At-Tin (95:1) — "By the Fig and the Olive"

### Quranic Verse

**Arabic:**
> وَالتِّينِ وَالزَّيْتُونِ

**Translation:**
> "By the fig and the olive."

### Related Hadith — Response to Surah At-Tin

**English Translation:**
> Abu Hurairah reported that when the Prophet recited this surah and reached "Is not Allah the most just of judges?" (95:8), he would say: "Indeed, and I am a witness to that." (بَلَى وَأَنَا عَلَى ذَٰلِكَ مِنَ الشَّاهِدِينَ)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collection** | Sunan Abu Dawud, Sunan al-Tirmidhi |
| **Grading** | Hasan |

### Scholarly Interpretations of "The Fig and the Olive"

Classical and contemporary scholars have offered multiple interpretations:

1. **Literal Fruits:** Hasan al-Basri, Ikrimah, 'Ata bin Abi Rabah, Jabir bin Zaid, Mujahid, and Ibrahim al-Nakha'i understood this as the actual fig and olive fruits — Allah swearing by them due to their blessed nature and benefits.

2. **Geographic/Sacred Locations:**
   - **Fig (al-Tin):** Damascus, or the Masjid of Nuh built on Mount Judi (al-Awfi, from Ibn Abbas)
   - **Olive (al-Zaytun):** Bait al-Maqdis (Jerusalem) / Palestine — the land best known for olive production

3. **Symbolic — Sacred Sites of Prophets:**
   - The fig represents the land of the Prophets in the Levant
   - The olive represents the blessed land around Jerusalem (Masjid al-Aqsa)

**Notes:** The oath by the olive in the Quran elevates its status significantly. Allah swearing by something in the Quran indicates its supreme importance and blessedness. This Quranic oath is the theological foundation behind the "blessed tree" language in the hadith narrations above.

## 10. An-Nur (24:35) — The Olive Tree Parable

### Quranic Verse (Ayat al-Nur)

**Arabic:**
> اللَّهُ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِ كَمِشْكَاةٍ فِيهَا مِصْبَاحٌ ۖ الْمِصْبَاحُ فِي زُجَاجَةٍ ۖ الزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوْكَبٌ دُرِّيٌّ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٍ مُّبَارَكَةٍ زَيْتُونَةٍ لَّا شَرْقِيَّةٍ وَلَا غَرْبِيَّةٍ يَكَادُ زَيْتُهَا يُضِيءُ وَلَوْ لَمْ تَمْسَسْهُ نَارٌ

**Translation:**
> "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche within which is a lamp; the lamp is within glass, the glass as if it were a brilliant star, lit from [the oil of] a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil would almost glow even if untouched by fire."

### Scholarly Tafseer on the Olive Tree Reference

- **Ibn Abbas:** The tree is in the desert, not shaded by any other tree, mountain, or cave — nothing covers it. This positioning gives the best quality oil.

- **Mujahid:** The tree is positioned where it receives sun at both sunrise and sunset, producing the purest, most luminous oil.

- **Sa'id bin Jubayr:** "This is the best kind of oil. When the sun rises it reaches the tree from the east, and when it sets, from the west. So the sun reaches it morning and evening, and thus it is not counted as being in the east or west."

**Theological Significance:** This verse explicitly calls the olive tree "shajarah mubarakah" (شَجَرَةٍ مُّبَارَكَةٍ — a blessed tree), which is the same phrase used in the hadith narrations. The connection between the Quranic verse and the hadith is direct: when the Prophet said olive oil "comes from a blessed tree," he was referencing this Quranic language. The olive oil in this parable represents the purest form of spiritual light — so pure it "would almost glow even if untouched by fire."

**No specific hadith from the Prophet about this particular verse and olive oil has been found in the major collections.** The tafseer tradition here draws on companion narrations (athar) from Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and others, rather than direct Prophetic hadith.

## 11. Fabricated and Weak Narrations

### FABRICATED: "Olive Oil Keeps Satan Away for 40 Days"

**Text Circulated:**
> "O Ali, anoint yourself with oil, because whoever anoints himself with oil and eats it with bread, the devil will not come near him for forty days."

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Attributed Narrator** | 'Ali ibn Abi Talib |
| **Grading** | **FABRICATED (Mawdu')** |
| **Assessment** | Imam al-Bayhaqi declared it a fabrication |
| **Source of Ruling** | [islamweb.net/en/fatwa/347251](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/347251/hadeeth-about-applying-olive-oil-to-keep-satan-away-is-fabricated) |

**Chain Issues:** The chain is composed of a succession of weak narrators (Musalsal bi al-Du'afa'). Al-Surri, Hammad, and 'Abdur-Raheem are all considered weak narrators.

---

### FABRICATED: "Olive Oil is a Cure for 70 Diseases"

**Text Circulated:**
> "Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it is from a blessed tree... and it is a cure for seventy diseases, among which is leprosy."

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Grading** | The first part (eat and anoint) is **authentic**; the addition about "seventy diseases including leprosy" is a **FORGERY** |
| **Assessment** | Shaykh Ahmad al-Ghumari stated the "seventy diseases" segment is fabricated |
| **Source** | [hadithanswers.com](https://hadithanswers.com/the-hadith-regarding-olive-oil-being-a-cure-for-seventy-illnesses/) |

**Notes:** This is a dangerous conflation where an authentic hadith has been appended with fabricated content. The core narration about eating olive oil and anointing with it is found in the Sunan and Musnad collections and is authentic. However, the claim about it curing seventy diseases is not from the Prophet and was forged. Researchers and speakers must be careful to separate the authentic portion from the fabricated addition.

## Key Scholarly Conclusions

1. **The core hadith is authentic.** The narration "Eat olive oil and anoint yourselves with it, for it comes from a blessed tree" is transmitted through multiple companions ('Umar, Abu Hurairah, Abu Asid) and recorded in Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah, and Darimi. Al-Albani graded the primary chains as Sahih.

2. **The Quran elevates the olive tree's status.** Allah swears by the olive in Surah At-Tin (95:1) and calls it a "blessed tree" in Surah An-Nur (24:35). The hadith narrations echo this Quranic language directly.

3. **Both internal and external use are Sunnah.** The dual instruction — eat it AND anoint with it — shows the Prophet recommended olive oil for both dietary consumption and topical application.

4. **Fabricated additions must be avoided.** The claims about olive oil keeping Satan away for 40 days and curing 70 diseases are fabricated. Scholars and researchers must not attribute these to the Prophet.

5. **Olive oil served as a medicinal carrier.** In authentic narrations (Ibn Majah 3449), olive oil was used as a vehicle for administering black seed medicine, indicating its trusted role in Prophetic medicine.
